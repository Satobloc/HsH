## 1. One-line state

The project is presently in a **convergence / translation-and-construction phase**: recovering the demonstrable SAT backbone from its historical record while building the finite-core H(s)H worldtube formulation toward it. The conceptual translation has substantially begun; machinery-level translation is underway; the equation-level bridge is not yet closed.

## 2. Current architecture

The clean working ancestry is:

**Fundamental Intuitions Extended → historical SAT corpus → reconstructed SAT backbone → live SAT solver machinery → SAT/H(s)H translation/interface → H(s)H finite-core worldtube construction**

This is an ancestry/dependency map, not a claim that every historical SAT mechanism survives unchanged.

### SAT reconstruction layer

SAT is **substantially reconstructed but not yet a closed mathematical system**. Historical concepts, equations, solvers, failed tests, revisions, and later corrections are being source-typed rather than flattened into a single retrospective theory.

Current live SAT geometric-solver machinery includes:

- **Whirligig / Donut** — live SAT geometric solver.
- **UI / TX** — live SAT geometric solver/component.
- **Three Spheres** — live SAT geometric solver/component.
- **Hagalaz** — live integrating operator tying the solver family together.

These functional/current classifications come from Nathan Direct and are independent of mathematical correctness or maturity. See [CURRENT_HSH_HYPOTHESIS_STATUS_2026-09-14.md](WORKSPACES/COMMON/CURRENT_HSH_HYPOTHESIS_STATUS_2026-09-14.md).

### Translation / interface layer

SAT → H(s)H translation has **already begun**. The current task is not to replace SAT wholesale but to determine, proposition and mechanism at a time, what survives when the simplified worldline/filament representation is rebuilt as finite-core worldtube geometry.

A useful translation packet is:

**FIE premise / SAT source object → demonstrable SAT function → unresolved defect or representational limitation → candidate H(s)H counterpart → equivalence status**

The working discipline is:

**recover → type → freeze → test → translate → compare**

Do not call two structures equivalent merely because they look analogous or produce similar language.

### H(s)H construction layer

H(s)H is the current finite-core/worldtube continuation of the SAT programme. Current construction work is actively exploring how inherited SAT functions should be realized by worldtube geometry, layers, cross-sections, deformation modes, interaction structure, readout/intersection, and recursive coiling/braiding.

**Kerr** and **Kelvin** are current live H(s)H hypothesis/construction lines. They are not to be demoted to historical scaffolding merely because precursor ideas exist. Their live status does not establish correctness.

Current Kerr/worldtube work is especially concentrated in the [Ravel workspace](WORKSPACES/RAVEL/). Its documents are sandbox/current-construction sources unless individual propositions have been admitted into BEDROCK with an explicit status.

## 4. Major open closure problem

The central unfinished job is the **mathematical bridge** between the reconstructed SAT functional backbone and a sufficiently explicit H(s)H finite-core object.

In practical terms, the programme still needs to show, source-first and with declared assumptions, which H(s)H geometric structures reproduce or replace the important SAT functions and which historical SAT mechanisms fail, become limits, or require revision.

This includes resolving the relationship among:

- full worldtube/history versus local readout/intersection;
- centerline/worldline limits versus finite-core structure;
- local core scale versus larger persistent-coil/carrier scales;
- deformation/propagation modes versus historical SAT traveling excitations;
- filament/timesheet interaction language versus the deeper force relation Nathan has identified as primitive;
- solver representations versus the H(s)H objects they may measure, transform, or constrain.

These are active construction questions, not settled entries merely because current sandbox documents discuss them.
