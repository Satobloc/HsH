## Controlling current intent

The live rebuild is not another retrospective synthesis attempt. Nathan describes
it as beginning after the conceptual picture gelled, in this order:

1. identify primitives;
2. characterize their domains and admissible relations;
3. calibrate scales against empirical/standard-physics benchmarks;
4. construct the finite-core object and its dynamics;
5. define resolving/intersection readout;
6. test older SAT-O, 4DHH, toolbox, roundup, and derivation machinery against
   that dependency chain.

The H(s)H and SAT26 materials are expected hardware-selection sources. SAT-O and
4DHH are valuable prior assemblies, but their completeness does not grant
authority. Historical checking labels and LLM judgments do not affect
mathematical status.

## New calibration proposal: what is and is not established

The terminal assistant response in the newer main-conversation snapshot proposes

\[
a_e=\frac{\hbar}{2m_ec}
\]

as an electron Kerr-core radius and assumes a closed circumferential mode travels
at \(c\). It then obtains

\[
\omega_1=\frac{c}{a_e}=\frac{2m_ec^2}{\hbar},
\qquad
2T_1=2\frac{2\pi a_e}{c}=\frac{h}{m_ec^2}.
\]

These equalities are algebraically correct given the two inputs. They do not
independently determine the H(s)H core scale or mode law. Still required are:

- a reason to identify the Kerr parameter with the modeled core radius;
- a typed closed carrier (\(S^1\) ring, shell mode, projection, or induced
  resolving-thickness circuit);
- a derived or independently calibrated propagation speed;
- a constitutive energy and coupling law;
- separation of electron-specific calibration from universal primitives.

**Classification:** algebra `STD/DERIVED` conditionally; Kerr-core assignment,
closed-mode selection, and physical interpretation `GEN/CANDIDATE`; framework
scale calibration `OPEN`.

## Immediate consequence for the build

The next primitive decision is not a numerical substitution. It is the typed
carrier/cross-section decision already exposed by the framed-curve work:
full \(B^3\) normal tube versus a selected rank-two \(B^2\) material subbundle,
together with the resolving-thickness geometry. Only after that choice can the
Kerr/Compton anchor be tested as a scale calibration rather than installed as a
definition.
