## 1. Entry method: empirical Minkowski map

The current rebuild begins from a richly specified Minkowski representation of
an observed phenomenon and a matched control without that phenomenon. Inherited
physical explanations are then set aside while the observable mapping is kept.

tested. Matching the construction target makes a candidate viable; unrequested
holdout or cross-sector success is the stronger promotion gate.

**Status:** `SRC/ACTIVE` as a declared research method. It is a method of model
construction, not itself evidence that any proposed H(s)H object is correct.

### Foundational control: what survives translation

`FUNDAMENTAL INTUITIONS.txt` supplies the early structural seed of the present
map: a four-dimensional history represented by filaments/worldlines, a moving

its history. It also establishes the methodological presumption that Standard
Model, GR, and quantum results remain benchmark constraints unless evidence or
the model's internal logic forces a change.

backreaction are the historical precursor of the Electrogravity question. The
braided organization and direct filament interaction are precursors of the
Interbraid question. Neither later two-force compression is derived merely by
recognizing these ancestors.

**Retained:** modeled history, resolving structure, intersectional readout,
interaction/backreaction, and inherited-physics benchmark discipline

**Displaced or unresolved:** claims that these model objects are literally

past tension; direct import of string theory; and proposed constant mappings.
These remain `SAT/HISTORICAL`, `QUARANTINED`, or `OPEN` and cannot control H(s)H.
