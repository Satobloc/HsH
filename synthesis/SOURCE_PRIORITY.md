## Priority lanes

1. **SAT-O EFT lane — candidate systemwide spine.** First establish its internal
   chronology and versioning from summaries, directives, indexes, and pipeline
   documents. Then read its core modules and derivations sequentially. Treat
   SAT-O as the strongest candidate for a cross-sector effective formalism, not
   as canonical in advance.
2. **4DHH lane — candidate local mathematical quarry.** Prefer 4DHH sources when
   they give a more explicit construction, action, domain, or derivation for a
   sector. Do not infer that locally stronger mathematics makes the whole 4DHH
   architecture current H(s)H.
3. **Late-2025/early-2026 tightening lane.** Use chronology to locate the latest
   coherent transition materials and compare them against both SAT-O and 4DHH.
4. **Derivation-retention lane.** Give elevated inspection priority to artifacts
   named `derivation`, `D1`, `D2`, `module`, `cycle`, `leg`, `bolt`, `torque`,
   `patch`, `spackle`, and related workshop language. These names often mark
   intermediate steps omitted from polished summaries; they do not certify the
   steps.
5. **Star-shaped derivation-map lane — cross-sector coherence spine.** Recover
   the process from direct conversations and working artifacts. Track each
   spoke back to its declared common hub and out to an independent sector.
   Preserve the useful discipline—shared assumptions, no inserted targets,
   cross-sector holdouts—while quarantining claims of zero-parameter closure or
   numerical inevitability until actually derived.
