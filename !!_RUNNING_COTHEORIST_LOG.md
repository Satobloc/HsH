## 2026-09-16 — θ₄, winding, and bosonic identity

### θ₄ baseline

Nathan-direct clarification: SAT θ₄ measures the filament–timesheet intersection angle from the **time normal**. Null/vacuum orientation is θ₄ = 0: filament tangent parallel to the time normal / timesheet propagation direction. For a particle at rest, θ₄ is equivalent to inverse helical pitch.

Working implication: θ₄ describes departure from null/inverse pitch but need not itself generate winding. Winding may be inherited from a larger universal winding action, plausibly connected to the black-hole → big-bang mechanism and possibly other contributors. The origin of that winding action is currently bracketed; its downstream consequences can be reconstructed without solving the upstream mechanism.

### SAT winding should not automatically map one-to-one into H(s)H winding

H(s)H has additional geometric machinery unavailable or less differentiated in SAT: finite worldtubes, Kerr-shell structure/deformation, inductive response, and reconnection. Reconstruction should therefore test whether things SAT described as winding/coiling/kinking are better represented in H(s)H as:

- winding proper;
- shell deformation;
- inductive propagation/reconnection;
- traveling kink/deformation;
- or combinations/sequences of these.

Do not force SAT terminology forward unchanged merely for continuity.

### Important SAT particle-zoo inheritance

The SAT Particle Zoo distinguishes **persistent/extended helical coiling** from **transient propagating helical excitation**. SAT “true bosons” are not defined by absence of helicity: neutrinos and photons can be helical excitations, but are not persistently coiled/tethered to one filament. SAT “true fermions” are associated with persistent/extended coiling. Thus the immediate reconstruction question is not simply winding vs. no winding.

### Current bosonic-identity fork — OPEN

Baryogenesis/persistent-fermion formation is out of present scope. Immediate question:

> Is Kerr-shell deformation and/or inductive reconnection sufficient to carry bosonic identity, or is the actual traveling kink of the SAT definitions still required?

Current tempting candidate mapping, **not yet adopted**:

- **Kerr shell:** carrier / response geometry rather than necessarily a particle identity itself.
- **Neutrino:** propagating inductive/reconnection state. This is attractive because the state could propagate/reconstitute through the underlying structure rather than requiring a substantial traveling material deformation, offering an internally natural route to very weak interaction with matter.
- **Photon:** actual traveling kink/deformation state. This is attractive because the geometry itself must deform as the excitation propagates, giving surrounding structure a more direct coupling target and potentially accounting for photons' much greater interaction with matter.

This candidate mapping must be tested from H(s)H geometry rather than accepted because it matches the desired phenomenology.

### Consequence if candidate mapping survives

The old SAT neutrino/photon distinction and therefore the dark-photon/dark-matter interpretation would need revision. Neutrino ↔ photon would no longer be merely two orientations/precession states of essentially the same traveling helix. It would involve a **propagation-mode transition** between inductive/reconnection propagation and kink propagation. θ₄/precession might participate in or trigger such a transition but would not by itself constitute the full transformation.

Neutrino flavor evolution could then potentially be an evolution internal to the inductive state, while neutrino ↔ photon conversion would be a different operation. This remains exploratory.

### Next reconstruction test

Hold cosmological winding origin, baryogenesis, and dark matter downstream consequences aside. Compare the candidate H(s)H bosonic modes directly:

1. Kerr-shell deformation alone;
2. inductive/reconnection propagation;
3. traveling kink propagation.

Ask whether the geometry itself predicts the qualitative interaction hierarchy between neutrino-like and photon-like states **without particle-specific auxiliary rules**. In particular, determine whether inductive/reconnection propagation naturally passes through matter-like persistent structures while a traveling kink naturally couples/scatters/absorbs more strongly.
