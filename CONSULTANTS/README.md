# CONSULTANTS

Independent consultant workspaces live here.

These workspaces are for bounded external review, comparative analysis, provenance checks, evidence organization, and methodological critique. They are not authoritative theory text and should not silently overwrite synthesis, formalization, archive, or COMMON coordination surfaces.

## General rules

- Treat primary sources as controlling over summaries, indexes, model recollections, and derived catalogs.
- Distinguish chronology, priority, independent convergence, structural similarity, exposure, influence, and correctness. Do not collapse them into one claim.
- Mark inference as inference.
- Preserve source provenance and dates wherever practical.
- Keep unresolved questions visible rather than smoothing them over.
- Quarantined-source material must remain out of repository notes: no names, excerpts, metadata, paraphrases, interpretations, or conclusions drawn uniquely from quarantined materials are to be written here unless Nathan explicitly lifts the quarantine.
- Consultant findings are provisional until discussed with Nathan.
