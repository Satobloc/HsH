The ball is placed inside the torus hole. Its center follows radius R−2r, so its outwardmost point remains on the torus inner wall at radius R−r. The sphere and tube radii are both r.

No-slip rolling assumption: spin angle = −(R−2r)φ/r. The spherical seam is a painted curve carried by the ball's orientation. This is a kinematic model, not yet a collision solver or a complete Whirligig mechanism.
