## 5. Measured system and data provenance — before numerical comparison

**System:** GW150914, detected 2015-09-14, GPS 1126259462.4 s, by LIGO Hanford H1 and Livingston L1. The official GWOSC O1/O2-preliminary v2 page lists calibrated H1/L1 strain products, including 4 kHz 32 s HDF5 files. These are free public data products.

**Measured-data source used here:** Ghosh, Brito & Buonanno (2021), Phys. Rev. D 103, 124041, arXiv:2104.01906. The paper's pSEOBNR parameterized waveform uses the full inspiral-merger-ringdown signal. It keeps the inspiral/merger GR structure and allows the dominant (ell,m,n)=(2,2,0) QNM frequency and damping time to vary; other QNM modes remain at GR values. The likelihood assumes stationary Gaussian detector noise with a PSD and a 20 Hz lower frequency for GW150914. This is a model-dependent posterior inference, not a ringdown-only raw-data fit.

**Post-merger-only and multimode cross-checks:** Abbott et al. (2016), PRL 116, 221101, fit a damped sinusoid to post-merger data at start times 1, 3, 5, and 6.5 ms after the MAP merger time. The 90% contour begins to overlap the GR QNM prediction at 3 ms; at 5 ms the MAP point is inside the GR contour. The corresponding damped-sinusoid-vs-noise Bayes factors/SNRs decrease with later start time (approximately log10 B=14, SNR=8.5 at 3 ms; log10 B=6.5, SNR=6.3 at 5 ms; log10 B=3.5, SNR=4.8 at 6.5 ms); these are signal-vs-noise evidences, not H(s)H-vs-GR odds. The signal becomes undetectable at about 9 ms. This gives an empirical transition window, not a timesheet-thickness measurement. Carullo et al. (2019), arXiv:1902.07527, find no evidence for more than one QNM and caution that central frequency/damping posteriors alone do not unambiguously identify a single mode; their Kerr-informed analysis cannot discriminate the l=2 and l=3 mode subsets. Thus the 220 assignment in the 2021 pSEOBNR analysis is model-conditioned, not an independent mode identification. These are complementary analyses of the same event, not independent experiments.

**Important independence limit:** the QNM-inferred remnant mass/spin and the IMR/NR-fit remnant mass/spin are different model routes on the same event, not independent experiments. A true H(s)H prediction must be generated without fitting this event and tested against held-out measured data.

**Calibration/noise:** the inspected pSEOBNR likelihood section does not document explicit detector-calibration nuisance marginalization. The GW150914 calibration literature reports frequency-dependent H1/L1 amplitude/phase uncertainties (broadly below 10% and 10 degrees across the analysis band; more detailed band extrema are available). Do not assume those uncertainties are included in the quoted QNM posterior unless a calibration-marginalized analysis is identified. The Gaussian-noise assumption is another model condition.

**Raw access limit:** the official GWOSC H1/L1 4 kHz 32 s HDF5 URLs were identified, but binary downloads failed in this runtime. No raw-strain reanalysis was performed. The pSEOBNR joint posterior samples/covariance were not retrieved; the paper provides marginal tables and plotted joint contours. Therefore no joint f-tau, mass-spin, or derived-Q credible interval is calculated here.

## 8. The missing Cruller-to-QNM map

The useful target is not “Kerr mass/spin fits a QNM.” That is the standard GR control. The H(s)H direction must start from the Cruller and work backwards to an observable.

A minimal **proposal**, not a derived H(s)H equation, is:

\[
\delta C^4
\longrightarrow
\mathcal L_{\rm Cruller}[K^4,W^4,\mathbf n,U?]
\longrightarrow
\lambda_{220}
\longrightarrow
(h_+,h_\times)
\longrightarrow
d_i(t),
\]

where the observed damped mode can be represented under e^(lambda t) convention by

\[
\lambda_{220}=-\frac{1}{\tau_{220}}-i\,2\pi f_{220}.
\]

The missing pieces are substantive:

1. Define the Cruller/worldtube state delta C^4 and its finite-core support without reverting to the retired reciprocal filament/timesheet model.
2. Define the active instantiation/intersection/cobordism/truncation map. Do not call it a passive readout slice.
3. Define how the matter-responsive time normal and any finite-thickness active-update region enter the dynamics. Do not silently call this Cartan torsion.
4. Derive the effective Lorentzian exterior/control metric from the native positive-Euclidean 4D geometry, or derive the QNM operator directly without assuming that map.
5. Derive the spectrum lambda_lmn from the Cruller/worldtube dynamics and boundary conditions. The current responsive-medium role is optional/open; do not assign it the damping term without a constitutive law.
6. Specify the global Klein Fabric to local Cruller/worldtube composition map.
7. Map the mode to detector strain with the standard detector response, and preserve calibration/noise uncertainty.
8. Establish identifiability. A single complex mode gives two real observables; it cannot uniquely reconstruct a general 4D geometry. At least a second independently identified mode or another independent observable is needed for stronger interior/no-hair claims.

The Kerr QNM result is an **external Lorentzian control** for the Cruller-generated spectral output. It is not an H(s)H prediction. The astrophysical Kerr exterior does not measure the proposed microscopic Kerr/ER core.
