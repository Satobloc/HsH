## 3. Nathan Direct geometry extracted

The most stable cross-version description is not a fixed inventory of physical entities. It is a representational pipeline:

1. **Observation:** the phenomenon to be described, without an ontological claim.
2. **Geometric representation:** candidate structures and relationships that organize the observation.
3. **Mathematical abstraction:** variables, constraints, maps, dynamics and readout rules representing that geometry.

Within the geometric layer:

- A filament/worldline is a candidate one-dimensional carrier/core in the earlier descriptions.
- A worldtube may denote a higher-dimensional history or finite-support representation; it must not silently be identified with the one-dimensional core.
- The timesheet is a three-dimensional moving surface/wavefront or readout surface. It is not automatically identical to the metric or to a spacetime fabric.
- The responsive medium is a provisional carrier/response description for electrogravity. Its relation to the timesheet is unresolved.
- A field can be a descriptive projection of observed behavior; a mechanism is a deeper structural account that may generate that behavior.
- Coupling is a coincidence or defined relation between structures. A mathematical split must preserve that relation.
- Symmetric structural attribution is a default modeling rule when no preferential carrier is known, not a physical equal-force law.
- A bosonic ripple and persistent fermionic coil are candidate descriptions; their exact carrier and transfer rules remain open.
