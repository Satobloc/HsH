## 4. Current-current taxonomy and minimal representation

The source family contains three distinct current types:

| Representation | Degree | Source status | Limitation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| SAT4D (J_\mu) | 1-form/vector | averaged flux current in the June 2025 draft | not specified as a Poincare-dual distribution; not explicitly tied to the Maxwell source term |
| Blockwave (J^{\mu\nu}) | 2-form | rank-2 worldsheet-labelled current in the Sep 16 artifact / Oct 19 assistant action | support on actual worldsheets is asserted but not constructed; same-named attachment identities must remain distinct |
| Page One (J_{\mu\nu\rho}) | 3-form | unbylined user-role paste; 3-form editorial direction was adopted by Nathan in Sep 18 conversation | no support map or normalization from the finite-core worldtube |

In four dimensions, a 3-form Hodge-duals to a 1-form; a 2-form Hodge-duals to a 2-form. The J3 and J2 objects are not Hodge duals of each other. More fundamentally, the Poincare-dual degree depends on the represented support dimension: 1D support gives degree 3; 2D worldsheet support gives degree 2; a 4D finite-core region has degree 0 density. The archive does not provide a source-faithful map that identifies these carriers.

The minimal candidate should therefore keep geometry primary and currents as derived representations:

```
R = (M^4, frame/signature, K^4, {Sigma_t^3}, U?, O, Pi, Gamma)
```

- (M^4): ambient domain, with the native Euclidean metric and Lorentzian/Minkowski control frames kept distinct.
- (K^4): finite-core worldtube support as primary object. A centerline is a parameterization/limit only when a source-backed map is supplied, not a second physical carrier by default.
- ({\Sigma_t^3}): labelled timesheet family.
- (U?): optional responsive medium. Keep the existing Kelvin-vortex/medium hypothesis as a candidate, not a newly invented constitutive law.
- (O): observable/readout map from worldtube-timesheet incidence and local geometry.
- (Pi): source, author, role, context, hash and formalization provenance.
- (Gamma): labelled component/interbraid/incidence graph for composition.

For finite-core (K^4), the timesheet section (K^4\cap\Sigma_t^3) is generally a 3D section. A separately defined 1D centerline intersection would be point-like, but must not be silently substituted for the finite-core section. Composition must preserve component labels and incidence edges; an unlabelled union loses multiplicity.

Geometry-to-equation requires explicit metric/frame, orientation, measure, support dimension, core profile, boundary conditions and any medium constitutive law. Equation-to-geometry is generally non-unique under gauge, diffeomorphism, reparameterization and field-redefinition freedom. A smooth current formula does not by itself establish distributional support on a filament/worldsheet.

This is a candidate typed interface, not a theorem, action, or physical model.
