### The information-preservation question

Nathan Direct says a 2D worldsheet simplification need not discard information; whether it does depends on the construction. The relevant formal question is not whether the source and target have matching dimensions. It is whether the joint readout Q=(O,C), containing both the observable O and the coupling signature C, is constant on every fibre of the proposed projection π.

PR #2236 formalizes the abstract criterion:

- a joint readout descends through a tube-only quotient iff it is constant on same-tube fibres;
- if the readout is timesheet-sensitive within a tube fibre, a tube-only reduction is forbidden;
- the symmetric sheet-only statements hold for same-sheet fibres.

This is the right conditional test for the direct statement. It is not a universal information-conservation principle and does not show that the actual H(s)H tube-to-worldsheet projection is lossless. No source-defined π or physical Q has been supplied.
