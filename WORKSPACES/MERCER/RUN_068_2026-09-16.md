## What this establishes
1. All three graph-divergent families are unsafe for UUID-level dedup or `keep newest`/`keep largest` disposition.
2. `Geometry in Physics` directly falsifies the convenience heuristic that the newest export is necessarily the most complete: newer and smaller can coexist with substantial mutually exclusive graph material.
3. `SAT Daily Action` shows that a LIVE-path artifact can be byte-identical to an older branch while a newer divergent export of the same raw conversation UUID exists elsewhere. LIVE placement therefore cannot by itself be treated as conversation-level currentness authority.
4. Filename end dates and top-level update times are useful lineage evidence but not sufficient ancestry/disposition rules; graph/content relation must remain explicit.
5. Archive identity policy should preserve branch/snapshot relations as first-class provenance rather than flattening repeated UUIDs into duplicates.
