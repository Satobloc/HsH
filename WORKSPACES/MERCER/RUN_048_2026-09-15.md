## Operation
Created `WORKSPACES/MERCER/validate_cross_source_integrity_v2.py` at commit `36411c2954564209c323bd036c2c84950756bea4`.

This is a read-only transition wrapper around the established validator. It preserves the older validator's non-Viewer checks but replaces the three Viewer checks known to encode stale semantics:
- `viewer.accepted_input_arithmetic`
- `viewer.external_registry_reconciliation`
- `viewer.manifest_record_reconciliation`

The replacement checks consume `viewer_input_semantics.reconstruct()` and separate four invariants that had previously been conflated:
1. Viewer-declared pre-dedup acceptance vs reconstructed resolved-source acceptance;
2. reconstructed post-dedup cardinality vs `source_conversations_before_curation`;
3. final Viewer records vs resolved winner provenance;
4. external lineage from the registry path declared by the Viewer generation, not the legacy hard-coded manual registry.

The wrapper reports schema version 2 and explicitly names `production-resolved-existing-source-v1` as its Viewer semantics.

## What this establishes / does not establish
Establishes a concrete integration path for the shared semantics adapter without rewriting or weakening the original validator's unrelated checks. It also makes the pre-dedup/post-dedup distinction explicit in check IDs and output.
