   - `LIVE DIRECT STATEMENT` — Nathan said it here.
   - `ARCHIVE-VERIFICATION TARGET` — historical/developmental claim worth corroborating/dating.

5. Once a matching raw export lands, backfill exact conversation/message identifiers and, where useful, promote suitable entries into `WORKSPACES/COMMON/NATHAN_VERIFIED_WORDS_COMPENDIUM.md` with historical relationships.
6. Do not use this file as a substitute for contemporaneous evidence when establishing earliest use or chronology.
