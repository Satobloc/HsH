## Limits
This is explicitly a **text-equivalence diagnostic**, not a complete human-visible-content serializer. It does not establish equivalence for images, attachments, citations, tool payloads, multimodal objects, or other non-text semantics. A zero `visible_text_changed_nodes` result therefore cannot by itself justify archive suppression or source disposition.

The script remains reporting-only. It does not rename, delete, suppress, rewrite, or disposition archive sources or Viewer records.
