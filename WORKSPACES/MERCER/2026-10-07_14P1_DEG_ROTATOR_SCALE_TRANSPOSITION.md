# Mercer sandbox — 14.1° rotator mapped across scales

Date: 2026-10-07
Status: SANDBOX / scale-transposition fixture.

## Construction
Target shadow tangent separation:
Δθ = 14.1°.

For the side shadow of a circular 4D helix,
Δθ = 2 arctan β,
hence
β = tan(7.05°) = 0.12367047701360828.

Thus every geometrically similar rotator in this family has the same tangential speed fraction:
v/c = 0.12367047701360828.

For any chosen radius R:
v = β c
ω = v/R
T = 2πR/v
f = v/(2πR)
P4 = cT = 2πR/β
P4/R = 50.80586295861223
a_c = v²/R.

Therefore this is a one-parameter similarity family indexed only by overall scale R.

## Important interpretation boundary
The rows below are scale transpositions, not claims that the named physical systems actually rotate at this speed or with this period. They answer:
"If a rotator of this size had the same normalized 14.1° helix geometry, what period/frequency would it require?"

## Similarity result
At fixed 14.1° shadow angle:
- β is invariant.
- P4/R is invariant.
- κR = β²/(1+β²) is invariant.
- τR = β/(1+β²) is invariant.
- T ∝ R.
- f ∝ R^-1.
- centripetal acceleration a_c ∝ R^-1.

Thus the geometry is exactly self-similar under scale change, while the dynamical burden rises inversely with size.

## Failure condition
This family is geometrically valid at all scales as a Minkowski lift of circular motion, but physical realizability is separate. Named systems must not be identified with the fixture unless independent standard-physics observations give the required β or equivalent geometry.
