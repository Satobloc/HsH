Three-Spheres and ᚼ share R4 primitive fixtures without implying homologous outputs. RUN099: F2-only SO4 frame rotation changes relative-frame output, not carrier geometry. Product of static endpoint frame ratios telescopes, NOT physical transport holonomy.

Whirligig -> ᚼ: flow encoding and group commutator possible but not universal. Whirligig must declare representation encoding and input recovery witness for historically reversible restricted cases.

Frenet -> two SO4 rates: S=k1^2+k2^2+k3^2, P=k1*k3. Rates squared equal (S ± sqrt(S^2-4P^2))/2. Many-to-one, cannot invert three k_i from two rates; equal rates force k2=0 and |k1|=|k3| under valid Frenet assumptions.

3-sphere equilateral fixture: r_c=d/sqrt(3), rho=sqrt(R^2-d^2/3), G=1-d^2/(3R^2). At d=R=1: r_c=1/sqrt(3), rho=sqrt(2/3), G=2/3. A bare 'carrier radius' is disallowed. Past independent Mercer RUN099 audit demonstrated actual collision, later typed manifest distinguishes quantities.

Euclidean 4D SO4 -> induced Lorentzian metric requires explicit unit flow/eigenline and metric/signature construction, not renaming SO4 rotations as Lorentz boosts. Time orientation is distinct from unit line.

Finite-core contact -> topological charge: scalar overlap does not encode orientation/framing/global link history. Gauge frame rotation -> worldtube dynamics: geometrical representation cannot be mistaken for physical excitation.
