# Mercer sandbox — 14.1° successive-tangent oscillator

Date: 2026-10-07
Status: SANDBOX / constructed geometry fixture.

Target condition:
Successive tangent angle at alternating axis crossings of the side-shadow sine wave is 14.1°.

For
x(w)=R cos(2πw/P4),
the slopes at successive zero crossings are ±m with
m = 2πR/P4 = β.

The angle between successive tangents is
Δθ = 2 arctan β.

Set
Δθ = 14.1°,
so
β = tan(7.05°) = 0.12367047701360828.

Therefore:
v/c = 0.12367047701360828.

Normalized choice R=1 gives:
P4/R = 2π/β = 50.80586295861223.

Thus one complete 4D turn advances ~50.806 helix radii along w.

Equivalent helix:
X(u) = (R cos u, R sin u, 0, b u)
with
b/R = 1/β = 8.086..., and P4=2πb.

Side shadow:
x(w)=R cos(2πw/P4).

At alternating axis crossings:
dx/dw = ±β = ±0.123670477...
and tangent orientations relative to +w are ±7.05°, giving 14.1° separation.

Interpretation boundary:
14.1° was imposed as the construction target here. This does not independently derive or validate any historical SAT 14.1° quantity.
