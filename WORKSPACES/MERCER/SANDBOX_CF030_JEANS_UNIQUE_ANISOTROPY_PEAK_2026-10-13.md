They satisfy spherical Jeans equilibrium exactly.

In a compact positive-density self-consistent spherical equilibrium, x is maximal at the unique root of F=2Psi+rPsi'=0. Uniqueness follows from F'= -G[M/r²+M'/r]<0, F(0)>0 and F(R)<0. Thus beta=-x/(1+x) has exactly one interior minimum and tends to zero at both endpoints. At the extremum GM(r*)/r*=2Psi(r*), relative to the energy cutoff.
