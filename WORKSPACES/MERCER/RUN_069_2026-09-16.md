## Time-order check for simple subset/superset pairs

For the 115 simple message-ID subset/superset comparisons, comparing conversation-level `update_time` from the retained artifacts gives:

- **106**: the later-updated side is also the larger/superset side;
- **9**: equal/tied `update_time` under this field;
- **0**: later-updated side is the smaller/subset side.

This is useful descriptive evidence for monotonic snapshot growth **inside the simple subset/superset class only**. It does not license a global `newest == authoritative` rule: Run 68 already establishes later/smaller mutually divergent states in graph-divergent families, including `Geometry in Physics` and `SAT Daily Action`.

## Relation vocabulary / QA contract

A repeated UUID should therefore be described along independent axes rather than collapsed to `duplicate`:

1. **byte relation** — exact vs non-exact;
2. **message-set relation** — equal / subset / superset / mutually side-exclusive;
3. **graph relation** — same topology vs topology-divergent;
4. **content relation** — metadata-only / readable-text-divergent / nontext-unresolved / other content-representation difference;
5. **time relation** — earlier/later/tied under the recorded timestamp field;
6. **presentation relation** — Viewer acceptance/path/LIVE placement, handled separately;
7. **authority/disposition** — explicitly unresolved unless established by independent provenance evidence.

This preserves the distinction between source identity, ancestry, content equivalence, publication placement, and authority.

## What this establishes

The corpus contains several materially different repeated-UUID phenomena. `Duplicate` is too coarse a provenance label. Exact copies, monotonic snapshots, metadata rewrites, same-graph readable-content revisions, unresolved nontext changes, and mutually divergent graph states all occur.

Within simple subset/superset pairs, recorded update time is strongly monotonic in this artifact (106 later/larger, 9 ties, 0 later/smaller). That regularity is bounded to that class and must not be extrapolated across branch/snapshot-divergent families.

## What this does not establish

- No source is designated canonical, obsolete, suppressible, or disposable.
- No Viewer presentation rule is changed.
- Equal graph structure does not imply semantic equivalence.
- A larger/newer/LIVE artifact is not thereby authoritative.
- The narrow text projection does not resolve multimodal/tool/attachment semantics.
- Archive cleanliness or relation classification says nothing about theory correctness.
