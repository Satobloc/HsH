## Independent construction
Represent three coupled geometric channels by
U = R23(c) R13(b,delta) R12(a).
The standard rephasing-invariant oriented volume is
J = c12 c23 c13^2 s12 s23 s13 sin(delta).
For small geometric mixings:
J = a b c sin(delta) + O(theta^5).
Thus a CP-odd response is necessarily cubic at leading order if all three pairwise channels are required.

Consequences:
1. If any one mixing channel vanishes, J vanishes exactly.
2. Mirror reversal delta -> -delta reverses J but preserves CP-even magnitudes.
3. For equal small angles epsilon and maximal orientation, |J| ~ epsilon^3.
4. This provides a clean candidate meaning for a 'Jarlskog Shadow': a local timesheet pseudoscalar response proportional to an oriented triple product, rather than a literal neutrino species.

## Failure / solver test
Build three coupled worldtube/timesheet modes with independently measurable pairwise mixing angles a,b,c and orientation phase delta. Sweep one angle through zero. The CP-odd/timesheet pseudoscalar must vanish linearly in that angle while CP-even mixing remains. Mirror the geometry; the pseudoscalar must flip sign. At weak equal coupling, log|J| vs log epsilon must approach slope 3. Failure of any of these kills this minimal geometric mechanism.
