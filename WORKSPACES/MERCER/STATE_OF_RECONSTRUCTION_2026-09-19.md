## What I would reconstruct differently now

I would now begin with **typed ancestry and named-check scope**: identify the exact source object, distinguish raw/source/generated/interface layers, record exposure state, classify duplicate/snapshot relationships, and state exactly what a check establishes before interpreting content.

For theory-bearing historical material, I would also type variables before attempting synthesis. The He-3 pass makes the reason concrete: two formulas using `Q` or two angles near 0.24 rad cannot safely be unified until their structural level, derivation stage, chronology, and source usage are known. This is a methodological improvement in reconstruction, not a claim that one historical formulation has already been selected as correct.
