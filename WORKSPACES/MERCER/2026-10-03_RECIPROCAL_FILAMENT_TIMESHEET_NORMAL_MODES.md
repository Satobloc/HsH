Decoupling begins when gradient/bending penalties compete with (g). Natural estimates are

[

## Failure conditions

Reject this minimal reciprocal architecture if:
1. no gapless common mode survives when translation symmetry should be present;
2. the relative gap fails the square-root coupling law;
3. long-wave speed is not the summed-stiffness / summed-inertia value;
4. finite-worldtube geometry generates unavoidable nonlocal coupling that cannot reduce to this local limit;
5. independent filament/sheet constitutive parameters cannot be defined even approximately in any controlled regime.
