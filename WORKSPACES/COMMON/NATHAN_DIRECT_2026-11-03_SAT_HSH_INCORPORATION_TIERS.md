## 1. The four incorporation tiers

A proposed ingredient, mechanism, structure, or hypothesis is assessed in sequence. Passing a tier permits consideration at the next tier; it does not imply automatic promotion.

### Tier 1 — Geometric admissibility / constrained possibility

A candidate must be suggested by the geometry, preferably required by it, or be one of a small number of alternatives genuinely constrained by it. If it is not geometrically suggested, required, or tightly constrained, it is not an SAT/H(s)H candidate.

Geometry is a visual mathematical system with built-in constraints. It rules out placements, relations, motions, attachments, incidences, scales, and transformations that a free-standing equation can permit without physical meaning. A formula that can be made to work while its plotted object is distorted, misattached, outside the relevant support, or otherwise geometrically impossible has failed. Do not use mathematical freedom to evade geometric impediments.

Record:
- the exact source-defined geometric object and the feature that motivates or requires the proposal;
- which alternatives geometry leaves open, and which it excludes;
- the source, coordinate/frame, dimensions, topology, metric/signature (if defined), scales, orientation, incidence, boundaries, and relevant projection/readout;
- whether the proposal is necessary, one of a constrained set, merely allowed, or not supported.

“Allowed” alone is not “required.” A structure that is merely algebraically convenient or empirically desirable does not pass Tier 1.

### Tier 2 — Tentative explanatory hypothesis

A proposal that passes Tier 1 and provides a neat explanation of a phenomenon, with multiple intuitive interaction surfaces, may be admitted as a **tentative SAT/H(s)H hypothesis**.

The interactions must be made explicit in the geometry. “Multiple intuitive interaction surfaces” is not a license for vague verbal resemblance: specify what meets, acts on, constrains, transports through, or changes what, and show the relevant interfaces. Mark the hypothesis tentative and retain alternatives and unresolved assumptions.

### Tier 3 — Unprompted cross-sector explanatory emergence

A Tier 2 hypothesis remains tentative indefinitely unless and until its presence in the model unexpectedly explains otherwise unrelated phenomena, without being separately tuned or asked to do so. Preferably, this should happen in multiple, genuinely different sectors.

The cross-sector result must be a consequence of the same construction, not a collection of post hoc analogies or separate fits. Record the sectors, shared geometric cause, derivation path, independent observables, alternative explanations, and what was not used to tune the hypothesis. When this criterion is met, the structure is eligible to become an **official SAT/H(s)H working hypothesis**, subject to the independent evidence and theory-status controls already in BEDROCK.

### Tier 4 — Counterfactual structural indispensability

A structure is part of the model when removing or changing it breaks, or predictably distorts, the other numbers in the model by a predictable amount.

This is a counterfactual dependency test, not a rhetorical claim of importance. Define the intervention (remove it, vary it, or replace it), keep other conditions fixed where appropriate, derive which outputs should change and by how much, then compare the predicted dependency pattern against the model's independently specified relationships. Document degeneracies and compensating changes. A structure whose removal leaves the model unchanged is not established as indispensable by this tier.

Tier 4 establishes structural membership within the model. It does **not** by itself establish that the model is physically true; physical verification remains a separate gate.

## 2. Status boundaries: do not collapse the gates

The four incorporation tiers operate alongside, and do not replace, BEDROCK's authority/status strata or the existing mathematical, formal, empirical, and Factory Floor gates.

Keep these distinctions explicit:

1. **Geometric admissibility:** a proposal is suggested, required, or constrained by the actual geometry.
2. **Tentative hypothesis:** it also provides a useful explanatory account with multiple explicit interaction surfaces.
3. **Official working hypothesis:** it earns unexpected, unprompted explanatory reach across unrelated sectors.
4. **Model constituent:** its counterfactual removal or change predictably damages other model outputs.
5. **Mathematical clearance:** the actual equations and derivations pass the stated mathematical checks.
6. **Empirical/physical verification:** real observations, independent tests, uncertainty and alternatives support the physical claim.

No one state silently implies another. In particular, formal proof is not source-to-geometry fidelity; geometric clearance is not physical truth; mathematical clearance only permits candidacy consideration; and an attractive fit is not an independent prediction. The existing real-data-first BEDROCK rule and prior-permission requirement for data-free calculations remain controlling.

That is an example of hypothesis status, not a Tier 3 or Tier 4 finding, not a validated derivation, and not permission to assume its details. ## 3. Geometry is the theoretical object; mathematics is not a substitute

Theoretical construction proceeds from the physical/theoretical geometry and its source constraints. Mathematics is then used to specify, calculate, derive, fit and check that object. A formula alone is not the object. Nor does a successful calculation certify an incorrect or underspecified plot.

Do not adopt the reversed workflow:

`differential equations → numerical solution → kinematic constraints → rendered geometry`

as the default way to invent a theoretical object. It lets equations explore possibilities without first imposing the geometry's actual constraints. Instead:

1. Recover the original source statements and the actual physical or theoretical object they describe.
2. Construct and specify the geometry before claiming that an equation represents it.
3. Show the geometry, including its constraints, attachments, incidences, topology, dimensions, boundaries and transformations as relevant.
4. Derive or recover the mathematics that describes that exact geometry.
5. Compare both directions: geometry → equations and equations → geometry. Identify non-uniqueness, gauge freedom, missing definitions, distortions and mismatches.
6. Use actual observed systems and genuine measured data for physical calculations. Do not invent fixtures or parameters; data-free calculations require Nathan's specific prior permission under BEDROCK.

The same requirement applies to papers: where a statement makes a point about a geometric form, show precisely what that form means. Include the exact system-specific geometric plot with the paper/file; formula-only, proof-only, code-only, prose-only, or generic conceptual diagrams do not substitute.

## 4. Factory Floor geometry gate and five representational levels

The controlling operational specification is [Factory Floor Geometry-First Admission Gate](FACTORY_FLOOR_GEOMETRY_FIRST_GATE_2026-11-03.md). Nothing enters Factory Floor without:
- a precise, source-faithful geometric plot of the actual system under study;
- independent hostile review of that exact plot's fidelity; and
- verification that the same plot/version is displayed with the associated file.

The five levels are distinct and are not substitutes for one another:

1. **Primary — observation.**
2. **Secondary — physical working model / hands-on demonstration / orrery.**
3. **Tertiary — precision dynamic geometric software model.**
4. **Quaternary — precision static geometric diagram.**
5. **Pentary — mathematics.**

The quaternary plot is the minimum mandatory visual deliverable for Factory Floor. If behavior, response, transformation or evolution is part of the claim, a static view alone may be insufficient and the tertiary dynamic model is required. Empirical claims require primary observation. The secondary model is used when a physical demonstration is relevant. The pentary math describes and tests; it does not replace the geometric object or its plot.

The review must challenge the object, dimensionality, topology, embedding, metric/frame, incidence, scale, projection/readout, dynamics, source correspondence, and equation↔geometry closure. It must be bound to the exact plot hash/version. Material changes invalidate the previous review until rechecked. The same exact plot must be rendered beside the associated file, with conventions, legend, caption and source pointer. See the linked operational gate for the full checklist, required evidence and status vocabulary.

## 5. Four-dimensional realism and the interpretive modes: methodological, not metaphysical

The SAT four-dimensional block treatment is a **methodological formalism**, not a settled ontological assertion that the universe is literally a static block.

The June 2025 SAT Big Paper, Module O0-A, describes the interpretive modes as a controlled ladder of approximations: begin with a frozen/simple construction, then relax specified freeze-outs and add realism in stages. Its operational labels are:
- **Mode 1 — True Block:** filament kinematics are frozen; the propagating time wavefront `Σt` accounts for apparent change. Used as the baseline for specified core derivations.
- **Mode 2 — Triggered Dynamics** (also called Semi-Dynamic in the supplementary summary): the time wavefront can trigger local filament-parameter changes.
- **Mode 3 — Solidification Front:** structure resolves at the wavefront.

These are interpretive / methodological frames for construction and analysis. Mode 1 can be used as the controlled static 4D formal frame without asserting that the universe is metaphysically static.

An earlier user-authored “Central Dogma” passage in the HsH archive is explicit: SAT adopts “a four-dimensional realism as a formalism”; whether the universe is a true static block, dynamic block, growing block, or another structure remains unknown. Preserve that original qualification. Do not turn the Mode 1 label into a claim about ultimate ontology, and do not silently collapse the three modes into one.
