## Primary citation

J. W. Alexander, “An Example of a Simply Connected Surface Bounding a Region which is not Simply Connected,” *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America* **10**(1), 8–10 (1924).  
DOI: `10.1073/pnas.10.1.8`.

Alexander constructs a genus-zero surface in 3-space whose exterior is not simply connected. The construction is recursive and uses progressively smaller tube-like pieces hooked around one another.
