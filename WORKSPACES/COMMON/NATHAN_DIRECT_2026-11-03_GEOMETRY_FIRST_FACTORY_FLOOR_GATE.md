# NATHAN DIRECT — Geometry-First Research and Factory Floor Admission Gate
**Date:** 2026-11-03  
**Authority:** Nathan Direct, user statement in the live conversation.  
**Status:** Controlling methodological directive; standalone durable record. This file records the directive and does not by itself prove adoption by every worker or integration into every control surface.

## Core principle

Everything in H(s)H is geometry. The theoretical object is the geometry, not the mathematics used to describe, calculate with, or fit it. If the geometry is wrong, mathematics may be uncoupled from the object under study, unnecessary, or irrelevant. Mathematics is an instrument of precision and fitting; it is not the physical object and not the theoretical object.

A mathematical derivation without a precisely specified geometric referent is not sufficient evidence of a meaningful H(s)H result. Any statement that makes a point about a geometric form, structure, relation, embedding, projection, transformation, motion, or topology must show exactly what it means geometrically.

## The five-object hierarchy

Nathan identifies five related epistemic objects:

1. **Primary — Observation.** The observed physical system or phenomenon.
2. **Secondary — Physical working orrery / hands-on demonstration.** A tangible, manipulable realization that exposes relevant structure and behavior.
3. **Tertiary — Precision dynamic geometry.** A software model of the evolving geometric system, with its state, constraints, transformations and observables inspectable.
4. **Quaternary — Precision static diagram.** A precise geometric plot of the system and the relationships being claimed.
5. **Pentary — Mathematics.** The symbolic handbook: equations, calculations, parameterizations, proofs and fitting apparatus describing the object.

This ordering identifies distinct forms of object and representation; it does not imply that a paper must fabricate a physical orrery before it can proceed. It does mean that mathematics must remain coupled to a specified geometry and, where empirical claims are made, to observation.

## Non-negotiable Factory Floor admission rule

**Nothing enters Factory Floor unless all of the following are true:**

- A precise geometric plot of the actual system under study is included.
- The plot depicts the specific geometry relevant to the claim, not a decorative illustration, generic analogy, or unexplained schematic.
- The plot's fidelity has been checked by hostile review.
- The plot is displayed with the associated file/artifact so a reviewer can inspect the geometry alongside the mathematics and claims.
- The artifact identifies the correspondence between geometric elements and mathematical objects, variables, constraints, coordinate choices, projections/embeddings, transformations and observables as applicable.
- Any unshown, ambiguous, idealized, omitted or unresolved geometric feature is explicitly identified; no diagram may silently imply more than the source geometry warrants.

If any requirement is unmet, the artifact is **NOT ADMITTED TO FACTORY FLOOR**. It may remain in a clearly labeled exploratory workspace, but it must not be described as Factory Floor-cleared.

## Hostile geometric fidelity review

The review must test, at minimum:

- **Object identity:** Is this the actual physical/theoretical system under study, with its source and provenance identified?
- **Dimensional fidelity:** Are dimensions, manifolds, embeddings, sections, boundaries and projections distinguished correctly?
- **Incidence and topology:** Are connectivity, intersections, linking, holes, boundaries, orientation and topology represented without visual substitution?
- **Metric and scale:** Are distances, angles, curvature, scale, units and metric assumptions stated where relevant?
- **Orientation and handedness:** Are axes, normals, rotations, chirality, sign conventions and observer frames explicit?
- **Dynamics:** Where motion or transformation is claimed, does the dynamic model implement the depicted geometry and its constraints?
- **Readout:** Are the path from geometric object to observer, measurement and reported observable explicit?
- **Mathematical coupling:** Does each equation actually describe the displayed object under the stated assumptions, or has the mathematics drifted onto a surrogate?
- **Known limits:** Are idealizations, singular limits, approximations and unresolved features visible rather than hidden?

A hostile reviewer should actively seek mismatches, not merely confirm that the picture looks plausible. The review outcome, reviewer identity/role, objections, corrections and final plot version must be recorded with the artifact. An unreviewed plot is not a hostile-reviewed plot.

## Scope

This gate applies to Factory Floor work and to papers, reports, code, simulations, derivations and other research artifacts whenever they make or rely on a geometric claim. If a statement invokes a geometric form, the form must be shown precisely enough to inspect. A formula, prose description, or attractive illustration alone does not satisfy the requirement.

## Relation to existing gates

This geometry-first admission gate is additional to, not a replacement for:
- BEDROCK's real-system / genuine-observation / real-data-first requirements and prior permission for data-free calculations;
- the source-first rule requiring recovery of Nathan's original subject-specific statements before formalization or critique;
- mathematical soundness and reproducibility checks;
- the distinction between mathematical clearance, tentative H(s)H candidacy, and physical verification.

A geometrically faithful plot does not prove the mathematics. A mathematically correct derivation does not prove that it describes the plotted object. Neither alone establishes physical truth.
