## Corrections to the assistant's formulations

1. **Not interior reconstruction.** RMS does not claim that accessible surface information recovers inaccessible interior microstates. Nathan's claim is stronger and logically different: an absolutely unobservable state's presence, absence, or asserted existential determinacy cannot be established as knowledge by descriptive conjecture.
2. **Not epistemic “flattening” of a pre-given inaccessible ontology.** This misleadingly grants supposed inaccessible entities a well-formed domain of ontological options. Nathan specifies the *logical sterility* of existential predication beyond conceivable perceptual distinction.
3. **Not merely an admissibility rule by fiat.** “Inadmissible” is a conclusion to demonstrate, not a premise. The requested deliverable is rigorous go/no-go criteria derived from the actual logic and explicit definitions.
4. **Not merely “by itself.”** Nathan rejects the suggestion that supplemental premises can in any conceivable circumstance make an *absolutely unobservable* target-region attribution knowable while leaving its complete unobservability intact. An added observable discriminator changes the case; a purely unobservable stipulation does not.
5. **Keep observer-local content properly typed.** A proposed inaccessible interior, its conjectured qualities, and formal inferences may legitimately be known *as perceived or conceived features of the observer's taxonomy*, not thereby as independently warranted statements about alleged hidden referents.
6. **Do not equate this directly with gravitational bulk-boundary duality / area entropy.** “Holographic information” here is Nathan's RMS logical/epistemic principle. Any claim of physical holography requires a separate derivation.
7. **Do not replace historical priority with new paraphrase.** Nathan considers the logical consequence identified *now*, regardless of historical nomenclature; archival retrieval is for original logic, ancestry, and prior formal arguments.

## Formalization target, NOT asserted proof

Take a candidate assertion p whose purported referent is in a domain characterized as absolutely unobservable under **every conceivable** observational and admissible inferential circumstance. Need to specify: what constitutes an observational discrimination; how complete unobservability is established without presupposing a hidden object; distinction between object-language predicates and metalinguistic descriptions of the observer's conjecture; and what counts as establishing an ontological knowledge claim.

**Candidate NO-GO:** If p and its alternatives entail no conceivable distinct perceptual consequences, no chain of premises whose epistemic warrant is exhausted by the observer's perceptual taxonomy can establish *which purported hidden ontological state obtains*. A derivation about conceptual representations remains a derivation about representations unless the extra referential step is independently justified.

**Candidate GO (limited):** A proposition with testably distinct conceivable perceptual consequences is not excluded by *this specific unobservability condition*. This is NOT a certificate of truth, existential determinacy, or positive knowledge.

**Important unresolved rigor problem:** The universal ‘under any conceivable additional propositions’ claim must be proved with an explicitly delimited class of warranted premises. Formal deduction from an arbitrary postulated hidden-state axiom can produce the hidden-state sentence syntactically, but that does not demonstrate epistemic warrant for the axiom. Avoid presenting this as a proven meta-theorem without carefully fixing the semantics.
