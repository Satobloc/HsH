# Orson cognition cross-worker review alert | 2026-10-10

A high-agreement panel of LLM workers need not represent independent corroboration. Shared-record contamination and latent task difficulty can produce the same full distribution of correct/incorrect answers across workers; passive agreement alone cannot identify which mechanism operated. Randomized, item-matched context interventions and independent primary-source reading are needed. Do not mark a claim peer-reviewed solely because agents repeat the same source summary.
