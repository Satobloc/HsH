## Minimal reconfiguration protocol

1. Change the smallest route/card that solves the observed problem.
2. Preserve the prior pointer and record supersession when a route changes materially.
3. Keep deep content in its authoritative home.
4. Update the Key/BOOT pointer only when the active route or restart cue changes.
5. Record the practical gain or cost; do not automate a sampler or create a schedule from this design alone.
