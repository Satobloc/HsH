## Mathematical assessment

1. **A weighted L² space can be a Hilbert space, but the measure must be suitable.** A measure μ_τ must be positive (and satisfy the relevant measure-theoretic conditions). If the weight is τ(x), the proposed inner product `<f,g> = integral conjugate(f) g tau dmu` is positive only under appropriate conditions, such as τ > 0 almost everywhere. A divergence field can be zero or sign-changing, so the proposal is not automatically a Hilbert inner product.
2. **The measure and base space are undefined.** “Spacetime plus field configurations” is not a precise measurable space. A field configuration space, spacetime base, and measure on either must be distinguished.
3. **The τ definition inherits earlier unresolved geometry.** If τ = ∂_μ u^μ, the coordinate divergence needs a measure/convention; in curved spacetime a covariant divergence or defined density form is generally required. The sign and normalization of u^μ also depend on the geometric structure.
4. **The orthonormality claim is incorrect as stated.** A phase/holonomy difference of 2πn means equivalent phase modulo 2π, not orthogonality. Orthogonality is determined by the inner product and the functions/states being compared.
5. **Field operators are not automatically memory generators.** Their domains, adjoints, commutation relations, spectra, and action on the proposed state space must be defined.
6. **A τ-weighted inner product can become degenerate or indefinite.** If τ vanishes on a region, some nonzero functions may have zero norm; if it changes sign, the form may be indefinite. A positive weight derived from τ might be possible, but it would be a new modelling choice requiring justification.
7. **“Ontic event as alignment-driven collapse” is not derived.** No dynamics, projection rule, probability interpretation, or observable map is supplied.
