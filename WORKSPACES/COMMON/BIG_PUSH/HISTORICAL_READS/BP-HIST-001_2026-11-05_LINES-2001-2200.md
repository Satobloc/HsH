High-pressure xenon short-arc lamps and their quartz envelopes can be hazardous even when not operating. Do not energize, open, strike, puncture, or casually handle it; any inspection should follow manufacturer/specialist safety guidance. For this historical recovery, treat it only as an unpowered object and do not develop experiments involving its activation.

A residual-field hypothesis needs a derived persistence mechanism and measurable prediction.
