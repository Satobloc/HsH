## Recovered design value

This tranche yields a foundational design requirement for the Big Push and any SAT prediction workflow:

- Separate hypothesis generation from prediction registration and result evaluation.
- Freeze predictions before accessing the relevant outcome data.
- Record source exposure and information provenance.
- Keep independent or blinded evaluation where possible.
- Log all hints, summaries, prior discussions, and data access that could leak the answer.
- Treat post hoc recognition as a lead, never as a successful prediction.
- Require a leakage audit before claiming a prediction was genuinely novel or independently successful.
