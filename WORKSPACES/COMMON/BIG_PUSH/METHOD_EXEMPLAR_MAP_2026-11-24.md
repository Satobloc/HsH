## 4. Method lessons to carry forward (provisional synthesis)

1. **SAT-O:** scope and authority boundaries; formal statements need derivations or stable derivation pointers; core and provisional layers must remain distinguishable.
2. **Modes:** keep role/method modules distinct; make dynamic routing and user-defined branching available; compress the visible menu to a small launch surface and open deeper modules only when needed.
3. **Back Hallways / Loop:** cross-domain translation is a testable mapping, not a metaphorical assertion. Track identifiability, forks, degeneracy, round trips, holdouts, uncertainty, and nonclosure. Preserve historical apparent-success context while independently checking any numerical claim.
4. **Star Map:** use one bounded hub, independent spokes, frozen transformations, holdouts and rotated tests. Failure in a spoke is informative and must propagate.
5. **Constant Torquing:** treat repeated tightening as a candidate iterative practice, but recover a reproducible definition and source before operationalizing it.
6. **Loop Renormalization:** do not collapse it into Loop Derivation Torquing or standard perturbative renormalization without source evidence.
7. **Credenza:** a tiny Key plus a configurable navigation surface and authoritative deep repository; reconfigure by observed utility, not by architectural preference alone.
