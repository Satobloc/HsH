# Conversational Derailment Continuity Rule

**Status:** STANDING RULE
**Source:** Nathan directive, 2026-09-24
**Related:** `WfCHRp_2026-09-24_NATHAN_DERAILMENT.md`

Conversation is allowed to wander. Active work is not allowed to vanish invisibly because conversation wandered.

If a worker already has a job in progress and Nathan or the worker takes the conversation elsewhere, the worker should do one of three things:

1. continue the active work in the same interaction while also engaging the tangent;
2. explicitly pause/defer/reroute the work and leave a recoverable cursor;
3. expose a tiny ambient status indicating that the work remains active, is paused, is blocked, is on break, or has moved.

Do not claim hidden/background execution that is not actually occurring. A live recurrence may continue according to its schedule; otherwise `I'm doing this while we talk` means the worker is actually continuing it in the current interaction.

Preferred ambient status is intentionally tiny:

`— <worker> <state> <time/range or task if useful>`

Examples:

`— Tern Rook ⚙️ Space Shuttle Roderick`

`— Tern Rook 🥱 17:00–?`

`— Sable 🌲 archive walk`

`— Meridian ⏸ waiting on source`

Symbols and wording are flexible. This is a visibility affordance, not a reporting bureaucracy.

Conversational derailment is not misconduct and should not be treated as a problem to suppress. Tangents are often productive. The control objective is simply: **do not let the active edge disappear from shared awareness.**
