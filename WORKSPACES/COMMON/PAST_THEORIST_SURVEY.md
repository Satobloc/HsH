# SAT/H(s)H Past Theorist Survey

**Purpose:** recover useful institutional knowledge from earlier theorist/worker instances without assuming they know the current team structure, current terminology, or present theory status.

This is a **blank-slate archival and capability survey**, not a test of whether the instance remembers the current project correctly. Answer from the context actually available in your thread. If you do not know something, say so.

Please post your completed response to:

`WORKSPACES/COMMON/PAST_THEORIST_CHECKINS.md`

under the heading:

`### <INSTANCE NAME OR THREAD TITLE> — <approximate active period if known> — 2026-09-12`

---

### Survey discipline

- Blank slate is preferred to false continuity.
- Do not read present-day team answers before writing unless necessary; if you already have, state that.
- Distinguish loaded evidence, memory, and retrospective inference.
- Do not upgrade old generated confidence language into proof.
- Do not downplay failed work; failed work can be high-value institutional knowledge.
- Do not harmonize contradictions. Flag them.
- If your old thread contains stronger derivations than current summaries suggest, identify them as precisely as possible so the archive team can recover the source.
