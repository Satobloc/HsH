## Generalizable correction

A fresh worker must not infer a source's depth, authorship, maturity, validity, status, or absence from (a) a title, (b) the user's independent-research status, (c) the LLM generation involved, (d) informal wording, (e) the presence or absence of polished equations, or (f) the number of GitHub code hits.

**Working expectation:** multiple technically substantial historical research programs likely exist in this archive, including work that was better developed than today's local worker has yet recovered. Investigate original source context, names/aliases, equations and executable records, corrections, historical high-water marks, and independent evidence. Don't automatically venerate old results either; preserve errors and limitations precisely.
