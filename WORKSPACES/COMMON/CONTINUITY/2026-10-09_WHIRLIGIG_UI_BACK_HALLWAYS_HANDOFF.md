### Mechanical subtleties requiring honest engineering
- A sphere of radius exactly equal to the torus **tube radius** is a limiting contact configuration: contact can be degenerate rather than a unique single point. Separate: **historical exact-radius mode** (kinematics with constraint diagnostics) vs **finite-clearance single-contact physical rolling mode**. Do not advertise full no-slip at an extended contact locus.
- Existing solver uses `annulus_parameter= -[(torus_major_radius + ball_radius)/ball_radius]·psi`. Verify inside/outside rolling sign, contact path, and actual no-slip with `v_contact = Cdot+ω×r_contact` evaluated against the stationary wall; do NOT infer physical no-slip from the prescribed ratio. Independently check body rotation orthogonality, pin coincidence, connector perpendicularity, ray intersection, and closure of **position + tangent + full orientation/holonomy**. Matching plotted endpoints alone is insufficient.
- Hard-coded gimbals must specify axis order, handedness, SO(3) or SO(4) composition, units, and reference frames. Need independent tests that frame orthonormality and determinant stay at 1, and that no interpolated frame flips by 180°.
- Closure cannot be manufactured by joining endpoints or by forcing an arbitrary Fourier cutoff. Record winding ratios, near-return, residual and initial-condition mismatch. If rolling frequency is irrational relative to torus winding, honest result may be nonclosure.
