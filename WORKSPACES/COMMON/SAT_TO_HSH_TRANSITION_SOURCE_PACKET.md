## How to use this packet

- **Historical source is not current endorsement.** Do not infer Nathan's present position merely because an idea appears in one of these documents or because he gathered the packet.
- **Preserve provenance and chronology.** Distinguish Nathan-origin statements, contemporaneous notes, later synthesis, LLM-generated formalization, code, and retrospective summaries.
- **Recover before reconciling.** Keep contradictory definitions and alternative constructions separate until their source lineage and intended objects are established.
- **Geometry leads.** For any proposed mechanism, equation, or paper claim about a geometric form, identify and show the precise geometric object and its relations. A mathematical expression is not a substitute for the geometry it is meant to describe.
- **No automatic promotion.** Inclusion here does not confer BEDROCK status, Factory Floor admission, Lean clearance, physical validation, or publication authorization.
- **Translate proposition by proposition.** The transition question is what survives, changes, becomes a limiting case, or must be discarded when SAT's historically worldline/filament-based descriptions are reconstructed in H(s)H's finite-core/worldtube geometry.
