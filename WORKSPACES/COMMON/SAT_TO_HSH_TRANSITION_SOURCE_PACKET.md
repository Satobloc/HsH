## Source hierarchy and research weighting

The source packet is **primary transition material, but not an exclusive canon**. The following hierarchy governs where to begin and how to weight evidence. It is a research-routing rule, not a claim that every source within a tier is correct, current, or equally authoritative.

### 1. Principal SAT source: the full Satobloc 2023–25 archive

The **complete [Satobloc/SAT_THEORY_ARCHIVE_2023-25 repository](https://github.com/Satobloc/SAT_THEORY_ARCHIVE_2023-25)** remains the best overall source for SAT. The four locations above are the initial entry points Nathan supplied and should be worked first, but they do not bound the search. Follow references outward through the full archive whenever a derivation, definition, geometric construction, prediction, correction, or earlier source requires it.

Within the SAT lineage, give particular attention to the **4DHH and other hyperhelical formulations**. These are much closer to SAT's actual hyperhelical theoretical conception than the later EFT formulation in the Big Paper. Do not let the Big Paper's greater formal consolidation or accessibility make it the default definition of SAT.

### 2. H(s)H conversation archive: corroboration and recovery of working thought

Use the **CONVOS in the HsH repository** alongside the source archive. They are essential for recovering fuller discussion, intermediate reasoning, questions, corrections, calculations, and the development of Nathan's thinking. Use them to corroborate and contextualize source documents, and to locate fuller treatments of calculations or arguments that a compiled paper or summary only partially preserves.

Conversation material must still be provenance-checked: distinguish Nathan's statements and decisions from assistant-generated suggestions, expansions, and formalizations. A conversation is valuable evidence of development, not automatic authorization or proof.

### 3. Main construction corpus: 4DHH, Blockwave, related materials, and October 2025–approximately April 2026

For **solid construction work**, the main concentration is the group of 4DHH constructions, Blockwave and related materials, and the materials from roughly October 2025 through April 2026. These should be read across both the archive and HsH CONVOS to recover the actual geometry, derivational chains, calculations, and discussion in sufficient depth to reconstruct and test the constructions.

The gathered folders are best understood as a mixed transition packet: **largely Nathan's H(s)H thoughts as they developed, together with substantial supporting SAT material**. Do not flatten that mixture into either “all current H(s)H” or “merely historical SAT.” Track each item's role and chronology.

### 4. Big Paper: useful EFT branch, not the master source for SAT

The Big Paper remains useful as an **EFT formulation and comparative formalization**. It can supply mathematical machinery, show one route by which selected ideas were expressed effectively, and help identify approximations, translations, and departures. But it is not, by itself, the primary or definitive statement of SAT. When its formulation differs from the hyperhelical constructions, preserve the difference and investigate its origin rather than silently privileging the EFT account.

### 5. Open-source rule: primary does not mean exclusive

Search beyond these named clusters when the source trail points elsewhere: earlier notes, related derivations, later corrections, code, diagrams, experimental discussions, and other HsH materials may contain essential evidence. Record both positive and negative findings, and do not treat absence from the initial folders as evidence that an idea or calculation does not exist elsewhere in the archive.

## How to use this packet

- **Historical source is not current endorsement.** Do not infer Nathan's present position merely because an idea appears in one of these documents or because he gathered the packet.
- **Preserve provenance and chronology.** Distinguish Nathan-origin statements, contemporaneous notes, later synthesis, LLM-generated formalization, code, and retrospective summaries.
- **Recover before reconciling.** Keep contradictory definitions and alternative constructions separate until their source lineage and intended objects are established.
- **Geometry leads.** For any proposed mechanism, equation, or paper claim about a geometric form, identify and show the precise geometric object and its relations. A mathematical expression is not a substitute for the geometry it is meant to describe.
- **No automatic promotion.** Inclusion here does not confer BEDROCK status, Factory Floor admission, Lean clearance, physical validation, or publication authorization.
- **Translate proposition by proposition.** The transition question is what survives, changes, becomes a limiting case, or must be discarded when SAT's hyperhelical constructions and their supporting geometric/physical commitments are reconstructed in H(s)H. Do not reduce the SAT starting point to the Big Paper's EFT-specific worldline/filament and time-front formulation.
