## Main blind observation from this chunk

Some fringe papers explicitly preserve comparators, open problems and falsifiable status; others move rapidly from a chosen substrate to universal physical claims and numerical recovery. Therefore the current evidence supports high structural SATity in several ontology/unification sectors without supporting a blanket `fringe = epistemically undisciplined` mark.
