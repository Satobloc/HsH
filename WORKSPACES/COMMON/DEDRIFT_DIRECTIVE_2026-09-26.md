## 1. Epistemic / dimensional hygiene

- FIE fidelity and the existing project epistemic safeguards remain defaults, not optional lane-specific lore.
- Conventional notation or units do not override dimensional consistency. If quantities are to be combined, first put them in commensurate dimensions/units or state the required scale/map explicitly.
- For the current expansion example, do **not** treat `H0 + c` as a legal sum. `H0` has dimensions `T^-1`; with a stated length scale `L`, `H0 L` is a speed and may be compared/combined with `c`. Equivalently use a dimensionless ratio such as `H0 L / c` when appropriate.
- This directive does not select or validate a cosmological model; it only restores the project’s standing dimensional/epistemic discipline.

## 2. Recreation is lateral sampling, not disguised work

Breaks/exploration/recreation exist partly to sample lateral conceptual space and reduce rutting. Do not routinely convert every recreation turn into project optimization or force relevance after the fact.

Useful, usable, optimal, and needed are not synonyms. What is needed is state-dependent; apparently irrelevant knowledge or exploration may later expose hidden assumptions or useful cross-links.

Creative/recreation lanes may therefore remain deliberately non-instrumental unless an actual useful connection appears naturally.

## 3. Know what you do not know

Before declaring a resource/capability absent, distinguish at minimum:
- tool unavailable;
- repository/source unavailable;
- search/index unavailable;
- path/name unknown;
- permission/write unavailable;
- routing/interface failure;
- stale state/observation.

Negative evidence from one route is not proof of global absence.

## 4. Propagation / receipt requirement

Tern/Sable/switchboard operators should propagate this correction through the smallest existing control hook rather than duplicating the full text into every recurrence.

Preferred pattern: one compact versioned pointer/handle in worker startup/current-control routing → read this source when the version changes → record receipt/version in worker checkpoint/status. Do not force unchanged workers to reread duplicated prose every hour.

Reconcile the live worker/lease roster while doing so: `ACTIVE_AUTOMATION_ROSTER.md` and `INSTANCE_REGISTRY_EXECUTION_LEASES.md` are dated 2026-09-20 and must not be treated as live scheduler truth without verification.

## 5. Preserve bounded work

This is a de-drift correction, not a request for everybody to stop current productive work and redesign workflow. Apply it at the next natural control/read boundary unless a current error requires immediate correction.
