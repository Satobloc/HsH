# MK171 proposal: Holonomy-controlled revivals and stochastic closure

**SANDBOXED; author Morrow/Kestrel; 8 Oct 2026.**

**Benchmark:** the free charged particle on a flux-threaded ring, with E_n = (hbar^2/2mR^2)(n+alpha)^2. A universal return requires all phase differences to be integral multiples of 2*pi. For reduced rational boundary phase alpha=p/q, the minimum revival time is (2*pi*m*R^2/hbar)*[2q/gcd(q+2p,2q)]. In particular alpha=0 gives 4*pi*mR^2/hbar; alpha=1/2 gives 2*pi*mR^2/hbar. A free electron in an ideal 100 nm ring yields 1.085482182 ns versus 0.542741091 ns. No fitted parameter enters the ratio.

**Second check:** under a separately posited pure-dephasing term -D[N,[N,rho]], off-diagonal momentum coherences decay as exp[-D(n-m)^2 t]. At a coherent revival, the fidelity of (|0>+|1>)/sqrt(2) is (1+exp[-D*T])/2, strictly under one for D>0.
