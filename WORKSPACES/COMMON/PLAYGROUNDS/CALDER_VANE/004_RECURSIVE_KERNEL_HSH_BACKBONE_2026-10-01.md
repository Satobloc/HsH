## 1. Starting point

Use a parent carrier X_0(s,t) and recursive internal/child coordinates X_1,...,X_N. At each level let the quadratic Fourier-space action be

L_n = 1/2 rho_n omega^2 |X_n|^2
      -1/2 D_n(k)|X_n|^2
      -1/2 Lambda_n |X_{n+1}-P_n(k)X_n|^2,

where D_n(k) is the intrinsic stiffness of level n and P_n(k) is the linearized ᚼ promotion operator.

For curvature-driven promotion, P_n(k)=-ell_n^2 k^2 is the simplest local choice.

## 2. Exact recursive elimination

Let K_n(k,omega) be the inverse response kernel of level n after all deeper levels have been integrated out. Then the two-level quadratic block is

1/2 [K_n^0 + Lambda_n |P_n|^2]|X_n|^2
+1/2 [K_{n+1}+Lambda_n]|X_{n+1}|^2
- Lambda_n Re[X_{n+1}^* P_n X_n],

with K_n^0 = D_n(k)-rho_n omega^2.

Eliminating X_{n+1} gives the exact Schur-complement recursion

K_n =
K_n^0
+ Lambda_n |P_n|^2
- Lambda_n^2 |P_n|^2/(Lambda_n+K_{n+1}).

Equivalently,

K_n =
K_n^0
+ |P_n|^2 Lambda_n K_{n+1}/(Lambda_n+K_{n+1}).

This is the desired continued-fraction engine.

Iterating gives a nested rational response rather than a fundamental infinite derivative polynomial.

## 4. Infinite self-similar fixed point

Now impose a scale-stationary toy hierarchy:

D_n-rho_n omega^2 -> K_0,
Lambda_n=Lambda,
ell_n=ell,
and after rescaling suppose K_{n+1}=K_n=K_*.

Then

K_* = K_0 + p K_*/(Lambda+K_*),

where p=Lambda ell^4 k^4.

Multiply through:

(K_*-K_0)(Lambda+K_*)=p K_*.

Hence

K_*^2 + (Lambda-K_0-p)K_* - Lambda K_0=0.

So

K_* =
1/2 [
K_0+p-Lambda
± sqrt((Lambda-K_0-p)^2+4 Lambda K_0)
].

The physical branch is whichever continuously approaches K_0 as ell -> 0 and respects positivity/stability in Euclidean response.

This is a nontrivial closed-form response of an infinite recursive ᚼ hierarchy.
