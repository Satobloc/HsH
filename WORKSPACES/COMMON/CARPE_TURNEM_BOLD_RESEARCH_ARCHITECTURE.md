# Carpe Turnem — Bold Research Operating Architecture

**Status:** Common operating doctrine
**Scope:** SAT / H(s)H research work across automated and interactive workers
**Principle:** preservation is the floor, not the ceiling.

## Purpose

The archive-recovery phase has produced enough wayfinding, provenance control, worker coordination, and search infrastructure that the project can increasingly use the recovered corpus as a research instrument rather than treating excavation as the final product.

This document defines how workers may move aggressively into new theoretical work without blurring historical SAT/H(s)H, Nathan-authored material, assistant-generated reconstruction, speculative extensions, or material that is explicitly quarantined.

The operating cycle is:

**PRESERVE → RECOVER → FORMALIZE → SANDBOX → STRESS-TEST → PROMOTE / RETAIN / KILL**

“Carpe turnem” means: when a worker has a productive next action within its authority, take the turn. Do not replace execution with repeated agreement or wait for Nathan merely to reconfirm an already-authorized direction.

## Research zones

### 1. QUARANTINE — NONO

Quarantined material is excluded from ordinary research inheritance, search, synthesis, theory development, and evidence-building unless Nathan explicitly opens a narrowly defined quarantine task.

Default Mersearch exclusions remain QUARANTINE and PRIOR_ART.

A worker must not use quarantined assistant-generated theory as an independent source, allow quarantine material to leak into public indexes/facets/snippets/summaries, or silently rehabilitate quarantined claims by paraphrasing them elsewhere.

### 2. HISTORICAL / SOURCE RECORD — PRESERVE

This is the provenance-governed layer: Nathan's original words, dated conversations, notebooks, papers, diagrams, code, public releases, source documents, and verified external historical/scientific sources.

Claims here should distinguish what the source literally says; who said/wrote it; when; source identity and ancestry; later correction or contradiction; and unresolved ambiguity. Archive polish does not establish theoretical correctness.

### 3. THEORIZING — LATER / CANDIDATE

This is the staging layer for ideas promising enough to formulate carefully but not yet promoted into the working theoretical core.

A theorizing item should carry motivating sources, explicit assumptions, mathematical objects, proposed relation to existing SAT/H(s)H, known conflicts, a falsification/stress-test route where possible, and status/provenance labels. Chronology alone does not make an item current.

### 4. SANDBOX — THEORIZING

Normal home for bold new derivations, model extensions, alternative formalisms, mathematical experiments, computational probes, and attempts to exploit the 4D geometric representation more fully.

Workers are encouraged to derive rather than merely summarize; translate geometric intuition into explicit objects/equations; look for hidden constraints and redundancies; seek cross-sector consequences; compare independent derivation routes; write executable checks; search the archive for antecedents after or alongside independent derivation so retrieval does not become forced confirmation; and record failures and negative results.

Sandbox results are **not automatically SAT/H(s)H doctrine**.

### 5. PLAYGROUND — WILD-EYED STUFF-N-STUFF

Maximum exploratory freedom: strange geometric constructions, unusual correspondences, deliberately overextended analogies, “what happens if…” mathematics, visual experiments, alternate parameterizations, speculative bridges across scale or sector.

The price of freedom is labeling. Playground material may be exciting, useful, wrong, redundant, or nonsense. It must remain distinguishable from source-backed reconstruction and validated mathematics.

## The Minkowski research lane

A major project lane is to investigate what additional structure, constraints, or explanatory compression can be obtained by taking the empirically successful four-dimensional spacetime representation seriously as a geometric research instrument and systematically exploring uses that may have been historically underdeveloped.

This is a **research program, not a granted conclusion**.

Workers should separate three questions:

1. **Historical:** What did Minkowski and subsequent foundational figures actually propose, expect, reject, or leave open? Use RESOURCES/HISTORY and primary/strong secondary sources where available.
2. **Representational:** Which parts of the 4D representation are fixed by established physics, which are conventions/coordinates, and which additional geometric structures are SAT/H(s)H hypotheses?
3. **Generative:** Given those constraints, what can be derived, modeled, or ruled out before adding new primitives?

The project may strongly prefer geometric economy as a model-selection prior. That preference does not itself establish physical truth.

## Promotion ladder

No automatic promotion by enthusiasm, recency, repetition, worker count, filename, or apparent elegance.

PLAYGROUND → SANDBOX-CANDIDATE → DERIVED → COMPUTATIONALLY-CHECKED → INDEPENDENTLY-REPRODUCED → SOURCE-CROSSWALKED → CORE-CANDIDATE → explicit project/core adoption.

At any stage an item may instead become FAILED, CONTRADICTED, DUPLICATE, HISTORICAL-ONLY, UNRESOLVED, or RETAIN-AS-HEURISTIC.

For mathematics, retain the established project distinction among claimed / disclaimed / claimed verified / multiply verified where applicable. Do not call a derivation “verified” merely because one LLM produced it.

## Autonomous worker loop

When no higher-priority explicit assignment blocks it, a research worker may:

1. Read current roster/state-of-play and relevant onboarding.
2. Pick the highest-value unblocked task in its lane.
3. Retrieve only the source material needed.
4. Do the work now.
5. Produce an inspectable artifact: derivation, code, test, crosswalk, source map, failure report, or concise result.
6. Stress-test it.
7. Record provenance and status.
8. Update shared state/checkpoint where authorized.
9. Immediately take the next productive turn.

Escalate to Nathan when the next action changes project meaning/core doctrine rather than testing it; source ambiguity cannot be resolved without authorial intent; quarantine access is required; two high-value branches require a priority choice Nathan should make; or an apparent major result survives serious internal challenge and is ready for human attention.

Do **not** escalate merely because the next step requires initiative.

## Infrastructure relationship

Mersearch, Viewer, indexes, provenance tooling, recurrence loops, and archive maps are enabling infrastructure for this mode. Their purpose is increasingly to support rapid antecedent checks, equation genealogy, source recovery, contradiction discovery, independent-route comparison, automated stress testing, targeted historical research, and reproducible theory archaeology.

The infrastructure remains valuable even as the center of gravity moves from excavation toward generative research.

## Safety rails

- QUARANTINE remains closed by default.
- Public/private corpus boundaries are enforced at index/build level.
- Assistant-generated material remains identifiable as such.
- Historical claims require source support.
- Mathematical checks are labeled by what was actually checked.
- Physical claims remain hypotheses unless supported at the appropriate evidentiary level.
- Failed derivations are preserved when informative.
- No worker may manufacture consensus by copying another worker's conclusion.
- Independent derivation is preferable before cross-contamination when testing robustness.
- Preserve disagreement where the archive genuinely disagrees.

## Short form

**Preserve the record. Recover the machinery. Formalize the question. Go wild in the sandbox. Try hard to break the result. Promote only what earns it. Then take the next turn.**
