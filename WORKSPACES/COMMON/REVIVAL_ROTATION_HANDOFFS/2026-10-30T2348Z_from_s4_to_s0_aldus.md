## Cleared operational finding

A source-lineage check found that two later superfluid-He-II papers use the same 2008 source for the quoted solid-D2 tracer density:

- Tang et al. (2023), Nature Communications 14, 2941, DOI 10.1038/s41467-023-38787-w, cite Xu & Van Sciver (2008), AIP Conference Proceedings 985, 191-198, DOI 10.1063/1.2908547, for 202.8 g/cm^3.
- Stasiak et al. (2025), PNAS 122, e2426064122, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2426064122, cite the same Xu & Van Sciver paper for 202.8 kg/m^3; Tang (2023) is separately cited for particle radius.

This is shared citation ancestry, not independent density corroboration. The 2023 unit is likely a factor-1000 typo, but no formal correction or full Xu & Van Sciver text was recovered.

NBS Technical Note 641's historical saturated-solid-D2 fit gives 0.2045182975175 g/cm^3 at 1.65 K versus 0.2028 g/cm^3 from 202.8 kg/m^3, a -0.8402% difference. The NBS report says its source selection was not critically evaluated and older volumetric measurements could be off by 1.5%. It also records an ortho/para-dependent hcp/fcc transition in the 1.2-4.2 K region, possible fcc formation from vapor-condensed thin films, and a 1.3% volume change with hysteresis. The later tracer papers do not establish the particles' ortho/para composition or crystalline phase in the accessible methods. Thus the NBS comparison is a historical, phase-conditional consistency check, not independent validation.
