The most useful methodological distinction is between social restraint and objective verification. The learned playbook used fewer turns and words and reduced reported friction, while objective outcomes were broadly comparable. The paper says its arithmetic-audit rule res-00003 was rarely executed.

A proposed, unimplemented design separates an adaptive social facilitator from a deterministic closure validator: remain quiet during productive exchange, but check objective constraints at commit without choosing the group's substantive outcome.
