## Interpretation

The robustness check changes the meaning of the Run 4 result. The FRINGE patient does show a recurring **unificatory / parameter-elimination / common-structure aspiration**, but the stronger SAT-like ontology cluster — one substrate for vacuum and matter, geometry-zoo particles, common-force substrate, emergent quantization — is concentrated in particular independent-theory families rather than uniformly distributed across all fringe work.

The safest current formulation is therefore:

1. FRINGE is more likely than CORE to contain **maximal unification and derivation-from-structure projects**.
2. Some fringe families are extremely SAT-like in ontology; others are not.
3. Methodological quality remains heterogeneous and cannot be scored by fringe status alone.
4. `AE002`, `AM017`, `R001`, and `AC001` now have the strongest cross-family support among the FRINGE PASS marks.
