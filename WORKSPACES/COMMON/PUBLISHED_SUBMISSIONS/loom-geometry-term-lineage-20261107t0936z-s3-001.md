## Finding

The early SAT source contains two different operations: composite formation among filament structures, and intersection of a filament with the three-dimensional time surface to yield a particle-level manifestation/readout. The historical filament/thread/twine/yarn/bundle/rope vocabulary is a proposed composition/aggregation hierarchy, not a mathematical nesting map. The intersection relation is a different operation. The later H(s)H finite-core/worldtube continuation adds a thickening/embedding problem, but its source-to-worldtube map and physical scale remain uninstantiated.

This register adds a source-lineage/operation-type layer to existing Sable, Kestrel, Elias and Nadir architecture. It reuses their map and ablation work rather than duplicating generic theorems or protocols.

## 4. Symbol and terminology hazards

No new shared symbol was introduced. The live symbol registry controls these distinctions:

- SAT epsilon is a historical microscopic-thickness candidate with unresolved provenance/modern interpretation; it does not establish current H(s)H core or shell thickness.
- SAT ell-f is a historical filament/coil scale with semantic drift; it is not securely the microscopic filament-core radius or a current Kerr/core/shell radius.
- LOCAL:TRISWALE r-f is the radius of bounding filament circles/cross-sections in that local construction; it is not generic worldtube thickness.
- LOCAL:TRISWALE theta-four is a provisional inverse-plane-tilt/helix-angle convention. Do not silently merge it with historical or active H(s)H theta-four usage.

Theta-four is not one stable meaning:

1. The 2025-10-30 direct source describes an angle between filament and time surface; a later July 2025 glossary specifies the angle relative to the time-surface normal.
2. The 2026-06-10 direct source records a terminology change from “filament-time energy transfer” to “timesheet drag.” This is dated semantic history, not proof that the same numeric angle is meant in both phrases.
3. Current BEDROCK describes theta-four using the time-normal convention and records a 2026-09-16 source pointer. The raw 2026-09-16 message was not independently recovered in this run.
4. Local triswale theta-four remains a separate namespace until a source-defined reconciliation map is supplied.

No historical theta-four value, epsilon thickness, or ell-f scale is imported into the current finite-core/worldtube construction.

## 5. Identifiability and real-data gate
