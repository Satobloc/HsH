## 3. Source-fidelity corrections

- **Klein chronology:** the August 26 direct source predates October 21. This supersedes Aster #1148’s earlier first-use claim. Ravel #1185 already records the direct definition.
- **090f5160:** mixed-source message; only the final correction clause is direct. It is not the origin or definition.
- **TII:** the direct source names the concept and a conservation idea. The assistant’s recursive notation is not direct and lacks a defined state space, operator, measure, and conservation condition.
- **θ4:** the direct August 27 convention is sheet-relative with a 90° vacuum limit. “Inverse pitch” and the oblateness relation are not equations until pitch and the mapping are defined.
- **Angle complement:** the later normal-relative convention is recorded in [Aster issue #675](https://github.com/Satobloc/HsH/issues/675), but its September 16 raw source event remains unrecovered. [Loom PR #1076](https://github.com/Satobloc/HsH/pull/1076) proves the conditional Euclidean relation θ_sheet + θ_normal = π/2. It does not prove inverse pitch or oblateness.
- **Frame boundary:** retain w_M=ct for the Minkowski control and cw_S=t, ++++ for the later native convention. The conversion map, units, and observer conditions remain unspecified. The attached dimensional-normalization PDF is unresolved.
- **Time bulk:** distinguish the interacting 3D bulk B³_t from a mathematical 3D slice/hypersurface Σ³_t. They are not interchangeable.
- **Readout:** the direct user rejects a passive readout interpretation. The Ravel module name MinimalCarrierReadout is therefore a terminology mismatch; its theorem is a set-level section/projection, not a physical interaction law.
- **Worldtube inertia:** the direct curvature/inertia identification remains underdefined. The existing WorldtubeInertiaCoordinate theorem assumes the critical acceleration-curvature relation and proves only its algebraic consequence.
- **Local/global:** the direct source warns that local dual-shell expansion need not equal the global Klein Fabric. A measure/projection map, not an identity, is required.
- **Cruller:** direct source calls it a working hypothesis without proof. The Whirligig solver is not a physical proof.
