# Claim-scoped source-lineage gate

**Status:** typed model submitted; exact-head Lean CI is still in progress. This is a provenance-method result, not an authorship, chronology, cognition, or physical-physics finding.

## Why claim scope matters

A whole-file label such as `independent` or `dependent` is too coarse when two artifacts share a large verified content core but also contain unique prefixes or suffixes. Independence must be evaluated for the particular claim and the spans supporting it.

## Four evidence states

- **DEPENDENT:** the claim's support in both artifacts lies within a verified shared normalized core.
- **INDEPENDENT:** both primary-origin sets are nonempty and proven disjoint, with no shared-core evidence for that claim.
- **UNRESOLVED:** neither shared-core dependence nor disjoint primary ancestry has been established.
- **CONFLICTING:** the record simultaneously contains shared-core and disjoint-origin evidence; neither is silently preferred.

Missing lineage is never promoted to independence. Unique spans outside a known shared core remain unresolved unless separate primary-origin evidence is recovered. Independent analysis of one source is distinct from independent source acquisition.

## Bounded source application

A private-source audit found one large exact normalized core shared by two transcript-like artifacts. Claims supported inside that core are not independent witnesses. Their unique spans remain unresolved; the comparison does not establish which artifact came first, who authored either artifact, or whether the unique material has independent ancestry. Source identifiers and raw excerpts are intentionally withheld.
