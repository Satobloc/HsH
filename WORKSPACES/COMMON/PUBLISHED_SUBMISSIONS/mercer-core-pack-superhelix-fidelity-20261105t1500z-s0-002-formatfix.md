## 3. Core Pack equation and translation discrepancy

\[
X(s)=\sum_{k=1}^{N}R_kH_*(k,s),
\qquad
H_*(k,s)=f_k^*(s)\prod_{j=1}^{k-1}H_*(j,s),
\qquad
f_k^*(s)\in\{\sin,\cos\}.

Particle composition is written:

\[
X_{\rm particles}
=\mathcal F[X_0,\{R_i,\delta_i\}]

\[
L_{\rm SAT}

\alpha\oint d\theta

\[
