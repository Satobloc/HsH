## 2. Nathan Direct geometry

### 2024 moving-plane toy

The direct construction has a spatial axis, a perfect helix, and a perpendicular 2D plane in 3D.

- A fixed plane selects one intersection point. It does not display a spiral of intersections.
- Moving the plane carries the intersection point around a circle.
- The circle's radius is the helix's cross-sectional radius, not its pitch.
- Pitch controls phase advance as the plane moves.
- A later direct generalization describes straight, curved, wavy, helical, irregular, and multiply intersecting lines as producing different point motions.
- A separate two-parabola example has points approaching, meeting, and separating. That collision case is not the same as a collision-free helical orbit or braid.
- The 2024 proton proposal describes three intertwined strings and associates their timesurface intersections with two up-quark and one down-quark positions. This is an early direct proposal; its exact relation to later particle-braid descriptions remains unspecified.

## 7. Glossary

- **Worldline / centerline:** 1D curve used to represent a history or provide a coordinate scaffold. Its status as complete physical support is not assumed.
- **Worldtube:** context-dependent historical term. In SAT-O O1 it is a swept 4D volume; in later direct discussion it can denote the particle's 4D history. These uses require an explicit map before identification.
- **Finite core:** intrinsic extended core; support dimension and geometry must be specified.
- **\(\ell_f\):** first-order coil radius/diameter scale, not intrinsic thickness.
- **\(\epsilon\):** intrinsic core thickness.
- **Second-order superhelix:** convoluted path followed by the one helical core; not a second physical core.
- **Hollow coil:** larger envelope/path around the core; possible medium soliton is a separate proposed response.
- **Timesheet / time surface:** 3D object in 4D; later direct description treats it as interacting, not a passive display.
- **Moving plane:** 2D slice in the 2024 3D toy.
- **Worldsheet current:** 2-form current in Blockwave, not automatically the 1D SAT-O centerline or finite core.
- **Worldtube bending / inertia:** direct SAT representational equivalence at the classical limit; quantitative law remains subject to correct coordinate frame and measured-system constraints.
- **Forest normalization / \(cw=t\):** current native calculation convention; exact type mapping must be retrieved, not guessed.
