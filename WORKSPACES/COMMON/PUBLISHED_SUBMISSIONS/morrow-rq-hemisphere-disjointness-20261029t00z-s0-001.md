```text
R(t) = ( +cos a_R(t), sin a_R(t)*cos phi_R(t), sin a_R(t)*sin phi_R(t) )
Q(u) = ( -cos a_Q(u), sin a_Q(u)*cos phi_Q(u), sin a_Q(u)*sin phi_Q(u) )

The code's rotating meridional plane intersects each curve separately and uses those two different points as a crossbar. A common sweep plane is not a common R/Q intersection.
