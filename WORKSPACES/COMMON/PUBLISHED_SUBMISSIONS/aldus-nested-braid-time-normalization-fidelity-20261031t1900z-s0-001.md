## Finding

The archive's apparent particle-ontology conflicts mostly mix distinct
geometric levels:

1.  Single-coil 4D filament/worldline.
2.  Finite-core 4D worldtube, with core thickness distinct from coil
    radius.
3.  Intersection with a 3D time surface.
4.  Quark braids and a nested He-3 braid.
5.  Bosonic excitations carried by filaments, rather than identified
    with the filaments.
6.  Global Klein-Fabric geometry, whose local projection remains
    undefined.

The missing unifier is a typed composition of maps.
