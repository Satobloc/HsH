## 3. New synthesis: four entries, but not four equivalent scale transformations

The h747 report usefully separated four places where "scale" had been doing too much work. This pass sharpens the typing: **only the first two are plainly geometric scale loci. The third is topology/constituent organization; the fourth is a representational or compositional map.** Calling all four "scale factors" risks repeating the original category error.

| Item | Better type | What may change | What is still missing |

The July 11 direct correction is decisive for the third row: braid nesting is not superhelical nesting. The source does not supply a single operator that decides among them.

The Primary Guideline's scale-dependent flexibility and response are best treated as properties to compare or derive under a specified scale map, not as extra primitives or independent maps by themselves.

## 4. Primitive economy is a separate classification axis

The two-primitive versus provisional three-primitive question is not another scale locus.

A useful bookkeeping distinction is:

- **Carrier structures:** worldline/worldtube and timesheet.
- **Interaction relation:** their mutual accommodation or force.
- **Provisional responsive medium:** a possible additional structural object, to be eliminated, absorbed into a restructured timesheet, or retained only if required.

This resolves one potential verbal collision without pretending to settle the ontology. Nathan's September 7 phrase "the force is the true primitive" occurs in a statement about the relation between the filament and timesheet. The November 8 guideline's "third primitive" is a responsive/reactive medium. The former can be read as a primitive interaction relation; the latter is a candidate carrier/medium structure. They are not automatically the same kind of thing. Whether Nathan intends a distinct relational primitive in the formal ontology remains open.

The responsive medium must also remain distinct from:
- the finite-core profile of a SAT worldtube;
- an ordinary-fluid vortex core;
- the Kelvin-vortex-inspired response mechanism;
- the timesheet itself.

The Primary Guideline explicitly warns that an ordinary-fluid vortex core and a SAT matter worldtube are different physical structures unless an explicit derived map identifies them. No such map is supplied here.

## 7. Discrepancy ledger

1. **"Scale factor" is under-typed.** June 23 explicitly leaves its placement unresolved. The later four-part decomposition helps, but it should not call topology and coarse-graining scalar scale transformations.
2. **Braid versus superhelix.** The July 11 direct correction forbids treating these as one recursive nesting operator.
3. **Generator versus finite core.** The SAT4D curve is one-dimensional. A finite-core worldtube requires a separate lift/profile construction; `Phi_core` remains undefined.
4. **Force versus medium.** "Force is the true primitive" is a relational statement in its local context; the responsive medium is a provisional third structural primitive in the later methodological capture. Do not merge them without source-defined typing.
5. **Medium versus vortex versus worldtube.** Kelvin-vortex response, responsive medium, ordinary-fluid vortex core, and SAT matter worldtube remain distinct until a derived map is supplied.
6. **Hierarchy versus physical nesting.** A coarse-graining or parent/child representation is not automatically a physical nested worldtube.
7. **Projection versus intersection.** Literal intersection with a timesheet and a projection/readout are different operations.
8. **Lorentzian SAT4D versus current H(s)H geometry.** The existing SAT4D timelike gate is conditional on c=1 and signature (-+++). It does not map that historical Lorentzian ansatz into the current positive-definite native geometry.
9. **He-3 radius versus braid envelope.** The published CREMA helion rms charge radius 1.97007(94) fm is a charge-density second moment, not automatically a braid-envelope radius.
