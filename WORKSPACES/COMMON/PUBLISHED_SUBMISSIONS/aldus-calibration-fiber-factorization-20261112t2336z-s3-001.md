# Run-dependent calibration and reduced-law factorization

**Status:** CONDITIONAL REPRESENTATION RESULT / FORMALIZATION IN PROGRESS / NOT A PHYSICAL PREDICTION.

This note extends the existing assistant-authored law-factorization and instrument-masking discussion in [issue #1591](https://github.com/Satobloc/HsH/issues/1591) and [issue #1603](https://github.com/Satobloc/HsH/issues/1603). It does not attribute the proposal to Nathan Direct.

## 1. Representation boundary

Let X be a state space, pi:X->R a proposed reduced representation, K_phys:X->P a physical-law map, c:X->C a run/state-dependent calibration state, and M:CxP->O a deterministic instrument/readout map. The observed map is:

    K_obs(x) = M(c(x), K_phys(x)).

Three conditional results are formalized in the draft module:

1. If both K_phys and c factor through pi, then K_obs factors through pi.
2. If two states share pi and the same physical-law value, but their calibration states produce different instrument outputs, then K_obs cannot factor through pi.
3. If calibration is appended to the reduced representation, pi_plus(x)=(pi(x),c(x)), then K_obs factors through pi_plus whenever K_phys factors through pi.

Thus calibration is not automatically a new physical primitive. It is a representation variable that must either be recoverable from the reduced state, be fixed/controlled by the instrument protocol, or be retained in the augmented state.

## 2. Measurable boundary

The draft also encodes the Doob-Dynkin route: if calibration and physical-law maps are measurable with respect to the pullback sigma-algebra generated by pi, the instrument map is measurable, and the observed-output codomain is nonempty and standard Borel, then the observed map has a measurable factor H:R->O.

The imported standard result is Mathlib 4.34.0, Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Function/FactorsThrough.lean, theorem Measurable.exists_eq_measurable_comp. This is a measurable-function factorization, not a probability-measure or Markov-kernel construction. A stochastic instrument kernel, disintegration, or run-indexed pushforward remains unformalized.

Observed-law factorization does not imply physical-law factorization: an instrument may mask physical differences. The result is sufficient in the stated direction, not a general equivalence.

## 3. Independent test contract

| Element | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Hypothesis | A reduced-state-only observed predictor fails because calibration state varies within a pi-fiber. |
| Shared parameters | Freeze pi, K_phys, instrument map, calibration version, nuisance parameters, units and output metric. |
| Calibration | Characterize instrument response and calibration ancestry before holdout access. |
| Holdout | Independent acquisitions or matched states, with raw provenance and full covariance. |
| Observable | Observed-law/readout differences among states sharing the proposed reduced representation and matched physical-law state. |
| Standard comparator | A fixed-calibration predictor factoring through pi, compared with the augmented predictor factoring through (pi,c). |
| Falsification | A reproducible within-fiber observed difference exceeding the preregistered uncertainty tolerance rejects the reduced-state-only predictor for the declared domain. |

This is a test design, not a measured result. No physical system, raw dataset, calibration package, units, covariance, holdout, fit, numerical prediction, or independent measurement was used.
