## 3. Source-faithful taxonomy

The direct 2024 outline describes a composition sequence:

- Filament: 1D constituent.
- Thread: filaments tightly intertwined.
- Twine: threads intertwined.
- Yarn: twines bound together.
- Bundle: yarns bound/interconnected into composite structures.
- Rope: large cohesive group of bundles, with gravity-dominated spheroidal bodies as a proposed large-scale manifestation.

The direct outline separately describes **composite strands** (threads, twines, yarns, bundles) and **strand complexes** (ropes). It does not define unique embedding, inclusion, projection, or composition maps between every level.

Other direct relations:
- Yarns may interconnect multiple bundles, so the bundle-level organization is networked, not only nested.
- Organisms are described as persistent 4D patterns whose constituent yarns can change. Fixed membership is not the source's identity criterion.
- Heliod/heloid, loop, and tether morphologies are explicitly not rope-exclusive; smaller strand structures may share them.
- The spherical time-surface expansion is a simplifying assumption. Other 4D wavefront geometries remain possible.
- The source distinguishes complexity arising from interactions among 4D structures from the 3D manifestation/readout of those structures. The intersection/readout is not asserted to cause the complexity.

2. **Cross-links:** bundles may be connected by yarn networks, so a strict tree loses direct-source connectivity.
3. **Pattern persistence:** organism identity is a pattern-level description with constituent turnover, not fixed-member identity.
4. **Morphology:** heloid/loop/tether descriptors crosscut levels; treating them as a rope-only class is inaccurate.
5. **Dimensional typing:** only the filament is explicitly 1D in the source; composite “3D manifestations” are not backed by carrier dimensions or a readout map.
6. **Readout vs cause:** the direct source places complexity in interactions and 3D appearance in manifestation; assistant summaries sometimes collapse or invert that distinction.
7. **Core substitution:** a finite-core W⁴ around γ¹ is a separate 2026 candidate; no map from a multistrand bundle/rope to that core/support is supplied.
