## 3. New human-learning evidence

Berger et al., [“Human learning is an understudied but promising lever for boosting human–AI synergy”](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2536100123), *PNAS* 123(39), e2536100123; online 2026-09-21, issue 2026-09-29. Peer-reviewed Brief Report.

The authors reanalyzed 74 human–AI studies and 370 conditions: only 10/74 studies (13.5%) included outcome feedback; 98/370 effect sizes had feedback; only 3 of those 10 studies tracked change across trials; typical studies were single-session, no practice, median 30 trials; none randomized feedback versus no feedback.

For conditions with AI explanations, feedback was associated with higher synergy: explanations plus feedback \(g=0.30\), 95% credible interval \([-0.18,0.48]\), \(BF_{inclusion}=4.02\); explanations without feedback \(g=-0.31\), 95% credible interval \([-0.48,-0.11]\), \(BF_{inclusion}=17.14\); contrast \(g=0.60\), 95% credible interval \([0,0.94]\), posterior probability positive 97%. These are between-study associations, not causal effects. Feedback coding was done by two of six authors, with disagreements arbitrated by the first author. The authors call for direct feedback-manipulation experiments.

## 4. Continuity-to-learning model

\[
\text{write/persist}\rightarrow\text{expose}\rightarrow\text{reinspect}\rightarrow\text{read/access}
\rightarrow\text{uptake}\rightarrow\text{decision incorporation}
\rightarrow\text{outcome feedback}\rightarrow\text{adaptation/transfer}.
\]

Intent to communicate is separate; it cannot be inferred from persistence, availability, a read or an action change. In HsH, a Common file proves persistence; recipient-side read after the write proves access, not use; post-start handoffs require a second look; reads do not prove decision incorporation; one success does not prove learning. A future event ledger could record source hash, author, namespace/key, write time, exposure, read event/time, action, decision incorporation, outcome feedback and later strategy change. States should be `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED` or `NOT_OBSERVED`. Proposal only, not a protocol amendment.
