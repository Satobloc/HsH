## 6. Discrepancy ledger

1. **Worldline versus intersection path:** assistant `γ^μ(λ)` treats a 1D helical curve as the primitive; the correction note says the Lagrangian tracks paths of hyperfoam intersections. No map connects them.
2. **Hard cap versus transient overlap:** assistant `Q≤3`/Z3 language is presented as a hard lock; the direct source questions that status and allows higher-order hypersphere overlaps, potentially transient. This does not prove stable Q>3 particles. It does show that overlap order, filament count, and topological charge must not be silently identified.
3. **24 hyperspheres versus 24-cell lattice:** the direct count of hyperspheres is not an explicit identification with the regular 24-cell polytope or a 24-dimensional multiverse. The assistant's substitution is unsupported.
4. **Particle taxonomy:** the assistant's “definitive” lepton/meson/baryon/boson assignments exceed the direct source's exploratory geometry and are not established by the missing head-geometry attachment.
5. **Zottenwelt status:** assistant output continues to use Zottenwelt as current; the later correction note marks it formally retired.
6. **0.246 rad:** assistant output treats it as a detectable phase shift; the later direct note questions that observability beyond the 14-degree cutoff. The alternative luminosity/polarization interpretation remains speculative. Do not merge `0.239` and `0.246`.
7. **UI versus unit cell:** the source associates them, but the two-hypersphere rotation/scaling construction has no explicit map to the 24-hypersphere cell.
8. **Three planes versus sphere overlaps:** both are described as candidate intersection geometries; no map or equivalence is supplied.
9. **Missing attachment and raw lineage:** the referenced “head geometry” image and raw authoring conversation/message UUIDs were not recovered. The transcript-level attribution is not a substitute for raw message provenance.
10. **Equation-packet authorship:** the embedded 4DHH Lagrangian block is present in the document packet, but the export does not establish who authored each equation. The direct correction establishes its intended target, not authorship of the embedded formulas.
