For smooth embedded submanifolds meeting transversely in a four-dimensional ambient manifold, the expected intersection dimension is:

dim(C ∩ Σ³) = dim(C) + 3 - 4 = k - 1.

The existing RavelCarrierSheetDimensionGate already formalizes the conditional arithmetic, including the one-dimensional and full-core cases. The result is not a theorem that a particular physical intersection exists or is transverse.

A finite-thickness object ΔΣ must be typed separately. If it means a nonzero-thickness neighborhood of a hypersurface inside M⁴, it is a four-dimensional band, not another three-dimensional hypersurface. Its intersection with a carrier is locally of the carrier's dimension while the carrier lies in the band. Thus W ∩ Σ³ and W ∩ ΔΣ cannot be treated as interchangeable readouts.

- If the carrier is a 1D filament, intersection with a 3D timesheet is generically 0D.
