Three alternatives must remain open:

**A. Fabric equals resolving timesheet.**  
The historical AHA wording identifies the surface of topology with the resolving surface/timesheet. In this interpretation \(F^3\equiv\Sigma_t^3\); there is no independent shell/timesheet intersection. The source does not supply the embedding or evolution map.

**B. Fabric is a separate tube-skin.**  
If \(F^3=\partial W^4\) and \(\Sigma_t^3\) is a separate section, then \(R_t^2=F^3\cap\Sigma_t^3\) is a two-dimensional rim, while \(M_t^3=W^4\cap\Sigma_t^3\) is the three-dimensional core mouth, under clean/transverse intersection.

**C. Klein surface is a two-dimensional base.**  
If a smooth compact \(K^2\hookrightarrow M^4\) is intended, its normal bundle has rank two. Given a metric and a radius \(\epsilon\) below the normal injectivity radius, the standard tubular-neighborhood construction is:

\[
W_\epsilon^4=\exp^\perp(D_\epsilon(\nu K)),\qquad
F_\epsilon^3=\partial W_\epsilon^4
             =\exp^\perp(S_\epsilon(\nu K)).
\]

Then, for a smooth timesheet \(\Sigma_t^3\) meeting these objects cleanly/transversely:

\[
M_t^3=W_\epsilon^4\cap\Sigma_t^3,\quad
R_t^2=F_\epsilon^3\cap\Sigma_t^3,\quad
C_t^1=K^2\cap\Sigma_t^3.
\]

The corresponding dimension counts are \(4+3-4=3\), \(3+3-4=2\), and \(2+3-4=1\). A normal-bundle projection \(\pi_\nu: D_\epsilon(\nu K)\to K\) gives the candidate composition
\(p_t=\pi_\nu\circ(\exp^\perp)^{-1}|_{M_t}\).

This is a **standard conditional geometric proposal**, not a recovered Nathan equation. It assumes an embedded two-surface; if the intended object is a Möbius band with boundary rather than a closed Klein bottle, additional boundary pieces must be included. The source does not establish that \(K\) is two-dimensional.

### Forced versus optional structure

- If \(K^2\) is embedded in \(M^4\), the normal rank is two. The particular radius/profile \(\epsilon\) is not fixed by topology.
- Once a finite tubular core is chosen, its boundary shell is mathematically required. A physical contact-force or constitutive law is not.
- Nonorientability is part of a Klein/Möbius-type choice; a metric normal connection and a numerical twist rate are additional structure.
- The two radii in the transcript are embedding/metric data, not invariants of abstract topology.
- A rotational expansion field is not forced by Klein topology. The explicit \(H,\Omega\) evolution formula in the transcript is assistant-generated.
- A changing slice-intersection component count is not by itself physical particle creation. The current BEDROCK requires a separate source/conservation account before making a production claim.
