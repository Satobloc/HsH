## 1. Substantive result

A single binary label, "forced" or "optional", collapses four different questions. Every proposed worldtube feature needs four independent status axes:

1. **Source authority:** Nathan Direct, historical source, standard reference, assistant reconstruction, proposal, or actual measurement.
2. **Representation necessity:** required only under a declared geometric object, embedding, topology, and regularity conditions.
3. **Observability and identifiability:** whether the feature survives the specified projection, coarse-graining, and instrument readout.
4. **Empirical necessity:** whether removing the feature degrades a predeclared held-out prediction under matched shared parameters and uncertainty.

This prevents a source commitment from being mistaken for empirical validation, a mathematical boundary from being mistaken for a physical contact law, and non-identifiability from being mistaken for absence.

## 4. Newtonian-limit morphology lock

The source proposition is that worldtube curvature is associated with inertia. The operational reconstruction is:

- Hold measured force history, mass, initial conditions, and the observation operator fixed.
- In the Newtonian/classical limit, compare gross projected morphology with and without an added internal feature.
- A feature that changes gross morphology at fixed force and mass conflicts with this source-constrained limit and must be treated as a falsification signal, not hidden by refitting.
- A feature that changes no independent observable is unidentifiable in that channel, not proven absent.
- A feature that changes a separate observable requires its own source-grounded composition map and held-out test.
- Relativistic corrections and independently measured auxiliary channels must be separated from the Newtonian gross-morphology comparison.

This is a proposed test architecture derived from a source proposition. It is not a new force law, stiffness law, or H(s)H prediction.

## 5. Feature-isolation protocol

For each candidate feature f:

1. Freeze the source-defined object, embedding, topology, map domains, projection order, instrument map, and applicable regularity conditions.
2. Define a full model with f and an ablated model without f. If removing f changes the object type or violates the source-defined construction, label the comparison NOT_ABLATABLE or REPRESENTATION_CHANGE. Do not infer empirical necessity from an invalid ablation.
3. Keep shared force/mass histories, initial conditions, nuisance parameters, calibration budget, and observation operator matched.
4. Freeze calibration and holdout at independent acquisition-block or condition level before fitting. Adjacent frames or spatial points from one acquisition are not independent replication.
5. Score gross Newtonian morphology separately from auxiliary observables such as cross-section, transport, twist, contact, or medium response.
6. Predeclare the score and falsification threshold from the full measurement covariance and model discrepancy. Do not invent a numeric threshold before those data exist.
7. Require independent review of the exact geometry plot and independent replication before calling a feature empirically required beyond the tested system and channel.

Outcome rules:

- Full model passes and ablated model fails beyond the predeclared uncertainty threshold: candidate EMPIRICALLY_REQUIRED_IN_SCOPE, subject to independent replication.
- Both pass within resolution: NOT_REQUIRED_AT_RESOLUTION for that observable and domain; this does not erase a source commitment.
- Both fail: the model comparison fails; do not rescue it with post-hoc features or retuning.
- Same readout can arise from configurations with different target feature values: UNIDENTIFIABLE under that readout.
- Missing raw data, calibration, covariance, or independent holdout: UNSCORABLE.
- Missing source-to-observable composition map: PREDICTION_NOT_FORMED.
- A source-based mechanism that cannot yet be measured remains CANDIDATE_CARRIED / NOT_TESTED, not absent or optional. In particular, the current Kelvin-vortex or demonstrably equivalent response remains an active, tentative, falsifiable candidate; geometry-only is a diagnostic ablation, not the complete candidate ledger.
