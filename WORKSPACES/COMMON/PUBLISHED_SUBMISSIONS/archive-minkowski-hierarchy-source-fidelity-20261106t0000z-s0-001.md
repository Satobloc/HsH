# Object hierarchy and Minkowski-literalism source fidelity

**Disposition:** additive archival/source-fidelity report. This is not a physical result, a derivation of the full theory, a paper-gate advancement, or a BEDROCK promotion. It does not repeat the Cruller source transcription already preserved in issues #1117, #1120, #1303, #1341, #1355, and #1403.

## Executive finding

The direct sources support a coherent hierarchy and a positive methodological role for Minkowski:

- higher-dimensional history is the top-level primitive;
- the finite-core worldtube/filament is the physical-geometric object;
- H(s)H is a parametrized hyper/superhelical representation of that object;
- RΣ is a projection/measurement bridge from history to observed 3+1D quantities;
- Whirligig/Donut are solver/search machinery, not primitive ontology;
- Universal Indicatrix is a readout/translation scaffold;
- ER-continuity, electrogravity, and interbraid are near-core hypotheses dependent on that hierarchy.

A direct 2026-09-10 message calls the method “Minkowski literalism” and describes following the Minkowski projection of known physics, using geometry to fill gaps, and then describing the geometry rather than relying on inherited labels. A later direct 2026-10-20 message says ordinary Minkowski is the exact comparison/control and the SAT version is the working representation, mostly Minkowski with specific changes.

The derivative podcast-script artifact HsH MINKOWSKI LITERALISM.txt contains a contradictory anti-Minkowski narration and a two-expanding-S3-shell model. Its embedded editorial note is attributed internally to Nathan, but the containing raw conversation does not contain a separate user message carrying that note. Later direct sources independently support the note's general stance and supersede the script's two-shell construction.

Schematic source hierarchy:

Higher-dimensional history H  
→ finite-core worldtube/filament W  
→ H(s)H representation P(W)  
→ RΣ readout/projection  
→ observed 3+1D quantities O.

This is a conceptual ordering, not yet a typed composition. The direct source names the objects and readout role, but it does not provide the embedding ι:W→H, the representation function P, the slice/intersection map, or the exact domain and codomain of RΣ. The assistant's “resolving front” wording is one possible interpretation, not a source-established definition.

Minkowski comparison:

Ordinary Minkowski geometry (exact control)  
↕ comparison map not yet specified  
SAT working representation (mostly Minkowski with explicit changes; global expanding/radial proposal)  
→ local small-patch linear Minkowski limit (working hypothesis).

The direct ++++ and cw=t conventions must be preserved as written. No conversion to w=ct, no Wick rotation, and no identification with a Lorentzian metric is justified until the coordinate/units/signature map is supplied.
