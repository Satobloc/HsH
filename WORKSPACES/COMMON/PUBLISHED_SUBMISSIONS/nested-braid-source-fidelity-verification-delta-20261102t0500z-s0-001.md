## 3. Nathan Direct geometry, with mathematics bracketed

The stable descriptive hierarchy is:

1. **Morphology:** the underlying filament/worldtube shape and its nested organization. The June transcript correction puts this first.
2. **Superhelical nesting:** nested path/curve morphology. It is explicitly not identical to braid nesting.
3. **Braid nesting:** an interweaving hierarchy. The July source proposes Q=3 at the hadronic level and helium-3 as a higher-order braid of three triple braids.
4. **Interbraid/back-tug:** proposed filament-on-filament interaction, including trans-temporal tug. This is a mechanism proposal, not a supplied force law.
5. **Persistent braid state:** a crossover that cannot be flattened without breaking holonomy. The direct transcript also asks whether whole-body rotation creates such a state for every particle pair; the threshold remains open.
6. **Holonomy:** a later quantization/closure layer, not a substitute for the morphology that it quantizes.
7. **Observable readout:** measured nuclear quantities should constrain the geometry. The source does not provide the density/projection map from braid geometry to the measured charge second moment.

The label Q=3 is not automatically the standard braid group B3. Likewise, C3 cyclic symmetry, S3 endpoint permutations, and B3 braid transport are distinct mathematical objects.
