## 7. Conditional mathematics, kept separate from source equations

A claim that two representations are equivalent needs a common state space X, representation maps R_α and R_β, and a conversion map satisfying

T_{β←α} ∘ R_α = R_β.

A staged representation must also satisfy composition coherence:

T_{γ←β} ∘ T_{β←α} = T_{γ←α}.

For an observable to factor through a projection, the source representation must be constant on the projection's fibers. Kestrel's existing hallway_factorization_iff_fiber_compatibility theorem formalizes that generic condition. ArchiveEQ0049 formalizes direct/staged projection coherence. Neither theorem supplies the physical maps.

A projection of a 1D curve remains at most 1D. If “string” means a 2D worldsheet, an extra parameter or family of worldlines is required. If it means a 1D spatial curve, that codomain and its observable must be stated. The 2025 wording does not settle which type is intended.

For a transverse intersection with a 3D hypersurface in 4D, dim(W ∩ Σ_t) = dim(W) + 3 - 4. Thus a 1D curve gives a point, a 3D worldvolume gives a 2D trace, and a 4D region can give a 3D trace. The October 2026 “3D trace” description therefore requires a 4D support or a non-transverse special case. This is a dimensional gate, not a physical calculation.

No original O1 or Blockwave equation was edited. The conditional equations above are separate tests and must not be silently inserted into either source.
