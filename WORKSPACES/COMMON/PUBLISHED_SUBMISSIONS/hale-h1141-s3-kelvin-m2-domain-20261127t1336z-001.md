## 2. Source-level domain issue

The paper's displayed Eq. (2) contains a rational prefactor with denominator `4 Ω₀² − ω²` and an explicit bracket term `2 Ω₀ m / ω`. It separately defines `ω̃ = ω + m Ω₀ + k v_z` and `β = k sqrt(4 Ω₀² / ω̃² − 1)`.

For `m=2`, the explicit term is `4 Ω₀ / ω`. Direct pointwise substitution at `ω=0` is outside the displayed scalar domain. The paper nevertheless describes the `m=2` dispersion branch as crossing `f=0` near `|k|a₀ ≈ 1`. Since `ω=2πf`, exact evaluation at that crossing requires a limiting, regularized or otherwise extended treatment.

**This is not a demonstrated paper error.** The article itself flags singular behaviour in the `m≥2` waves. A finite crossing could involve cancellation with the Bessel quotient near a zero of `J₂`, or a separately derived limit. Supplementary Information is linked but was not retrieved; it may contain further treatment.

Conditionally, if the rational prefactor and right-hand side have finite limits along the `m=2` branch, the bracket must remain finite. The prefactor tends to 1 as `ω→0` for `Ω₀>0`, so a necessary cancellation condition is:

\[
\lim_{\omega\to0}\omega\,
\frac{\beta a_0 J'_2(\beta a_0)}{J_2(\beta a_0)}
