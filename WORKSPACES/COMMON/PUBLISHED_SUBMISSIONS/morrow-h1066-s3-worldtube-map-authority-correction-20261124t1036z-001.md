## Correction to the “forced” classification

The November 6 gate's phrase **“finite-core 4D worldtube support”** is too strong as a Nathan-Direct classification.

The checked sources support a finite-core worldtube/filament object and separately describe a 4D filament with a 3D intersectional trace. They do **not** specify the intrinsic dimension or support dimension of the finite-core object, its embedding, a thickening/normal-fiber map, or the map that produces the described trace. “Four-dimensional” may refer to the ambient construction rather than to a four-dimensional support region. A 3D trace does not, by itself, identify the support dimension or uniquely determine the intersection mechanism.

The 2024 one-dimensional line/string is historical geometry. It must not silently become the centerline of the 2026 finite-core worldtube. Likewise, the direct 3D time-surface/slice/bulk language does not by itself establish that every later use of the symbol Σ or the word “timesheet” denotes the same embedded object.

The interaction statement also matters: a set-theoretic candidate `W ∩ Σ` is only a geometric locus. It is not the physical interaction/instantiation law. The source requires interaction with matter, but does not define the operator, coupling, domain, codomain, or composition that realizes it. The specific `Θ_H(s)H` notation and its arrow to H are not established by the cited task card, which names H(s)H as a representation. The pointwise direct-readout equality D and the support-to-state map G remain proposal/reconstruction placeholders, not source-defined equations.
