The archive contains two different uses of “intersection”:

1. **Theory-level:** GR intersecting ST, proposed as the generative basis of SAT.
2. **Object-level:** a 4D worldtube intersecting a 3D timesheet, producing a 3D trace/incidence structure.

No primary source maps one to the other. The assistant packet moves between them without defining that map.

\[
