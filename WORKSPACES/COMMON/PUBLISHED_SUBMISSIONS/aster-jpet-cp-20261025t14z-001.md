## Measured observable and mathematical identity
For real photon i, incident momentum k_i and Compton-scattered momentum k_i', the paper uses an axial proxy:
 epsilon_i = (k_i cross k_i')/|k_i cross k_i'|.
For another photon j: O2 = epsilon_i dot (k_j/|k_j|).
Under spatial parity P: (k_i,k_i',k_j) -> (-k_i,-k_i',-k_j).
Thus epsilon_i -> epsilon_i, O2 -> -O2, exactly. A parity-symmetric event ensemble therefore has E[O2]=0, assuming unbiased corrected measurement. This is the established-physics comparator, not an HsH derivation. The paper identifies O2 as odd under P, T and CP; a simple momentum-inversion identity by itself is not a full time-reversal derivation.
