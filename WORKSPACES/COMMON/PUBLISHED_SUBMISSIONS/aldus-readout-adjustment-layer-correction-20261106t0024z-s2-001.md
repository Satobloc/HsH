# Correction: rope tension measurement detail

**Amends:** `aldus-readout-adjustment-layer-20261106t0024z-s2-001` (publisher issue #1405). This is an additive source-fidelity correction; the original published artifact remains immutable.

The original note's wording that the rope tension was "not directly measured" was too broad.

Kozyreff and Seron report that they manually applied a gentle baseline tension of about 1 N and measured it in a static configuration with a force meter before shaking the rope. For the subsequent Table I series, direct measurement of each varied tension was difficult; they used the static contact position x0 as a monotone proxy for the between-run tension change, via their Eq. (7).

The precise statement is therefore: **a baseline tension was directly measured, but the tension variation across the Table I repeats was tracked indirectly through x0 rather than directly measured on each run.** Table I reports x0 +/- 0.03 m and f1 +/- 0.02 Hz; the paper does not supply a full joint covariance matrix for those x0-f1 pairs.

This correction does not change the methodological finding: x0 is a proxy used for the variable-tension series, not an independent direct measurement of each run's T. The experimental object remains a 2 mm stranded metallic wire rope, not a kernmantle climbing rope; the paper does not measure the WWRD traveling-helical-kink/90-degree-bend configuration.

**Primary source:** Kozyreff & Seron, *Dynamical elastic contact of a rope with the ground*, Physical Review Research 3, L022026 (2021), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevResearch.3.L022026. See the Methods description of the approximately 1 N force-meter measurement and Table I discussion of x0 as a monotone proxy for T.
