### Proposed conservative overlay

This is a gate for **empirical model selection or claims of distinctive physical prediction**, not a ban on exploratory sandbox development.

**A. Source and representation admissibility**

1. Preserve exact source identity, authorship, context, notation, and status.
2. Require explicitly typed projection, observation, and composition maps for the declared object and target observable.
3. Do not infer a shape-nesting relation from analogy. State domains, codomains, conditions, and composition laws.

**B. Measured-system and comparator readiness**

1. Identify an actual measured system and versioned dataset, with provenance, acquisition identifiers, units, calibration, uncertainty, and covariance.
2. Use a matched standard-physics comparator under the same measurement operator and declared acquisition scope.
3. Separate calibration from an independently controlled holdout.
4. Freeze shared parameters before holdout access; expose any channel-specific retuning and count it as flexibility.

**C. Identifiability and held-out adequacy**

1. Test whether the target readout is constant on the fibers of the proposed coarse-graining/projection.
2. Test whether the candidate response is absorbable by the standard nuisance tangent space.
3. Require a predeclared held-out score and falsification threshold derived from the actual data and uncertainty model.
4. If data, maps, or independence evidence are missing, classify the result as UNRESOLVED / NOT TESTED, not as confirmation or absence.

**D. Non-scalar complexity ledger**

Record a componentwise footprint rather than an arbitrary weighted sum:

- added primitives/state sectors;
- unconstrained functions or constitutive laws;
- free parameters;
- unconstrained boundary/initial conditions;
- channel-specific retunings;
- model-discrepancy degrees of freedom;
- external priors required to close the model.

Do not assign an invented exchange rate between these dimensions. Use componentwise comparison/Pareto dominance and explain any dimension that cannot be ordered.

**E. Intelligibility preference**

Only after the evidence gates pass, and only among candidates empirically equivalent at the declared resolution/uncertainty, may human geometric/causal intelligibility act as a tie-break. The preferred candidate must not be more flexible componentwise solely because it is easier to picture. Record the preference qualitatively; do not turn it into an unsupported numerical score.

This is a conservative implementation, not a complete normative theory of model choice. If candidates have distinguishable held-out performance, report that empirical difference rather than letting intelligibility override it. If no measured system exists, intelligibility may guide sandbox exploration but cannot establish empirical selection or physical necessity.

## 3. Forced, optional, preference-only, and unresolved geometry

Keep these statuses separate:

- **SOURCE-REQUIRED:** the recovered source explicitly commits to the feature.
- **REPRESENTATION-REQUIRED:** the declared object/map cannot be defined under its stated conditions without the feature.
- **OBSERVATION-REQUIRED:** the target readout does not descend through the reduced projection, or the feature produces an identifiable direction outside the declared nuisance span.
- **EMPIRICALLY-REQUIRED:** a preregistered, matched, independent holdout distinguishes the extended model from the reduced/standard comparator under the same measurement and uncertainty controls.
- **PREFERENCE-ONLY:** the feature improves human geometric intelligibility but has no established distinguishing observable.
- **UNRESOLVED / NOT TESTED:** source maps, measurements, or independence evidence are insufficient.

A feature not yet forced is **not automatically optional**. Optionality is relative to a declared model class, observable set, scale, and uncertainty. Missing maps or measurements require UNRESOLVED.
