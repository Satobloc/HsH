## 4. Nathan Direct descriptive structure

The most precise source-faithful structure is a **layered phenomenological classification with cross-cutting relations**, not a spatial manifold and not a simple tree.

### Glossary and hierarchy

- **Perceptual instance:** a particular experienced item or occurrence, such as a specific experience of yellowness.
- **Modality:** a class/type of experience within a field, such as sight within sensory input.
- **Perceptual field:** a broader class of experience, such as sensory input, feelings, emotions, or thought.
- **Nexus:** a higher-order grouping or connective organization among fields. Two direct examples are the Feelings-Sensory Nexus and Thought-Emotion Nexus.
- **Dom:** a tentative cross-field correspondence associated with a particular experience. It may connect instances in multiple fields, or be a singleton “Dom of One.”
- **Domdom:** a meta-Dom over Doms.
- **Taxonomy:** the whole personal organization of fields and their relationships.

The user's own qualification matters: the organization has hierarchical aspects, but is not asserted to be hierarchical through and through. Doms cross-cut field membership; they are not one item per field.

A yellow sensory instance, a remembered yellow object, and a thought about yellow are plausible examples of corresponding instances. The exact correspondence relation is not defined in the source.
