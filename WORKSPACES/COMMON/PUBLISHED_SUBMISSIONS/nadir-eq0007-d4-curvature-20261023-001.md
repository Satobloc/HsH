tensor Laplacian. On a flat background the corrected expression is

\[

\partial^\rho\partial_\mu h_{\nu\rho}+
\partial^\rho\partial_\nu h_{\mu\rho}-
\Box h_{\mu\nu}-\partial_\mu\partial_\nu h

Thus the printed final term must be `-1/2 Box S_mu_nu`, not
`-1/2 g_mu_nu Box trace(S)`. Curved-background commutator/curvature terms
