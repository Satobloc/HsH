## 5. Mathematical reconstruction and proof status

A useful abstract representation is:

\[
G_K=(V_K,E_K)
\]

for the knowledge/capability graph, and

\[
G_C=(V_C,E_C)
\]

for the workflow-control graph.

A coupling would be a partial map such as

\[
\Phi:
(V_K\times P_{\mathrm{out}}\times S_{\mathrm{source}})
\rightharpoonup
(V_C\times O_{\mathrm{proposal}}),
\]

where the domain records a module, one of its output ports and a pinned source state, and the codomain records the corresponding control-plane operation proposal.

Composition between modules would additionally require typed port compatibility or an explicit adapter map. The direct source does not specify the port types, schemas, adapters, identity/version semantics, authority rules, failure behavior or transition relation needed to instantiate this map. This notation is an assistant reconstruction, not a verbatim Nathan equation.
