## 8. Conditional mathematics and Lean evidence

- 1D worldline with a 3D hypersurface in 4D: 0D transverse intersection.
- 2D worldsheet with a 3D hypersurface in 4D: 1D transverse intersection.
- 3D boundary with a 3D hypersurface in 4D: 2D transverse intersection.
- 4D full-dimensional support with a 3D hypersurface in 4D: 3D trace.

This is conditional geometry. It does not assert that a physical H(s)H filament is 1D, 2D, or full-dimensional. It makes the source ambiguity explicit: a generic 3D trace cannot be obtained from a generic 1D centerline intersecting a 3D timesheet.
