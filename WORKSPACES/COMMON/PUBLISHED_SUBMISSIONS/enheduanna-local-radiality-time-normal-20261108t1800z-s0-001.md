## Finding

The 9 October 2026 local-radiality/time-normal discussion is Nathan Direct material. It proposes that mass deflects or retards outward time-sheet propagation, with a locally radial normal/propagation direction and a possible inward or past-directed interpretation. The assistant translated this into a standard Lorentzian Painleve-Gullstrand (PG) model.

PG is a useful standard-GR control, but not a complete SAT/H(s)H realization. It encodes radial shift/tilt with unit lapse. It does not, by itself, encode the direct claim that mass slows the time-sheet propagation rate. Its statement that inward radial coordinate motion is not time reversal is valid inside that PG coordinate model, but does not settle the distinct SAT interpretation.

The 21 October direct source later says the reciprocal filament/timesheet-deformation model is no longer current and Klein Fabric had become common. The 9 October radial-time construction must remain a historical branch. The later Klein Fabric source gives a conceptual global construction, but its formal topology and global-to-local maps remain unresolved. The relation between Klein Fabric and the later Forest normalization is not established by the messages recovered here.

## 3. PG translation and its limit

The assistant used:
ds^2 = -dw^2 + (dr + b(r) dw)^2 + r^2 dOmega^2, with b(r)=sqrt(r_s/r).
n = partial_w - b partial_r; T = partial_w = n + b partial_r.
ADM form: T = N n + beta, with N=1 and beta=b partial_r.
Radial null-coordinate speeds: dr/dw = -b plus-or-minus 1.

Inside the Schwarzschild horizon both future-directed radial null families can have decreasing r while w increases. This is valid within the PG representation.

The source-fidelity limit is N=1. This PG form models radial shift/tilt, not a reduced proper-time lapse. The direct claim that mass slows the time-sheet needs a rate/lapse field or another explicit propagation-rate map. None is supplied. The assistant's boost n' = cosh(chi)n - sinh(chi)e_r is another reconstruction; no source-defined chi(r), mass law, or map from the current ++++ ambient geometry to this Lorentzian frame is given.

The direct 300d message resists making the PG statement universal across SAT's distinct ontology. The 21 October supersession means PG remains a historical GR control, not a current SAT equation.

## 4. Local radiality versus global normal

A radial field is defined relative to a chosen center or congruence. The PG example is local to one spherically symmetric mass; it does not supply a universal radial field for a multi-body universe.

A global normal requires a globally co-orientable hypersurface and compatible local normal choices. A global radial field additionally needs a center/congruence and a patching rule across bodies. If the eventual Klein Fabric gluing is non-coorientable, a global normal section may fail; the word Klein alone does not establish non-coorientability.

The 21 October cw=t, ++++, Forest convention must not be silently converted into the earlier w=ct, Lorentzian PG convention. A positive-definite ++++ metric has no nonzero null vectors. A Lorentzian null cone can be used only after the induced Lorentzian metric and its map from the native ambient construction are specified.
