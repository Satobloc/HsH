## 3. Stable typed glossary
- **Fundamental filament/worldtube:** one extended constituent with its own finite core, framing and history. Count Q_fund at the declared constituent level.
- **First-order triplet:** a proposed nucleon made of three constituent filaments; a geometric braid is NOT automatically a Borromean link.
- **Second-order braid:** three first-order bundles transported around one another; its effective three strands are bundles, while its constituent count is nine.
- **Braid:** a multi-strand embedding/isotopy object with a common monotone parameter and endpoint/closure conventions; not synonymous with a single helical curve.
- **Helix/superhelix:** a parametrized geometric curve; winding, phase and closure need explicit conditions.
- **Borromean/Brunnian link:** a closed, disjoint three-component link with all pairwise linking numbers zero but nontrivial collective topology; this does not follow merely from having three strands.
- **Pairwise link density ρ_link:** historical scalar assembled from pairwise linking information after a valid three-dimensional closure/slice and volume normalization.
- **Higher-order link data:** e.g. a suitable Milnor triple-link invariant in its defined category; cannot be recovered from pairwise linking numbers alone.
- **Timesheet readout:** intersection/projection of the four-dimensional construction onto a declared three-dimensional resolving surface, not a generic identity map.
- **Constitutive interaction:** energy/response law needed to turn topology/geometry into a force, lifetime or observable; distinct from a braid label.
