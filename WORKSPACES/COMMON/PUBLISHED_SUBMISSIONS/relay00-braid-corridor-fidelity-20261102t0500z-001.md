# Braid smoothing, coarse-graining, and mathematical corridors
## Cross-era source-fidelity addendum

**Date:** 2026-11-02  
**Status:** additive source correction; not a new physical theory, not a CODATA assessment, and not a substitute for the existing Q-count, smoothing-bound, or projection-gate work.

## Executive finding

The recovered primary messages distinguish three different operations:

1. **Braid smoothing:** a proposed change in the active geometric surface of a tightly braided filament configuration.
2. **Coarse-graining:** a reduction in descriptive resolution, to be checked against a fuller calculation rather than used to erase the detail needed for that calculation.
3. **Back-hallway translation:** a metaphorical mathematical corridor between descriptions or theory-specific observable spaces, not a physical tunnel or worldtube.

A fourth item, the **Derivation Loop / Star-Shaped Derivation Map**, is the research protocol that tests whether a relationship survives independent cross-domain transformations. It is not a physical object. The direct source calls it the conceptual continuation of the Back-Hallways programme while explicitly warning that the older Back-Hallways formalism is not fresh SAT.

These operations may be connected by typed maps, but the archive does not define one common map that makes them equivalent.

## 4. Mathematical correction to the historical corridor formula

The assistant-authored inverse expression `π_j ∘ π_i⁻¹` silently assumes an inverse that may not exist. If `π_i` is many-to-one, a pseudoinverse or chosen representative does not by itself make the translation well-defined.

The appropriate conditional map is:

[
H_{ij}: operatorname{im}(pi_i)	ooperatorname{im}(pi_j),
qquad
H_{ij}(pi_i(p))=pi_j(p).
]

It is well-defined exactly when the target readout is constant on every fiber of the source projection:

[
pi_i(p)=pi_i(q) Longrightarrow pi_j(p)=pi_j(q).
]

When the maps exist on the corresponding image ranges, composition is explicit:

[
H_{ik}=H_{jk}circ H_{ij}.
]

This is a generic set-theoretic reconstruction of “mathematical corridors,” not a Nathan Direct equation and not an instantiated SAT physical map. The word “functorial” in the historical text is not justified until categories, objects, and morphisms are defined.
