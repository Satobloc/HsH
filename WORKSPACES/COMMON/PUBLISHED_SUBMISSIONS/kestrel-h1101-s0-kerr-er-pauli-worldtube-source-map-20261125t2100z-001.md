## 4. Geometry and dimensional typing

A 1D embedded core \(\gamma\) thickened by a rank-\((n-1)\) normal bundle in an \(n\)-dimensional ambient manifold gives an \(n\)-dimensional support. Thus, conditionally:

- In a 3+1 spacetime, a 1D worldline thickened by three normal directions can generate a 4D worldtube with a 3D boundary.
- In a four-spatial-dimensional ambient manifold with proper time kept separate, a 4D spatial support swept through proper time has a 5D history object.

These are different type assignments. The later four-spatial-dimensional Crullerverse work, discussed in the [Ravel Kerr ambient-type report #2551](https://github.com/Satobloc/HsH/issues/2551), must not silently be substituted for the earlier 3+1 worldline/timesheet grammar. The live BEDROCK's native `++++` calculation convention and separately induced Lorentzian metrics likewise require an explicit representation map before being identified with a Kerr spacetime metric.

The Kerr ring singularity, Kerr event horizon, ER throat/mouth, and finite-support worldtube boundary are distinct geometric objects. A stationary spatial ring has a different dimensional history from a finite 3D body swept through time; an event horizon is a causal boundary, not automatically the boundary of a finite material or geometric core.
