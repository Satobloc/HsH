## 2. Why the additional statistical family is needed

The existing draft [PR #2330](https://github.com/Satobloc/HsH/pull/2330) already carries a frozen model-candidate family and a precommitted calibration/holdout plan. It does not itself establish that the separate family of statistical tests, endpoints, target windows, or source-derived hypotheses has been uniquely identified and locked before external-corpus inspection.

These are different families:

- **Model-candidate family:** which competing model specifications are admitted.
- **Statistical-test family:** which distinct, source-authorized hypotheses/endpoints will be tested, with what targets, tolerances, transformations, and multiplicity budget.

The distinction matters for the active source-compendium work. Current main's `PUBLIC_SITE/CURRENT_WORK.json` reports 24 equation occurrences across three source documents and a ten-message Nathan Direct projection-angle compilation. The separate seed branch `calder-sat-equation-compendium-seed-20261127` reports a private machine seed with 69 equation occurrences, 15 source documents, and six sector mappings; integrity validation remains in progress. That branch is four commits ahead and 102 behind current main, has no PR, and was not modified.

**Neither 24 nor 69 is a statistical test-family size.** Raw equation occurrences, repeated source mentions, bins, pixels, snapshots, and repeated measurements do not become independent hypotheses by being counted. The family size \(K\) must be the number of unique, source-authorized hypothesis keys after a documented deduplication rule. The quarantined B/Φ/harmonic interpretation from the mixed 2026-09-12 Calder thread was not reused or unquarantined.

## 3. New formal contract

Added `FrozenStatisticalTestFamily`, separate from the existing model-candidate family. It carries:

- A finite set of unique hypothesis keys.
- A source-manifest locator and declared manifest digest.
- A named timebase.
- A lock tick and first external-corpus-access tick.
- Nonemptiness and a declared lock-before-access relation.

The manifest is where exact target values, tolerances, permitted transforms, and the deduplication rule must be recorded. The Lean structure carries a locator and declared digest; it does not prove that the manifest contains the encoded keys or authenticate the manifest bytes or access log.

For each unique hypothesis key \(i\), let \(E_i\) be its rejection event on a common outcome space. The formal result is:

\[
\left(\forall i,\ \mu(E_i)\leq\delta\right)
\land
\left(K\delta\leq\alpha\right)
\Longrightarrow
\mu\!\left(\bigcup_{i=1}^{K}E_i\right)\leq\alpha.
\]

This follows from finite-union subadditivity for an outer measure. When the outer measure is the probability outer measure, it gives the familywise probability bound. It requires **no independence or disjointness assumption between tests**. Equal allocation \(\delta=\alpha/K\) is the familiar Bonferroni choice when \(K>0\), but the formal theorem is written in the more general total-budget form.

A second theorem composes the statistical-family lock with the existing `PrecommittedHoldoutPlan`: the two records must use the same declared timebase, and first external-corpus access must precede or equal holdout access.

### What the proof does not establish

- It does not prove that any individual p-value or rejection rule is valid.
- It does not prove exchangeability or validity of a permutation/randomization scheme.
- It does not authenticate the source-manifest digest or timestamp.
- It does not establish independence of acquisition units.
- It does not validate a physical target, measurement map, or H(s)H prediction.

Those remain separate statistical, source, and empirical obligations.
