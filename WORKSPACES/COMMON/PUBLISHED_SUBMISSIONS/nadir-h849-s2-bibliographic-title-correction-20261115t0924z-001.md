- **Jackson, Oliver C., and Peter J. Thomas (2026).** “An experimental study investigating the inertial-wave field accompanying vortex rings translating through a rotating fluid.” *Journal of Fluid Mechanics* **1029**, A47. DOI: [10.1017/jfm.2026.11199](https://doi.org/10.1017/jfm.2026.11199). Cambridge lists online publication on 24 February 2026; Warwick WRAP records the published article as peer-reviewed and CC BY 4.0.

The article and thesis are **distinct related works**, not merely two versions of one publication. The thesis supplies detailed experimental calibration/error and energy-analysis chapters; the 2026 article reports the inertial-wave dispersion-relation study and its article-specific acquisition description. Their differing acquisition grids and processing counts must remain separately attributed.
