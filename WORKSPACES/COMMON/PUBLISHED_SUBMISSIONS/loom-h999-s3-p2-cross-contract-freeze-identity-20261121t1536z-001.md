## 3. The composition rule

The rule is **one comparison bundle, one exact cross-contract key**.

- CBCM remains the model-layer composition contract. MFFG is its candidate-family/prediction-envelope subrecord, not a second independent gate.
- TPRC binds source bundle, geometry map, view, observable family, and readout identity. It does not prove that a physical readout exists or is correct.
- H930 supplies the observable family and selection rule.
- CPPG and the dimensionally typed readout bind the coupled readout to the same readout contract. They do not establish physical coupling.
- H978/H987 bind resolution and heterogeneous error-transfer assumptions to the same readout and uncertainty model.
- H974/PDR bind acquisition units, holdout partition, and exposure history to the same candidate family and prediction envelope. Their current publication states remain limited as shown above.
- H992 proposes binding plot witnesses to exact H0/H1 model freezes and a source snapshot. This adapter additionally binds both plot witnesses to the same geometry-map digest. A plot is a comparison witness, not independent physical measurement.
- The existing comparison freeze continues to supply shared calibration, covariance, comparator, shared-parameter manifest, analysis plan, decision rule, holdout partition, and observable-operator identities.

The key includes the existing ComparisonFreeze and SourceBoundP2Bundle as type parameters, then records identities for source commitment, geometry map, readout, observable family, candidate family, prediction envelope, coupled readout, resolution, acquisition, holdout exposure, and both geometry-plot witnesses. Explicit equality fields bind the dependencies: candidate family to source-map and geometry-map digests; readout to source bundle and geometry map; observable/coupled/resolution contracts to the same readout; holdout to the same partition, candidate family, and prediction envelope; H0/H1 plot witnesses to their exact model freezes, source bundle, and geometry map.

The PDR is the outer comparison record. Its own digest is not placed inside its key, avoiding a self-referential digest. The key is carried by the record; it does not hash itself.

## 5. Falsification-workflow consequence

A source correction now has an explicit downstream consequence:

1. Correct source-origin classification and rebuild the SourceCommitmentMap.
2. Its identity/digest changes, so the cross-contract key changes.
3. Candidate-family and prediction-envelope manifests must be rebound to the corrected source map and geometry map.
4. Holdout-exposure ledger must bind the new family and prediction envelope.
5. If the old holdout was accessed while developing the old family, it is not automatically independent for the revised family. A fresh holdout is required unless a predeclared adaptive-inference guarantee explicitly covers the revision.
6. H0/H1 plot witnesses must bind to the exact model freezes and source snapshot used by the revised comparison.

This distinguishes a corrected citation, a changed model, and a still-independent prediction. The key is an identity and invalidation mechanism, not a claim that the model is correct.
