## Three logically distinct cases
A. **Strict same-slab locality**: a newly generated disturbance propagating no faster than c, with no carrier or coupling across active slabs of duration tau, reaches at most R<=c tau. At R=1 m this requires tau>=3.335640952 ns. This is a conditional no-go for arbitrary-range same-slab propagation, not a prediction of the actual sheet thickness.
B. **Retarded vacuum Maxwell response**: an eternal stationary charge yields Coulomb E=q rhat/(4pi eps0 r^2), while changes propagate with retarded time t-r/c. No dissipative persistent material medium is necessary to express this Green-function solution; a viable wavefront interpretation must nevertheless account for the entire retarded causal path and source history, not erase it between slabs.
C. **Persistent-response scalar comparator**: (c^-2 partial_t^2 + 2 gamma c^-2 partial_t - nabla^2) phi=rho/eps0. Both gamma=0 and gamma>0 have the static 1/r Green function. Harmonic transfer is exp(i k r)/r with k=sqrt((omega^2+2i gamma omega))/c, Im(k)>=0. For gamma=1e6/s and f=1 MHz, Im(k)=0.003295162592/m; at 100 m the damped-to-undamped amplitude is 0.719271590269. This scalar comparator is NOT a Maxwell theory; nonzero gamma requires independent electromagnetic consistency tests.

## Charge-conserving dynamic test
In units c=1 and 4pi eps0=1, use localized dipole source rho=-p(t) dot grad delta^3(x), J=dot p(t) delta^3(x). The continuity equation holds distributionally. Let p(t)=0.3 cos(0.7t) zhat, t_r=t-r, n=x/r. Exact source-free fields outside the origin:

E=[3n(n dot p)-p]/r^3+[3n(n dot pdot)-pdot]/r^2+[n cross (n cross pddot)]/r;
B=(pdot cross n)/r^2+(pddot cross n)/r,
all dipole moments evaluated at t_r.

Centered-difference Maxwell residuals at x=(2,1,0.5), t=5: h=0.1 gives div E=1.927387250e-4, |curl E+partial_t B|=1.872644881e-4, |curl B-partial_t E|=1.826048600e-5. At h=0.05 the respective values are 4.825965992e-5, 4.675630931e-5, 4.575430002e-6, consistent with second-order convergence. The source-free identities hold analytically for r>0; finite differences are only a numerical check.

## Discriminator
Static Coulomb scaling alone cannot distinguish undamped retarded response from a damped constitutive model. A finite-speed time-varying conserved source, transverse response, and attenuation/dispersion are necessary. A same-slab bound applies only if the model really forbids any cross-slab causal propagation; it cannot be inferred from the word 'wavefront' alone.
