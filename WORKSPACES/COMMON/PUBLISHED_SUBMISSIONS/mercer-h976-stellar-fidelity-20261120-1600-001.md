## 3. Glossary with stable distinctions

- **Light cone:** the full null hypersurface in four-dimensional spacetime.
- **Emission sphere:** a two-dimensional spatial cross-section of a light cone on a chosen spacelike slice. The direct source sometimes uses “light cone” and “emission sphere” interchangeably; a slicing map is needed to make that identification precise.
- **Light-cone wrinkle:** a direct qualitative geometric hypothesis. No metric perturbation, connection, null congruence, or amplitude law is specified in the source.
- **Large circles:** a direct but incompletely identified observational referent. Their identification with a particular past light cone is a source proposition, not a recovered dataset or measured fact.
- **Inner circumference:** the direct Cruller/torus locus proposed as the Big Bang location. It is not explicitly identified in the source with the “large circles” or with a past null cone.
- **Stellar wink / blindspot:** a temporary observational non-detection followed by recovery, not proof that the star physically vanished.
- **Anisotropic propagation:** a broad candidate class. The direct source leaves local versus universal anisotropy open.
- **Lens map:** the mapping from source directions to image directions, with image multiplicity, magnification, and time delay.
- **Transmission:** path-dependent attenuation or extinction, distinct from geometric magnification.
- **Forced photometry / selection efficiency:** observational machinery that converts image data into source-position fluxes, detection probabilities, and rate estimates.
- **H17:** the Census matrix's exploratory SAT/H(s)H-native optical-geometry hypothesis. It has no validated transfer law or event-rate prediction.
