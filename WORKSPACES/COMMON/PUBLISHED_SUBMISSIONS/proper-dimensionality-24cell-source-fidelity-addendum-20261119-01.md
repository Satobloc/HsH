The mixed source's assistant-voiced passage at line 889 says the “24-cell HSUCV lattice functions as a discrete 3-manifold generated by its 1-skeleton.” This conflates the polytope boundary with its graph.

The standard 24-cell has 24 vertices, 96 edges, 96 triangular two-faces and 24 octahedral three-cells. The polytope is four-dimensional. Its boundary is a three-dimensional cell complex homeomorphic to S3. Its 1-skeleton is a graph with 24 vertices and 96 edges. The graph is not itself a three-manifold. The vertices can be represented by the 24 Hurwitz units, but the vertex set, adjacency graph and full boundary cell complex are different objects.

The charitable reconstruction is that the intended “discrete 3-manifold” is the entire boundary cell complex, not the 1-skeleton. That repair requires the two-cell and three-cell incidence data. It is a proposed clarification, not an accepted rewrite.
