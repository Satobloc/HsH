## 6. Geometry crosswalk: where could “active surface” live?

The direct March 13 statement refers to a braid having less active surface available to interact with the time surface. The later direct November 8 geometry supplies a possible support hierarchy, but the two sources do not explicitly identify the active surface with a particular boundary intersection.

In a four-dimensional ambient domain, assuming smooth transverse intersections:

| Support | Dimension | Intersection with Σ³ | Readout type |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Core Γ¹ | 1 | 0 | Point/event |
| Optional worldsheet S² | 2 | 1 | Curve |
| Tube boundary B³ | 3 | 2 | Surface |
| Tube region W⁴ | 4 | 3 | Region |

The dimensionally coherent candidate for a time-surface contact patch is therefore:

~~~text
A_t² := B³ intersect Σ³
A_active := a chosen 2D measure of A_t²
~~~

This is conditional on B³ being an actual smooth embedded boundary, Σ³ being a smooth embedded timesheet, and the intersection being transverse with finite measure. It is **not** a recovered Nathan Direct equation.

A possible typed construction is:

~~~text
{Gamma_i¹, braid data, Q_const, o_coil}
        |
        | finite-thickness / framing map (missing)
        v
W⁴  -- boundary operator -->  B³
                                  |
                                  | transverse intersection with Sigma³
                                  v
                              A_t²

                                  v
                      (A_active, theta4|A_t, braid data)
                                  |

                                  v
                           emergent mass
~~~

The missing maps are substantive:
1. A thickening/framing map from one-dimensional braided centerlines to a finite-thickness four-dimensional tube.
2. A boundary map defining B³.
3. A specified family of timesheets and transversality conditions.
4. A measure on the two-dimensional intersection.
5. A map carrying the March 2026 theta4 definition onto that intersection, if that is intended.
6. A readout functional connecting the resulting geometry to mass.
7. A scale/coarse-graining map from constituent filaments to any effective hadron worldtube representation.

The direct source does not say whether “active surface” means the tube's entire boundary, its intersection with the timesheet, a projected envelope, or another geometric object. B³ ∩ Σ³ is the best current dimensional candidate, not a settled identification.

The direct “same nucleon count, roughly same mass” hypothesis also remains in tension with any model in which different equal-count braid morphologies produce materially different mass readouts. That tension must be resolved by a source-defined readout map, not by selecting an assistant-generated smoothing factor.
