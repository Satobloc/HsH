# Observation–geometry–mathematics fidelity gate
**Status:** provisional methodological reference; not a physical claim, theory promotion, or BEDROCK amendment.  
**Source window:** primary user-authored working discussion, 2026-11-04 to 2026-11-05. The raw transcript is not redistributed in this artifact.

## 1. Three epistemic layers

Keep these distinct:

1. **Observation** — empirical contact with phenomena and recorded relationships.
2. **Geometric representation** — an explicit construction of the observed structure: objects, dimensions, embeddings, boundaries, intersections, orientations, couplings, and permitted transformations.
3. **Mathematical abstraction** — typed variables, maps, constraints, equations, units, and uncertainty relations that represent the geometry.

The chain is:

`Observation O → Geometric representation G → Mathematical abstraction M`

Neither arrow is an identity. Each requires its own fidelity assessment. A correct proof of M does not establish that G faithfully represents O, nor that the translation from G to M preserved the intended structure.

The source uses the terms *isometric* (geometry) and *metrological* (mathematics) precision. A useful assistant-origin gloss is: isometric fidelity checks shape, distance, orientation, incidence, and transformation; metrological fidelity checks quantities, units, calibration, and uncertainty. These glosses are aids, not independent source equations.

## 2. Coupling and mathematical decomposition

A coupling is a coincidence of structures at the same point or a separately defined geometrical relationship between them. Separate mathematical variables may be useful, but separation in notation does not establish physical independence.

Any decomposition must preserve the relation that makes the original configuration admissible. A constraint such as `Φ(A,B)=0` is one possible encoding, not a canonical law or a source equation. Independent variation is permitted only when the geometry and evidence permit it.

A practical hostile-review question is: **Can the mathematical model produce a configuration that the specified geometry cannot realize?** If so, identify the missing constraint or state that the mathematical space is deliberately broader.

## 3. Do not conflate two different triads

The earlier geometric-view triad (reduced Minkowski diagram, full 4D bulk, and a 4D view with a 3D slice) is not the same classification as the epistemic triad (observation, geometry, mathematics). Their connecting projection and composition maps remain unspecified.

Likewise, the candidate dynamic-block, worldline-only, and mutually distributed descriptions of worldtube/timesheet response remain alternative representations. Mutual worldtube–timesheet curvature is the current working hypothesis; equivalence to one-sided descriptions has not been proved. The timesheet/timefront and the propagation direction are distinct. Whether the responsive medium is a substitute for, addition to, or equivalent description of the timesheet remains open.

The labels “Reality-Approximation Principle,” the constraint form `Φ(A,B)=0`, and a three-level coupling taxonomy were introduced by the assistant as proposed summaries. They are not verbatim Nathan equations or settled theory terms.
