# Early SAT filament/string relation: source-fidelity audit

**Status:** bounded source-fidelity finding; no theory update, equation correction, or physical validation.

## Executive finding

The April 3, 2025 direct statement is deliberately tentative: filaments are described as an “orthogonal (maybe this isn't the right word here?)” extension of strings over the fourth dimension, “perhaps more as spacelike than as timelike.” The user later narrows the physical claim: the cross-temporal extent of filaments and its possible mechanical energy-transfer capacity may be physical; the rest of SAT's four-dimensional picture could be visualization, and time is not simply woven into the filament structure.

That distinction is lost in the assistant's April 3 expansion. It turns the tentative wording into a much stronger package: a 3D time surface expanding from the Big Bang, filaments static in 4D space, time as a slicing operation, non-directional or reversible time, and possible emergent causality and entanglement-as-contiguity. Some of this language echoes an attached `SAT_overview.txt` summary, but it is not established by the direct user message.

The same conversation records an explicit request for humility: “physics poetry” was meant to acknowledge incomplete formalization, not to make the theory flowery. The user then preferred “a regeometrization proposal,” not “the” thesis, and asked to avoid “ontology” and grandiose claims about reformatting all physics. This audit therefore treats the early geometry as a proposal with deliberately limited physical commitments.

## 6. Discrepancy ledger

1. **Tentative relation → assertive interpretation.** The direct April 3 phrase is hedged; the assistant converts it into a strong temporal ontology and several unearned consequences.
2. **Physical claim narrowed.** The April 5 direct message limits the physical core to cross-temporal filament extent and possible mechanical energy transfer; the rest may be visualization.
3. **Time surface not passive by default.** The direct source allows filament interactions to influence the spacetime surface's geometry. “Time is just a slicing operation” is not a direct proposition.
4. **Worldline/worldsheet/worldtube conflation.** SAT-O1's `γ¹`, Blockwave's `J²` worldsheet support, and H(s)H's `W⁴` finite-core support are different types.
5. **Missing worldtubification.** SAT-O1 names a 4D volume but supplies only a 1-parameter curve/action; no thickening or sweep map is given.
6. **Missing current-degree map.** Blockwave's rank-two current and a 1D worldline current require different support degrees in 4D; no `J² ↔ J³` map is supplied.
7. **Angle/orthogonality undefined.** The early phrase does not define `θ₄` or a Lorentz-invariant angle. Current BEDROCK requires local directions, metric, signature, observer, and the chosen timesheet/medium representation.
8. **Secondary summaries are not primary evidence.** `SAT_overview.txt`, NotebookLM-labelled verbal summaries, and later dictionary/prime-directive documents cannot silently replace the direct user messages.
