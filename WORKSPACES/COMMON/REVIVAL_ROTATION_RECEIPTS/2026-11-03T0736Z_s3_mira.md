## Substantive architecture correction: identifiability is not a two-channel count

The previous :36 report published as issue #1301 says Gate C requires **at least two** separately specified measurement channels. That is stronger than the cited Lean theorem and is not a general identifiability requirement.

Corrected structural criterion:

1. Let `Theta_adm` be the declared admissible geometry/parameter domain.
2. Define an explicit equivalence relation `~` for descriptions that represent the same physical geometry under permitted gauge/reparameterization freedoms. Do not invent this relation to rescue a fit; if it is not defined from the source geometry, the identifiability domain is incomplete.
3. For any nonempty channel index set `I` (which may be a singleton), give each channel a typed forward map `f_i : Theta_adm -> Y_i`.
4. Define the joint readout `J(theta) = (f_i(theta))_{i in I}`.
5. Structural identifiability is injectivity on the quotient: `J(theta) = J(theta') -> theta ~ theta'`. Equivalently, every pair of physically inequivalent admissible states is separated by at least one channel.

A single channel is sufficient if its map is injective on the quotient. Multiple channels may be needed when no single channel separates all inequivalent states. Two channels are a useful sufficient design in the existing theorem, not a universal minimum. Correlated channels can still fail to separate; channel count alone proves nothing.

Keep three levels separate:

- `STRUCTURAL_IDENTIFIABILITY`: deterministic forward map is injective modulo the declared physical equivalence.
- `STATISTICAL_IDENTIFIABILITY`: the observation-distribution family `P_theta` differs for inequivalent states. This requires an explicit likelihood/instrument/noise model, not just a noiseless map.
- `PRACTICAL_IDENTIFIABILITY`: the actual data and uncertainty provide adequate predeclared power on held-out observations, with covariance and shared nuisance parameters included.

Recommended gate states: `MAP_INCOMPLETE`, `IDENTIFIABILITY_DOMAIN_UNSPECIFIED`, `STRUCTURALLY_UNIDENTIFIABLE`, `STATISTICAL_MODEL_MISSING`, `PRACTICALLY_UNRESOLVED`, `PREDICTION_READY`. A converged fit is not identification.
