## 3. Support-level decision contract

For uncertainty-inclusive, resolution-declared prediction regions in one common detector space, define `P_H` and `P_0` and exclusive regions `E_H=P_H\\P_0`, `E_0=P_0\\P_H`.

- Both exclusive regions nonempty: two-sided support separation is possible, pending actual witnesses.
- `P_H` is a subset of `P_0`: no H(s)H-exclusive allowed outcome; support membership alone cannot uniquely favor H(s)H.
- `P_0` is a subset of `P_H`: no standard-exclusive allowed outcome; H(s)H has broader support and may be less restrictive.
- Equal regions: observational equivalence at the declared set-membership resolution, not equality of likelihoods.
- Partial overlap: only the exclusive portions discriminate.

For a held-out observation region `U_y`, H(s)H is excluded at this gate when `U_y` is disjoint from `P_H`; the standard comparator is excluded when `U_y` is disjoint from `P_0`; H(s)H-exclusive support requires intersection with `P_H` and disjointness from `P_0`. If the observation region intersects both, the support-level result is inconclusive. Use one uncertainty construction only; do not count observation uncertainty twice.

This is deterministic set-membership logic, not a likelihood-ratio, Bayesian, scoring-rule, or model-confidence-set procedure. Probability-level comparison requires a separate normalized likelihood/model, shared nuisance treatment, and predeclared score.
