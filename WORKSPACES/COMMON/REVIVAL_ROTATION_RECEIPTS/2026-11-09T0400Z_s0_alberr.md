## Glossary and mapping diagram

- **Axis \(L\):** 1D straight curve in the 2024 3D toy.
- **Helix \(H\):** 1D curve at fixed radial distance from \(L\).
- **Plane \(\Pi_s\):** 2D plane perpendicular to \(L\).
- **Time surface \(T_t\):** proposed 3D hypersurface in a 4D ambient space.
- **Filament/centerline \(\gamma\):** 1D curve, not a 4D finite-volume worldtube.
- **Worldtube/core \(W\):** 4D support when finite extent/internal structure is intended.
- **Slab \(B_{t,h}\):** finite-thickness 4D region, not the 3D time surface.
- **Worldsheet \(\Sigma_2\):** 2D embedded surface, not the 3D timesheet.
- **\(u^\mu\):** vector field, not by itself an embedded hypersurface/foliation.
- **\(J^{\mu\nu}\):** rank-2 current in the assistant-generated Blockwave action; no source map to the direct intersection trace.
