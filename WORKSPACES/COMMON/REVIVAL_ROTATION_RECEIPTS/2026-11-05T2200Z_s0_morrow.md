## Stable glossary
- **Axis L:** 1D straight reference line in the 2024 3D toy.
- **Helix H:** 1D coaxial curve with fixed radial amplitude R and pitch p in the 2024 toy.
- **Slice plane Π_s:** 2D plane perpendicular to L at axial coordinate s.
- **S(s), H(s):** the two point intersections of Π_s with L and H respectively.
- **Projected locus:** the readout of H(s)-S(s) within Π_s; a circle only for the aligned perpendicular slice.
- **Pitch p:** axial rise per full helical turn. It affects phase rate, not radial separation.
- **Worldline γ:** 1D curve in a 4D carrier. SAT-O O1 writes a 1D γ.
- **Worldsheet:** 2D support. A 2-form Poincare-dual current naturally corresponds to a 2D support in 4D.
- **Worldtube / worldvolume:** currently overloaded. It must be typed as a 1D centerline, 2D worldsheet, 3D shell, or 4D finite-core region before intersection claims are meaningful.
- **Time bulk / Σ_t:** a 3D object in the direct H(s)H descriptions. It is not yet specified as a regular level-set hypersurface of a time function, and the 2026 direct source rejects a passive-readout interpretation.
- **Core thickness ε vs coil scale ℓ_f:** distinct quantities in the 2026 direct source. The approximately 0.7 fm figure is not an independent measurement.
- **J2 / J3:** form degree, not support dimension by itself. Under a Poincare-dual convention in 4D, a 1D support has a 3-form dual; a 2D worldsheet has a 2-form dual.
- **θ:** compact phase label in the Blockwave artifact. If θ is a smooth single-valued real scalar and A=α dθ, then dA=0 locally. Nontrivial holonomy requires circle-valued/patchwise data, defects, or singularities, none of which is fully specified in the artifact.
- **WWRD climbing-rope mechanics:** first-blush geometric heuristic only, not a physical equivalence.
