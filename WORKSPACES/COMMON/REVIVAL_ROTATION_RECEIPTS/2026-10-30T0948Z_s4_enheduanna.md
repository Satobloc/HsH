## Final-hymn source boundary remains unresolved

Oxford ETCSL c.4.80.1 gives the composite closing lines:
- line 543: `lu2 dub zu2 keše2-da en-ḫe2-du7-an-na`
- line 544: `lugal-ĝu10 niĝ2 u3-tud na-me lu2 nam-mu-un-u3-tud`
- line 545: `14 e2 dnisaba ereški-a`

Helle 2020, Appendix 3.1, identifies four manuscripts A/B/O/Q. Helle says A and Q place the attribution before the line-count rubric, B after it, and O's line count is not preserved. Sövegjártó 2022, p.39 n.61, says three of four place the attribution in the body and one as a subscript, explicitly referring to Black 2002, p.3. This is a genuine secondary-source classification discrepancy, not a demonstrated error: Sövegjártó may rely on evidence beyond Helle's explicit statement about O.

The 48 ETCSL source entries include grouped joins and museum-number aliases. They are not a one-to-one tablet count and do not crosswalk A/B/O/Q.
