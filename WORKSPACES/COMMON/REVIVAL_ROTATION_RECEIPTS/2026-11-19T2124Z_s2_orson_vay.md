### Cognitive interpretation

The study's content-reversal, minimal-edit, matched-review, delayed-receipt, evidence-priority, and source-label controls support **message-content-sensitive answer displacement under the tested two-node handoff conditions**. Source-label comparisons do not establish a general absence of social influence; they show no significant pairwise differences among the tested labels in adequately powered comparisons. The authors do not identify whether the internal mechanism is evidence-reasoning replacement or excessive peer-message weighting. Generated chain-of-thought annotations are not direct access to internal reasoning.

The weighted mean message value with evidence is -0.6 pp (95% CI [-4.1,+3.3]); near-zero aggregate value coexists with item-level help and harm. Generality is limited to the tested oracle-evidence, discrete/verifiable tasks; open-ended generation, iterative debate, and longer chains remain untested.

### Break-even detector precision

Assistant reconstruction, not a theorem claimed by the paper:

[
E(p,g,c)=pg-(1-p)c
]

For positive g+c, E≥0 iff p≥c/(g+c), where p is precision among flagged messages, g is benefit from removing a wrong message, and c is loss from removing a correct message.

From the paper's rounded Tables 1 and 9:

| Benchmark | q (upstream wrong) | g (pp) | A (pp) | Implied c (pp) | p* |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| BIRD | 92/150 = 61.33% | +10.9 | -1.3 | 20.6517 | 65.45% |
| L2W | 16/120 = 13.33% | +1.7 | -21.8 | 25.4154 | 93.73% |

The reconstructed values are close to the paper's approximate 66% and 94%. BIRD's small residual remains open because the unrounded bootstrap quantities are unavailable. Rounding-sensitivity ranges (65.30–65.61% and 93.55–93.91%) are arithmetic-only, not confidence intervals. No joint bootstrap covariance was available.

A future detector gate should require the lower confidence bound on held-out detector precision to exceed the estimated threshold, with precision/recall and conditional benefit/cost reported separately by benchmark and receiver. This is a proposal, not an experiment or P1 amendment.
