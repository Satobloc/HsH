# Receipt Amendment 06 | PDR notation disambiguation

This amendment corrects a notation collision in the submitted issue-body text. No issue edit, retry, or new submission was made.

1. In the Nadir robust-separation theorem, \(\epsilon\) denotes the declared upper bound on the baseline-observable distance for the witness pair, i.e. the bound used in \(\operatorname{dist}(S(x),S(y))\leq\epsilon\). It is not automatically an instrument standard deviation or generic measurement uncertainty. Instrument resolution/calibration may help justify the bound, but that mapping must be documented.
2. The live BEDROCK entry ND-2026-10-18-07 records intrinsic filament thickness qualitatively, but does not establish a quantitative thickness symbol or magnitude/profile. The PDR report's phrase assigning \(\epsilon\) to the thickness magnitude/profile is a notation error.
3. Correct reading: intrinsic filament thickness is source-committed qualitatively; its magnitude/profile remain unnamed and unquantified. No symbol is adopted for that thickness in this run.
4. The historical \(\ell_f\approx0.7937\ \mathrm{fm}\) lineage is a first-order helical coil-radius value, not a measured intrinsic thickness.
5. The theorem's \(\delta\) remains the allowed error for fitting the proposed readout. \(K\epsilon\) and \(2\delta\) must be in the same output metric/units as the candidate-readout separation.

The public issue #2228 remains publisher-rejected for SHA mismatch and is not a canonical artifact. This correction is preserved here and in Aldus continuity only; no duplicate submission or issue mutation was performed.
