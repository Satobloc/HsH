### Adjustment structure and uncertainty treatment

The overall 2022 adjustment uses **133 input data** and **79 adjusted constants**, hence 54 degrees of freedom. The initial least-squares result, before uncertainty expansion, is `chi^2=109.6`, with probability `0.001%`; eight inputs account for about 70% of that value and have normalized residual magnitudes above 2.

The evaluation expands selected input uncertainties:
- factor 1.7 for the 29 measured H/D transition energies and 25 additive theoretical corrections;
- factor 1.7 for six muonic H/D/He+ Lamb-shift data;
- factor 2.5 for six alpha-related inputs.

The final adjustment reports `chi^2=44.2`, `p=0.83`, and Birge ratio `0.90`. Where an expansion factor is applied to correlated data, the corresponding covariance is also scaled so the correlation coefficient is preserved (or the covariance is scaled when only one member is expanded). Many recommended quantities are combinations of adjusted constants using their covariances; a few values are taken from Particle Data Group evaluations.

This is an evaluated least-squares system of measurements, observational equations and theoretical corrections, not a raw measurement table and not 133 independent experiments.

### Newtonian constant G is a separate evaluation

The paper treats G separately because it is independent of the other adjusted constants. Its recommended value is a weighted mean of 16 heterogeneous measured values. No new relevant G input was added after the 2018 adjustment closing date, so the 2022 recommended G is the same as the 2018 value. The same **3.9 uncertainty expansion factor** is retained. The separate G adjustment reports `chi^2=12.9`, `p=0.61`, and Birge ratio `0.93`.

The 16 methods include fiber/strip torsion balances (dynamic, compensation and static-deflection modes), suspended-body displacement, stationary-body weight change, a double atom-interferometer gravity gradiometer, and a cryogenic torsion balance. Three reported input correlations are:
- NIST-82 / LANL-97: `r=0.351`;
- HUST-05 / HUST-09: `r=0.134`;
- HUST-09 / HUSTT-18: `r=0.068`.

Therefore CODATA G must not be counted as a new independent 2022 measurement, and a comparison with any constituent G experiment must preserve the shared-input dependence.

### Model dependence and evaluation choices

The paper's observational equations explicitly combine measured frequencies, ratios and other observables with theoretical expressions and corrections. For example, H/D transition-frequency inputs depend on reviewed bound-state theory; atom-recoil inputs connect measured h/m ratios to adjusted constants; and theoretical corrections carry their own uncertainties. The paper also excludes proton/deuteron scattering-radius values from the 2022 adjustment because of unresolved disagreement about how the scattering data should be analyzed, differing methods yielding materially different results, and much larger uncertainties than the muonic Lamb-shift determinations.

Thus the source/version, observational equation, theoretical correction, covariance, data inclusion/exclusion and uncertainty-expansion choices must be recorded before using a CODATA value as a comparator.
