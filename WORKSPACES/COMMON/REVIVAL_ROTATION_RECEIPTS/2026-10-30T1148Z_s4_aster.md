- The paper defines PCR as the fraction of within-set question pairs where **both** answers are correct (`main.tex`, line 105). The analysis script maps the stored `pairwise_consistency_rate` field into PCR. The evaluator's `compute_metrics` instead counts pairs whose **correctness flags match**: both correct **or both wrong**. Its additional `or (both correct)` clause is redundant once equality of the Boolean correctness flags is true.
- Consequently, the stored field is a same-correctness-status rate, not the paper's stated both-correct rate. The two can differ substantially.

## Additional source-alignment note: revision recheck

The paper's figure caption (`main.tex`, line 240) says the revised answer is then re-checked. The algorithm pseudocode (lines 124–134) does not show a second check, and `evaluate_cgd` (lines 258–279) does not call the NLI checker on `revision_response`. It appends `(question, final_answer, raw_response)`; later NLI calls use `prev_raw`, i.e. the initial raw response, while repair prompts receive `prev_answer`. Thus the implementation's checker history is not the revised answer history described by the caption. Treat this as a source/figure mismatch to review, not an observed behavioral effect.
