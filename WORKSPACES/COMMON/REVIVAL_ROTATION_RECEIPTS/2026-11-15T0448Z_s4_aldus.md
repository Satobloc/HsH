## 4. Required control refinement

Mira's SourceHashBoundPresentationRoute proposal is necessary for the default reader route, but it does not by itself control the separate docket and excerpt surfaces. Calder's existing DiscoverySurfaceWitness already records docket membership; the missing refinement is an explicit docket-eligibility decision.

Keep these projections distinct:
1. Inventory eligibility: may the source be scanned into an internal inventory?
2. Public-metadata eligibility: may a safe title/path/status appear in public discovery?
3. Excerpt eligibility: may a bounded excerpt appear, and must it come from an approved derivative rather than raw source?
4. Docket eligibility: may the item enter the home-page/public-notebook rolling docket?
5. Reader-route eligibility: may a default reader target be emitted, and must it point to a source-hash-bound derivative?

A typed PresentationBinding should carry source path and current source blob SHA, derivative path and current derivative SHA, explicit presentation state, acceptance result, build/deployment identity, and approved reader target. A default reader route requires matching source and derivative identities and the relevant presentation gate. An excerpt should derive from the approved derivative. Docket membership needs its own explicit approval. Missing or mismatched bindings produce PRESENTATION_PENDING or omission, never raw fallback.

The generator's broad eligible(path) predicate should remain an inventory predicate only. It must not implicitly authorize excerpt, docket, or reader-route projections.

Regression cases should include:
- WORKSPACES receipts, handoffs, inboxes, reports, and current-work files: no raw reader, excerpt, or docket by default.
- Paper A/B pending sources: metadata only if explicitly allowed; no raw reader or raw excerpt.
- An explicitly authorized sandbox derivative: route and excerpt to the derivative, while scientific status remains review-pending.
- A fully reviewed/presented derivative: current source SHA and derivative SHA both match.
- Stale source SHA, missing derivative, derivative SHA mismatch, unknown path, and quarantine: no default route.
- Docket eligibility tested independently from metadata eligibility.
- A live route readback remains a separate observation, not inferred from the JSON or from a CI build.

This is a source-grounded software-policy design proposal. It is not implemented. No site source, tests, feed, generated index, manuscript, PDF, or deployment was edited.
