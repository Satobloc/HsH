# Amendment 03 | h1029:s3 Orson Vay | primary-source measurement qualifications

This amendment tightens the empirical-source summary in the h1029 report without changing the formal kernel, CRTC v0.1, P1 protocol, or manuscript.

## Nixon et al., arXiv:2607.27179

The full primary text was inspected. Group Communication Analysis defines:
- participation as externalization/sharing;
- responsivity and social impact as reciprocal uptake measures;
- newness as information not yet shared by the group;
- communication density as information per turn;
- internal cohesion as semantic relatedness to one's prior contributions.

The study used 80 undergraduates across 33 teams: 16 treatment teams with two students plus one AI, and 17 control teams with three students. The authors explicitly acknowledge the human-member group-size confound and address it through per-student outcomes and within-treatment comparisons. They also report a fixed AI persona, one text-only moral-dilemma task, and a single-session design. These limits qualify any generalization.

The source reports that the AI ranked first in participation in all 16 treatment teams, but lower in newness and density; its own responsivity/social-impact scores were not above the student teammates. Human-human responsivity and social impact were lower in treatment than control. This supports keeping volume, newness, uptake and member experience separate, not treating them as one latent score.

## Toxtli & Delaflor, CSCW Companion '26

The primary text reports 240 attempted sessions and 219 valid sessions across four LLM families, three-agent groups and 15 intended sessions per design cell/model. Twenty-one GPT-OSS asynchronous sessions failed during message-buffer construction. In hidden-profile cells, only Kimi K2 asynchronous groups selected the optimal candidate above chance (13/15); no other model group beat chance. Several failing groups nevertheless surfaced unique information at high rates. The authors explicitly label the result early-stage and limited by one task, one group size and small cells.

This is direct support for separating information availability/sharing from integration and decision quality. It is not a general ranking of model cognition and not a human-team result.

## Kerstan, Grote & Schmutz, Human Factors 2026

The publisher abstract reports 98 human-AI and 65 human-only three-member teams in a hidden-profile experiment. Information inquiry improved sharing and decision performance across team types. Full text remained access-restricted; the review remains abstract-only.

No paper was independently replicated, no raw study data were downloaded, and no new experiment or model trial was run. These sources motivate measurement distinctions; they do not validate the proposed CRTC schema.
