## 4. Substantive cognition work

The new work distinguishes monitoring validity from causal control, and reactive actions from prospective allocation. It proposes a five-axis diagnostic profile:
1. monitor discrimination/calibration;
2. signal-to-action causal coupling;
3. decision horizon (post-answer, in-process, pre-commitment);
4. controller locus (base model, wrapper/harness, separate meta-agent, post-trained policy);
5. incremental utility and transfer beyond external difficulty/trace features.

Primary sources cross-read:
- Kumaran et al., Nature Machine Intelligence 2026, arXiv:2603.22161: activation-steering evidence for confidence-to-abstention, with confidence representation separated from threshold policy.
- Kumaran et al., arXiv:2604.22271: PANL/LAT post-answer error monitoring and correction.
- Cacioli, arXiv:2604.15702: retrospective monitoring versus prospective regulation; T6 exploratory, r=.17 with wide confidence interval.
- Cao et al., arXiv:2605.14186: model-originated FOK/JOL signals consumed by an external SVM retry/stop harness.
- Al Nazi & Roy Dipta, TRIAGE, arXiv:2605.13414: prospective task-portfolio selection/order/token allocation.
- Kim, Lee & Kim, Findings of EACL 2026: prompted self-assessed confidence with a hybrid model/wrapper reasoning-stop controller.
- When2Think, arXiv:2609.19671: RLVR-trained compute policy using reference statistics and verifier feedback, not an explicit self-monitoring signal.

The resulting distinction is action-, locus- and horizon-specific. Causal confidence-to-abstention does not automatically establish prospective task allocation, long-horizon self-regulation or delegation. The future signal-ablation matrix is a proposal only.
