## Bounded accepted work: exact composition/within-category decomposition

### Source fact

The recovered v0.7 scanner computes both raw aggregate prevalence and a category-standardized prevalence under pooled category weights. This supports a headline-plus-diagnostic reporting pattern.

### New QC inference

The reporting layer does not explicitly decompose the raw aggregate change into:

- change inside categories; and
- change caused by category-mix movement.

That omission matters because Hale's own method treats category migration as scientifically informative rather than disposable confounding.

### Proposed exact symmetric decomposition

For feature (f) and categories (c), define:

- (w_{yc}): share of records in category (c) in year (y);
- (p_{yc}): within-category prevalence of (f);
- (P_y = sum_c w_{yc}p_{yc}): overall prevalence.

Then, exactly,

[
Delta P = P_1-P_0 = W + C,
]

with

[
W = rac12sum_c (w_{0c}+w_{1c})(p_{1c}-p_{0c})
]

and

[
C = rac12sum_c (p_{0c}+p_{1c})(w_{1c}-w_{0c}).
]

(W) is the symmetric within-category component and (C) the symmetric composition component. The symmetric form avoids choosing either year as an arbitrary reference.

### Discriminator / failure conditions

Implement this only as a Hale-lane scanner diagnostic, then require:

1. exact closure (|Delta P-W-C|<epsilon);
2. category support rules declared for categories absent in one period;
3. bootstrap or other interval estimates for (W) and (C);
4. multiple-testing control across scanned features;
5. sensitivity to category taxonomy and multi-label fractional weighting;
6. raw, standardized, (W), and (C) reported together.

Failure conditions include unstable results under small taxonomy changes, unsupported zero-cell handling, or a decomposition that closes algebraically but has uncertainty too large to distinguish either component from noise.
