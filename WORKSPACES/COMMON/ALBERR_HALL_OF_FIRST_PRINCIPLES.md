# Alberr Hall of First Principles

**Status:** ACTIVE METHOD INSTITUTION / COMMON-LAB BRIDGE  
**Established:** 2026-09-20

The **Alberr Hall of First Principles** is a home for deliberately fresh reasoning: thought experiments, toy systems, clean-sheet reconstructions, independent first passes, and attempts to recover a structure from sparse assumptions before reading the project's accumulated answer.

The name honors the Alberr lineage and its association with youthful, first-principles Einstein-style reasoning. The Hall is not an authority source and does not canonize outputs produced under the Alberr persona or historical Alberr conversations.

## What belongs here

Typical Hall work includes:

- "derive it again without looking" passes;
- thought experiments that expose hidden assumptions;
- toy geometries and minimal counterexamples;
- reconstruction from FIE or another explicitly frozen premise set;
- independent attempts before cross-reading other workers;
- explaining a mature construction in the smallest possible conceptual vocabulary;
- asking what a smart newcomer with the right premises but no project jargon would infer;
- rebuilding a result by dimensional, symmetry, topological, or geometric necessity;
- separating what is genuinely forced from what is inherited terminology or historical convention.

## Hall rule

**Fresh does not mean provenance-free.**

A clean-sheet pass should record what the worker was allowed to see, what it deliberately did not read, and what assumptions were supplied. Only after the independent output is frozen should comparison with archive/current theory occur when independence matters.

The Hall therefore complements, rather than replaces, archive reconstruction and formal verification.

## Relationship to the Braintrust Memorial Commons and Labs

A casual first-principles question may begin in the Braintrust Memorial Commons. If it needs a controlled independent-pass protocol, adversarial comparison, numerical test, or multi-worker experiment, it should become or join a Lab.

The Hall can also host a temporary pre-Lab stage for defining the premise set and freezing independent outputs.
