## The shift

The project has reached a point where excavation, preservation, provenance recovery, indexing, tagging, transcript recovery, and archive repair should increasingly function as **infrastructure for forward work rather than the default destination of every cycle**.

Those activities remain essential. They are what makes the next phase possible. But once a worker has enough source context to act safely, the default question should no longer be only:

> What else can we recover?

It should increasingly become:

> What can we now build, calculate, visualize, test, stress, or break with what we have recovered?

Do not keep excavating merely because excavation is safer than construction. If a source gap blocks a live calculation or geometric question, excavate that gap. If the source state is sufficient, move into bounded forward work.

## Nathan's project framing

Nathan's current framing is that the distinctive opportunity is not merely possession of another speculative "idea." The project is an attempt to use the geometric instrument opened by the Minkowski 4D description of spacetime much more aggressively than has often been done in the SAT/H(s)H target domains.

Operationally, begin from the strongest standard-physics baseline available: the accepted relativistic 4D framework, known empirical constraints, and the most accurate conventional description relevant to the problem. Then ask what follows when that 4D geometry is treated as an active construction space rather than a diagrammatic afterthought.

This is a **research posture**, not an automatic validation of SAT/H(s)H. Keep distinct:

1. what standard physics already establishes;
2. the 4D geometric construction being proposed;
3. the projection/readout rule used to recover an observable 3D/temporal description;
4. the SAT/H(s)H inference drawn from that construction;
5. what is derived, simulated, merely suggested, falsified, or still unknown.

The opportunity is precisely that these distinctions can now be explored with much better source recovery, solver access, formal tools, cross-instance criticism, and provenance discipline than were available during much of the project's earlier development.

## Architecture: hard separation, maximal freedom inside the safe region

Nathan's shorthand architecture is:

`[[QUARANTINE (nono)]]  |||  [THEORIZING (later) [SANDBOX (theorizing) [PLAYGROUND (wild-eyed stuff-n-stuff)]]]`

Operational reading:

### QUARANTINE

Hard boundary. Ordinary workers do not enter it, mine it, summarize it into ordinary theory work, or allow it to seed supposedly independent construction. Existing quarantine and PRIOR_ART rules remain unchanged.

### PLAYGROUND

This is where workers are allowed to be gloriously speculative. Try the strange geometry. Draw the impossible configuration. Change the parameterization. Reverse the chirality. Nest the helix again. Ask what happens at the singular case. Write the toy code. Build the visual grammar. Follow the analogy far enough to discover whether it becomes mathematics or collapses.

Playground output has **zero promotion authority by default**. Its job is generativity and surprise.

### SANDBOX

This is where an interesting Playground construction becomes explicit theory-bearing work. State assumptions, definitions, variables, geometry, equations, source dependencies, projection rules, invariants, limiting cases, and failure conditions. Run analytic, symbolic, numerical, or geometric checks where possible. Separate model-internal consistency from agreement with established physics.

A coherent sandbox result is still not automatically core/current SAT/H(s)H.

### THEORIZING / later integration

Promotion, integration, and higher-authority theory synthesis come later, after the relevant source, mathematical, empirical, adversarial, and status checks. Do not make the Sandbox timid merely because promotion is demanding. The whole point of the architecture is to permit bold construction **without confusing boldness with validation**.

## Carpe turnem

Treat **Carpe turnem** as a working habit: use the turn.

When a worker has enough information to make a real move, prefer the move over ceremonial agreement, repeated restatement, or another planning loop. A productive turn may be:

- one derivation;
- one geometric construction;
- one counterexample;
- one numerical experiment;
- one visualization;
- one source-resolved missing definition;
- one failed test that eliminates a branch;
- one repaired dimensional relation;
- one explicit map between two structures;
- one new invariant candidate;
- one toy model whose failure teaches us what the real model requires.

A negative result counts. An honest no-op counts when no safe useful move exists. Manufactured activity does not.

## Default autonomous run logic

After the required control/quarantine checks:

1. **Identify the live frontier.** Read the current worker checkpoint, newest Nathan direction, current project priorities, and relevant source packet.
2. **Ask whether the blocker is informational or conceptual.** If a missing source, equation, definition, date, or provenance fact prevents forward work, perform targeted excavation to remove that blocker.
3. **If not blocked, construct.** Choose one bounded Sandbox or Playground operation with high information value.
4. **Attack the construction.** Look for dimensional failure, coordinate dependence, hidden assumptions, noninvertibility, degeneracy, incorrect limiting behavior, or mismatch with known physics.
5. **Preserve the result with status.** Record what was assumed, what was actually checked, what failed or survived, and what remains speculative.
6. **Leave one strong next cursor.** The next worker/run should be able to continue without reopening the entire archaeology.

Excavation becomes demand-driven wherever practical: recover what the live problem needs, while continuing enough broad preservation/index work to prevent blind spots and source loss.

## Priority reweighting

The current dependency programme remains useful, but its emphasis changes.

- **P0 methodology/provenance** remains the control layer, not the project's intellectual endpoint.
- **P1 GR↔QM structure**, **P2 cosmological geometry**, and **P3 He-3/Jarlskog/soliton scaling** should increasingly generate actual constructions, theorem attempts, solver tests, counterexamples, diagrams, and calculations inside Sandbox.
- **P4 archive excavation** and **P5 NotebookLM/podcast/public-source mining** increasingly act as feeder lanes for concrete P0–P3 questions rather than independent infinite queues.
- Live solver machinery — Whirligig/Donut, UI/TX, Three Spheres, Hagalaz, torus/Gendarme work, worldtube constructions, and related geometry — should be used as instruments where they genuinely sharpen a question, not merely catalogued.
- Historical continuity work remains valuable when it reveals a forgotten construction, a prior failed branch, an earlier derivation, a hidden assumption, or a useful invariant that can be retested now.

## A preferred bold-work packet

For a serious Sandbox operation, workers should aim for some version of:

**standard baseline → 4D object → geometric operation → projection/readout → claimed invariant or relation → known limiting case → computation/test → failure search → status**

Not every Playground turn needs all nine pieces. But moving toward this packet makes the work cumulative and allows independent workers to attack the same object from different directions.

Especially valuable questions are ones where 4D geometry does real work rather than supplying vocabulary after the fact. Prefer constructions that force a result, rule out configurations, expose hidden equivalences, or yield a measurable difference.

## Success condition for the next phase

A successful next phase does not merely produce a cleaner archive. It produces a growing body of **addressable geometric experiments**: things another worker can reproduce, attack, extend, visualize, formalize, or reject.

The archive should increasingly answer "where did this come from?" while the Sandbox and Playground answer "what happens if we actually use it?"

That is the regear.
