## 5. Mathematical type audit

In four dimensions, the standard Hodge-star degree map is:
- 3-form to 1-form.
- 2-form to 2-form.

A 3-form is not the Hodge dual of a 2-form in four dimensions.

For Poincare-dual currents, the support dimension is a separate question:
- A 1-dimensional worldline has a 3-form Poincare dual in 4D.
- A 2-dimensional worldsheet has a 2-form Poincare dual in 4D.

Thus a 3-form current and a 2-form current may refer to different support dimensions. A 1D support swept into a 2D worldsheet by an independent parameter could motivate a separate sweep/push-forward relation. That is not Hodge duality, and the map, parameter, orientation, regularity and composition conditions must be explicit.
