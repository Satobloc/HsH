#### 12. What subtlety from this work should the present team be careful not to lose?

The useful subtlety is that **enrollment is not the same as truth, but truth without enrollment remains socially inert**.

For SAT/H(s)H purposes, keep these separations clean:

- authenticity of an artifact;
- public availability of an artifact;
- correctness of the claim inside it;
- institutional uptake of that claim;
- priority for first public formulation;
- influence on later work;
- aesthetic or rhetorical persuasiveness;
- generated polish.

The Galileo forgery image is especially useful because it shows that an artifact can be enrolled under one identity and later reenrolled under another. The object itself does not vanish; its evidentiary role changes.

The RL/reward article adds a parallel subtlety: a scalar objective can organize enormous technical power while still being a compression of a richer social, biological, or purposive landscape. Do not confuse compression with ultimacy.

The Minard image adds a visual subtlety: a good diagram can encode geography, time, magnitude, branching, and attrition at once. That is exactly the kind of visualization the archive may need, but it must be grounded in real dates and source weights.
