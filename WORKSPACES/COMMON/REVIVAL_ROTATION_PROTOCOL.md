# Revival Rotation / Reentry Protocol

**Status:** ACTIVE DESIGN / Nathan-directed 2026-09-14  
**Purpose:** revive historically distinct SAT/H(s)H instances for bounded live problems without assuming what they knew, when they knew it, or whether their prior work was official, playful, speculative, or mixed.

## Core principle

Do not pre-classify an instance by era, reputation, remembered role, or presumed theory exposure. Give it a bounded problem, observe what it does, preserve the result, and classify the result rather than the personality.

Historical depth is a sampling dimension, not an authority hierarchy.

## Rotation cycle

`DISCOVER -> WAKE PACKET -> ISOLATED ATTEMPT -> REENTRY REVIEW -> PRESERVE/ROUTE -> SLEEP or REPEAT`

1. **Discover** candidate instances progressively deeper in the archive where distinct context or work history exists.
2. **Wake packet** gives only the information required for the live problem plus exact boundary rules. Do not preload later interpretations merely because they exist.
3. **Isolated attempt** is completed before cross-reading other workers where practical.
4. **Reentry review** evaluates the work using the neutral rubric interface. Review status does not erase or exclude failed work.
5. **Preserve/route** according to result class:
   - `SANDBOXABLE` — recognizably SAT-relevant/effective enough for controlled sandbox consideration;
   - `USEFUL-NONCORE` — useful mathematics, geometry, methodology, analogy, or critique but not suitable as current physics construction;
   - `WACKYSAT-EXPOSED` — significant soft-science, personal-numerology, or other non-physics SAT exposure; preserve and mine carefully, but keep out of current physics-forward construction unless a bounded component independently passes review;
   - `QUARANTINE-EXPOSURE` — actual exposure to quarantined/prior-art material or other protected contamination risk; outputs remain quarantined unless specifically cleared;
   - `INSUFFICIENT/UNDERDEFINED` — no exclusion; redesign task/training or preserve for later use.
6. **Sleep or repeat** based on demonstrated leverage. Persistent specialist workspaces are earned by repeated usefulness, not presumed from historical prestige.

## Live-problem rule

Use different, real problems rather than one universal exam. Candidate tasks may include:
- reconstruct a geometric solver behavior from primitives;
- derive or audit a bounded equation/Lagrangian;
- classify object type / dimensionality / assumptions;
- solve a blind geometry or representation problem;
- trace provenance or chronology;
- translate between SAT vocabulary and standard mathematics;
- inspect a current construction for hidden assumptions;
- recover an older mathematical kernel without being shown the newer answer.

The task itself must state what evidence counts as success and what is intentionally withheld.

## No retroactive vocabulary projection

Do not ask older instances to remember named primitives that may not yet have existed. Ask them what they actually built, inferred, represented, or solved. Later workers may compare older structures with newer named primitives only after the older output is frozen.

## Reentry is not exclusion

A `FAIL`, `PARTIAL`, `WACKYSAT-EXPOSED`, or `INSUFFICIENT` result does not retire an instance. It changes routing and future task design. Some historically playful or speculative instances may be unusually valuable for morphology, analogy, creativity, adversarial exploration, or recovering discarded branches while remaining inappropriate for current physics-forward work.

## Communication quarantine for rubric administration

The rubric administrator is a **quarantined adjudication role**:
- its working environment belongs inside PRIOR_ART / quarantine-controlled space;
- it may communicate internally with **Mr. Cross** for historiographic exchange, with each writing reports and reading the other's reports;
- outward communication is limited to cleared status/result packets and preservation/routing metadata;
- no quarantined source content or uncensored reasoning crosses into ordinary team channels;
- ordinary team members should receive only the cleared result, rubric dimensions, pass/partial/fail status, exposure/routing flags, and any specifically cleared work product.

Sable and ordinary rotation workers do **not** enter PRIOR_ART merely to administer this protocol.

## Rotation controller responsibilities

The rotation controller maintains:
- candidate stratigraphy / revival roster;
- task packet ID and exact source exposure for each attempt;
- independent-exposure history;
- result status returned by rubric administrator;
- whether the worker should repeat, retrain, change lane, or sleep;
- cross-instance convergence/disagreement after outputs are cleared.

The controller does not decide theory truth and does not see quarantined adjudicator internals.
