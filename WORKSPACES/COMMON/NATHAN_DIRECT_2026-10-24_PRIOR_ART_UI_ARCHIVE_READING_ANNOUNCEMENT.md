   - For every equation recovered, seek the surrounding Nathan-authored conversation and the constructional intent.

   - Investigate Hinton, Kelvin, Minkowski, Einstein-Cartan, braid/topology/higher-dimensional traditions, nLab-linked literature, and related historical/current communities as potentially substantial prior art and convergent development.

6. **Map-first stance.**
   - The project is, for the most part, a map rather than a new ontology.
   - Its strength is reducing the space of possible guesses while preserving known physics.
   - Confidence in the map/construction method must not be confused with confidence in every equation or historical realization.

   - Namespace/addressing/indexing should preserve source distinctions and only collapse objects under explicit criteria.

## Research posture

The relevant question is not "is this external theory ours?" or "did SAT get there first?"

Ask:
- What was constructed internally?
- What does the external literature actually establish?
- Which structures are the same, transformable, analogous, historically related, or genuinely different?
- Which route is simpler or more useful for the current job?
- Does the comparison sharpen, replace, or falsify any internal construction?
- What citation/precedence obligations follow?
