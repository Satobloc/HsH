## 8. Conditional geometry check, not a physical result

For transverse embedded submanifolds A and Σ in an n-dimensional ambient manifold, the generic intersection dimension is:

dim(A ∩ Σ) = dim(A) + dim(Σ) - n.

For the 2024 direct dimensional picture, if dim(A)=1, dim(Σ)=3 and n=4, the transverse intersection is zero-dimensional. This is a conditional geometric type check consistent with point-like events in that specific 2024 construction.

For the 2026 finite-core worldtube W, dim(W) is not source-defined. If W is a 2D worldsheet, its transverse intersection with a 3D hypersurface in 4D is 1D; if W is a 4D bulk region, the intersection is 3D. The readout dimension therefore cannot be selected until W is typed.
