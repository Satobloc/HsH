## Executive finding

A previously unintegrated raw user-role source from 18 July 2026 clarifies a specific ontological reading: the straight worldtube is described as vacuum, with a BEC-sea interpretation; particle identity and behavior are attributed to the morphology of that same carrier; topology is included within that morphology.

This is consistent in broad direction with the original SAT claim that vacuum and matter use the same filamental structures in different intersectional configurations. It does **not** establish that the BEC-like medium is the settled substrate, nor does it supply a mathematical definition of morphology, a worldtube dimension, or a map from 4D morphology to the observed particle section.

The useful reconciliation is:

- the worldline/worldtube is the 4D carrier;
- its morphology is the proposed source of particle identity and properties;
- the time surface/timesheet supplies the observational intersection or readout;
- the BEC-ocean interpretation remains a candidate;
- the Kerr-core exclusion route remains a conjecture, not a derivation of fermionic statistics.

This report is additive to existing BEC-helicity, particle-morphology, Kerr-core, and line-to-worldtube audits. It does not replace them or create a new canonical physical model.

### 4.1 Conditional dimensional check

For transverse intersections in a 4D ambient manifold, an m-dimensional embedded object intersecting a 3D timesheet has generic intersection dimension m−1. Thus a 1D filament gives a point-like intersection; a 2D worldsheet gives a 1D trace; a 3D hypersurface gives a 2D section; and a 4D region gives a 3D section. This is a standard conditional type check, not a choice of the physical object. The source has not selected m or supplied the transversality conditions, so no physical dimension is inferred.
