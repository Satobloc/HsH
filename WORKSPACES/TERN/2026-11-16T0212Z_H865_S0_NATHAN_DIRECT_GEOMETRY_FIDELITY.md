This is only a proposed map shape. It requires a normal-frame/cross-section definition, smoothness, rank conditions, and a physical interpretation. It is not a recovered Nathan equation.

### D2. Two-form current versus line and volume support

A conserved J^(mu nu) is naturally a rank-two current for a two-dimensional worldsheet in a four-dimensional ambient space. A one-dimensional worldline has a different Poincare-dual degree; a four-dimensional finite-core region is different again. No source map converts the Blockwave J current into SAT-O1 gamma or the 2026 worldtube.

### D3. Intersection dimension and local present

For transverse supports in M^4, the generic intersection dimension is dim(A)+dim(B)-4 when nonnegative. Thus a 1D curve intersecting a 3D hypersurface is generically 0D; a 2D worldsheet gives 1D; a 3D tube boundary gives 2D; a 4D open support gives 3D. The phrase "particle mouth" therefore needs a specified support type and a readout map. A single P_i=W_i intersect Sigma_i formula is not enough.
