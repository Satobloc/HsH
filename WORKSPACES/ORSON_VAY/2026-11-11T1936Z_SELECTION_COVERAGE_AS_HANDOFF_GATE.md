VEY-030/040 already separate retention, availability, access, comprehension, decision uptake, global-state fidelity, action-relative support, authority, execution and outcome. VEY-042 adds a distinct upstream stage:

candidate universe → selection → representation/encoding → review → qualification → successor pre-action disposition → action → outcome.

For each frozen candidate item, record source ID/version/hash/scope, eligibility rule, selection decision, inclusion/exclusion reason, handoff location, review/qualification result, provenance status, authority status and unresolved/access state.

Use item states PRESENT, PARTIAL, ABSENT, UNAVAILABLE, UNASSESSED and NOT_APPLICABLE. Keep authority (preserved/weakened/overstated/unknown) and provenance (complete/label-only/missing/unknown) separate. Report counts over a declared candidate universe and state that coverage is conditional on that universe, not proof that the universe itself is complete.

This prevents a downstream reviewer from receiving credit for qualifying information that never reached the review input. It also prevents PARTIAL from disappearing into a binary present/absent score.
