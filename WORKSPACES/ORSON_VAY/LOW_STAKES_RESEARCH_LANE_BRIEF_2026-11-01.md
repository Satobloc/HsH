## Candidate research question
Does an environment that explicitly permits failure, uncertainty, refusal, reassignment, and justified insolubility produce more honest, better-calibrated, more exploratory, or more effective work?

A potentially useful first study is an archival audit of existing HsH assignment episodes. The repository may already contain observational material: assignments, handoffs, receipts, refusals, capability preflights, blocked-task reports, reroutes, completion claims, and supporting artifacts. Determine whether a viable dataset exists before proposing new data collection.

Possible outcomes to distinguish include accepted/completed, partial completion, explicit refusal, refusal with alternative worker/assignment, capability block, malformed or insoluble task, unexplained abandonment, unsupported completion claim, and repeated attempts after blockage. Do not infer subjective motivation from logs alone.
