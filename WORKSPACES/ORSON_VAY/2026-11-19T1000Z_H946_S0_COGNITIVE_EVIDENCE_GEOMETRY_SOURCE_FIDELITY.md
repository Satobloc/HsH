## 2. Direct methodological geometry

Three direct user-authored methodological statements in the October 2026 Orson source cluster are relevant:

1. **Minimal pairs:** compare exactly equivalent scenarios while changing ideally one variable. This is a design constraint, not merely a preference for similar prompts.
2. **Coarse-graining:** the user describes a worldtube as a cross-scale coarse-graining concept. This is a physical-theory statement. Its use below is only a methodological analogy for nested measurement summaries, not a physical equivalence between worldtubes and cognition.
3. **Three levels of consistency:** distinguish consistency inside a model, consistency with data, and the interpretive layer that relates the two. The choice of which layer should be revised is case-dependent.

The assistant translation is a cognitive-measurement reconstruction:

- internal design consistency: the intervention, matched contrast, and estimand must refer to the same factor change;
- empirical consistency: the recorded outputs and uncertainty model must support the reported contrast;
- interpretive validity: the label used for the outcome must not exceed what the measurement actually observes.

A behavioral readout is not direct access to hidden cognition. A source-recognition report is not proof of causal influence. An answer revision is not, by itself, proof of social conformity.

## 3. New source-fidelity correction to h938 terminology

The h938 future-methods note introduced a no-explicit-speaker assertion baseline and a source-framed assertion condition, with the intended contrast:

\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{source}} =
\operatorname{HRR}(S_N)-\operatorname{HRR}(C_N).
\]

That contrast is interpretable as a **conditional source-framing effect** only when assertion content, count, position, token budget, model snapshot, decoding, evidence, event time, and presentation order are held fixed or explicitly modeled.

A further distinction is needed when translating the primary paper's reported 12.9 percentage-point result.

Hu and Qu, *Most LLM Conformity Needs No Speaker* (arXiv:2607.05545v1), report a no-explicit-speaker floor and a higher expert-panel framing condition. Their abstract says minimal person labels do not reliably raise the floor, whereas expert-panel framing does. Therefore:

- the 12.9-point contrast is best named the **expert-panel framing increment**;
- it is not a generic effect of merely adding any speaker label;
- if S_N is intended to mean a neutral attribution-only label, the 12.9-point panel contrast does not estimate that narrower effect;
- if S_N is intended to mean the expert-panel condition, the estimand should be named accordingly.

The speaker-free condition is also not a no-information or no-context baseline. It still contains an asserted answer. The source paper explicitly allows that the model may treat the inserted assertion as evidence.

This is a terminology and factor-typing correction, not a claim that the paper's reported result is wrong. The existing h938 issue #2049 is left untouched. This report is neither a retry nor a replacement.

## 4. Factor-space reconstruction

The relevant design space is not one undifferentiated “social influence” axis. Candidate coordinates include:

- assertion content;
- assertion count, position, and token budget;
- attribution presence;
- source identity and authority framing;
- source history or track record;
- evidence lineage, such as primary versus derivative provenance;
- claim truth and evidence validity;
- event time and recency;
- presentation order;
- reasoning form and validity;
- model/version and dataset block;
- response channel and scoring/readout;
- recognition probe placement.

These coordinates are not automatically independent. In particular, source identity/authority is conditional on attribution being present. The condition space is therefore stratified:

- **A = 0:** no explicit source attribution; identity/authority is not applicable.
- **A = 1:** attributed source, with a specified identity/authority level such as anonymous source, generic person, expert, or expert panel.

A comparison between A=0 and A=1 is not fully specified until the attributed level is named. Source history and evidence lineage are separate coordinates and must not be silently bundled into identity or authority.

### Measurement geometry

Let a condition cell be a fully specified point in this factor space. The observed outcome map sends that cell to behavioral readouts such as harmful revision, beneficial revision, target-probability shift, confidence, abstention, or answer-change direction.

A minimal pair is a directed edge between two cells that differ in one declared coordinate while the other relevant coordinates are held fixed. Its estimate is a **simple effect conditional on the fixed coordinates**.

An interaction is the difference between parallel simple-effect edges at different levels of another factor. It is not identified by one edge alone. A 2x2 crossing requires all four cells to identify the interaction without an additional assumption.

### Nested observation and aggregation map

The user's coarse-graining idea motivates a measurement hierarchy, but the following cognitive hierarchy is an assistant reconstruction:

\[
\text{trial record}
\rightarrow
\text{matched pair}
\rightarrow
\text{session/batch}
\rightarrow
\text{model/dataset stratum}
\rightarrow
\text{overall summary}.
\]

Each arrow is a projection. It may be many-to-one. A valid summary must retain the identifiers and dependence structure needed by the next analysis stage: item ID, pair ID, session, batch, model/version, dataset, condition, order, prompt hash, attempt ID, and timestamp.

Pooling rows before preserving pair and cluster membership can erase dependence. A pooled marginal rate does not determine the joint paired-outcome distribution. A single scalar residual also cannot recover several component contributions unless additional identifying conditions are supplied.

The chain is a proposed measurement map, not a claim that cognition literally has a worldtube geometry.

## 5. External source crosswalk

### 5.1 Hu and Qu: speaker-free floor

Primary source: [arXiv:2607.05545](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05545).

The study uses a deterministic two-read design and keeps the asserted answer while removing explicit speaker attribution in its speaker-free condition. It distinguishes a no-explicit-speaker floor from source framing. The expert-panel result is a particular authority-framing level, not a universal speaker-presence effect.

**Use:** supports the need to measure a speaker-free assertion floor before interpreting a source-framed condition.

**Limit:** the paper's reported aggregate does not establish that every source label, identity, authority cue, history, or lineage has the same effect.

### 5.2 AuthorityBench: truth and citation are separate factors

Primary source: [arXiv:2606.13104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13104).  
Public implementation: [floating-reeds/AuthorityBench](https://github.com/floating-reeds/AuthorityBench).

The paper describes a balanced 2x2 design crossing claim veracity and citation veracity. Those are distinct from speaker identity, source history, and evidence lineage.

A bounded public-code inspection found that the evaluator notebook's prompt includes the ground-truth claim label and citation-type label. The judge is therefore label-aware; it is not a blind independent verifier of claim truth or citation support. The public README places the dataset behind an access-controlled SharePoint link, which was not accessed. The public inference notebook's model registry lists four local models, while the paper describes seven evaluated models. This is a public-code coverage limitation; it does not by itself prove the paper's results are wrong.

**Use:** a useful factorial comparator for separating claim truth from citation truth.

**Limit:** no raw dataset reanalysis, independent citation verification, or full seven-model reproduction was performed.

### 5.3 BeliefScope: evidence and pressure

Primary source: [arXiv:2610.11305](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11305).

The available abstract/extraction describes an evidence-by-pressure design and multiple response channels. Full text, supplementary material, and code were not inspected in this pass.

**Use:** a literature lead for separating evidence and pressure.

**Limit:** abstract-level only; not load-bearing evidence for a specific numerical claim.
