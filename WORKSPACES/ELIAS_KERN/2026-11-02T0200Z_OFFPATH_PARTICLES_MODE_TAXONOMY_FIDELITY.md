## 4. Glossary

- **Trajectory curve (Γ):** A one-dimensional curve whose intersection with successive time slices supplies a kinematic trajectory representation.
- **Time slice (Σ_t):** A three-dimensional section of the four-dimensional construction at the selected time parameter.
- **Readout (ρ_t):** The projection from an intersection to the stated spatial observables.
- **Timesheet:** The geometric slice description. Its physical status as passive section or active medium is not settled by the name.
- **Bulk response:** The alternative description in which the time-associated medium has an interacting bulk response.
- **Composite mode:** A proposed coupled sheet/filament description. It requires an explicit coupling map and component projections.
- **Worldsheet:** A two-dimensional history swept out by a one-dimensional spatial string. It is not the same object as a four-dimensional finite-core region.
- **Finite-core worldtube:** A proposed four-dimensional support region. Its construction requires an explicit core/sweep map; a one-dimensional centerline alone does not supply its transverse volume.
- **Mode equivalence:** A proposed relation between representations that must be expressed through maps and preserved readouts, not inferred from similar diagrams or labels.
