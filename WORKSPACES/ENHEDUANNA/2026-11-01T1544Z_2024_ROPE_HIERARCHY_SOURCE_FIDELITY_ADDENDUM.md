### Important taxonomy correction

The direct material does **not** support a simple exclusive tree.

- “Strands” is explicitly the collective term for thread, twine, yarn, and bundle.
- Rope is a larger cohesive complex of bundles, not merely the next name in an undifferentiated list.
- Bundles and ropes can interweave across scales.
- The user-carried outline says smaller strand structures can also take on heloid, loop, or tether morphologies. Therefore those labels are better treated provisionally as morphology predicates that may apply across object scales, rather than exclusive child classes of “rope”.

The taxonomy is consequently a cross-linked composition network with morphology labels, not a proven linear nesting hierarchy.

## 4. Nathan Direct geometry, bracketed from its claimed physics

### 4.1 Filament/time-slice readout

The clearest geometric statement is:

[
F_i subset M^4,qquad Sigma_t^3subset M^4,qquad
p_i(t)=F_icapSigma_t^3.
]

Here (F_i) is a 1D filament and (Sigma_t^3) is a 3D time surface. A point readout requires a unique transverse intersection. The early conversation does not define the general curved-surface conditions, but the later Ravel formalization covers the generic dimension gate.

This is a geometric proposal for a particle readout. It is not yet a map from the full 4D history of a filament to mass, charge, spin, or a quantum state.

### 4.2 Composite hierarchy

The direct source proposes the following construction relations:

[
	ext{filaments}
longrightarrow
	ext{threads}
longrightarrow
	ext{twines}
longrightarrow
	ext{yarns}
longrightarrow
	ext{bundles}
longrightarrow
	ext{ropes}.
]

The arrows above mean the described operations of intertwining, braiding, binding, and cohesion. They are **not** yet mathematical functions. The source does not define their domains, codomains, associativity, support dimensions, or information-preservation properties.

### 4.3 Rope, heloid, planet, and accretion disk

A useful direct correction distinguishes:

- diffuse, loosely arranged cords around a star, proposed as an accretion-disk-like distribution;
- tangled cords that cohere into their own ropes around the central bundle, proposed as planet-like bodies.

The planet is therefore not merely a path or orbit in this description. It is a composite rope whose 4D morphology may be heloid-like. The assistant’s later phrase “stable orbital system” is an interpretation of the 3D manifestation, not a complete map from the 4D rope to that manifestation.

### 4.4 Time surface and structural complexity

The later direct clarification says that structural complexity comes from the interactions among filaments and their composite strands. The time surface provides the 3D manifestation of those structures. It should not be silently promoted into the cause of all complexity.

The direct source also considers mutual tugs involving the time surface and cross-temporal interactions. Those are speculative additions, not a settled interaction law. The time-surface propagation direction and the temporal support of a tug are distinct variables. A one-way ordering of slices does not, by itself, prove that every interaction has only one-sided temporal support.

### 4.5 Radial geometry and epistemic status

The radial cords and spherical expansion are introduced as simplifying assumptions. The direct source explicitly permits other geometries and calls the model a heuristic, not an assertion of the universe’s true nature.

The dark-matter statement is also conditional: direct evidence requiring a dark-matter component would force substantial revision of the proposal. It is not a direct claim that SAT has already eliminated dark matter.
