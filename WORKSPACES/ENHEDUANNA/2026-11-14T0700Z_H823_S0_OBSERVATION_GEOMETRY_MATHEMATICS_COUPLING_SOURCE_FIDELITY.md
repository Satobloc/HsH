## 3. Nathan Direct geometry and intended meaning

### Stable source-level distinctions

- **Structure:** a filament/worldtube or other specified geometric carrier.
- **Perturbation:** a distortion or mode of a structure; not a free-standing object independent of all structure.
- **Transferability:** whether a perturbation can pass between individual worldtubes. “Unbound” is relative to one worldtube, not absolute independence from every carrier.
- **Candidate perturbation domains:** worldtube, timesheet, responsive medium. The source does not establish three separate particle species or prove that the medium and timesheet are equivalent.
- **Geometric coupling:** coincidence at a point or another explicitly defined relation between represented structures.
- **Admissible variation:** changes allowed by the coupled construction. A mathematical decomposition does not automatically license independent variation.
- **Observation:** the phenomena/relations being represented.
- **Geometric representation:** a constructed account of the structure of observations.
- **Mathematical abstraction:** a formal representation of that geometry.
- **Correlated refinement:** improvement in correspondence across all three levels, not isolated increases in symbolic precision.

The source says the worldtube, timesheet and medium may be alternative, additive or equivalent descriptions. It favors a possible medium/timesheet equivalence but explicitly leaves the question to empirical discrimination. That preference is a hypothesis, not an established equivalence. The same message characterizes the discussion as crystallizing concepts previously used interchangeably, not as a new theory addition; this audit therefore treats it as clarification and source-fidelity work, not a new physical proposal.

### Geometry is part of specification

The user rejects illustrative images that do not match the defined objects and says approximate geometry is not geometry. The car/driver example identifies a concrete failure mode: a representation can reproduce much of a system's behavior while omitting a coupling needed for another operation. The intended method is to construct the geometry, inspect it, identify the actual incidence/attachment/transport relations, and only then trust equations or readouts.

This is a methodological constraint, not a new physical law. It does not establish any particular worldtube/timesheet/medium coupling.

## 4. Historical assistant mathematics versus source

and distinguishes geometrical, kinematic and dynamic coupling. It also proposes a coupling ledger for constraints, permitted variation, transfer, conservation and approximation.

**Fidelity assessment:** useful operational reconstruction, not Nathan Direct notation. The source requires a defined relation, not necessarily a scalar equation. `Φ=0` is a special encoding that may be unsuitable for inequalities, topological incidence, nonlocal relations, multi-valued relations, or relations requiring more than one constraint. The more general representation is a predicate or relation \(R\subseteq X\times Y\), with admissible configurations

\[
C_R=\{(x,y)\in X\times Y:R(x,y)\}.

Independent variation corresponds to the full product \(X\times Y\), not merely to using two variables. If \(C_R\) is a proper subset, the relation restricts admissible pairs. This is a proposed formal vocabulary; the source does not yet define the particular spaces \(X,Y\) or relation \(R\).

\[
f(x)=f(x')\Longrightarrow q(x)=q(x').
\]

This is the exact condition for a property to survive a representation that identifies multiple source states. Applied conditionally to the proposed observation → geometry → mathematics chain, a property is recoverable from the final abstraction only if the composed representation map does not collapse source cases with different values of that property.
