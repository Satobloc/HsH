## Geometry glossary and mapping

- **Centerline / filament** gamma^1: a 1D carrier.
- **Worldsheet** W^2: a 2D carrier; not interchangeable with the centerline.
- **Finite-core tube** T^4: a full-dimensional 4D region in the current tube picture.
- **Timesheet** Sigma_t^3: a 3D hypersurface/readout surface.
- **J3:** rank-3 current. A natural standard interpretation is the Poincare-dual current of a 1D carrier in 4D.
- **J2:** rank-2 current. A natural standard interpretation is the Poincare-dual current of a 2D carrier in 4D.
- **Hodge star:** in 4D, degree k maps to 4-k. Thus a 3-form maps to a 1-form; a 2-form maps to a 2-form.
- **Interior product** iota_u: a degree-lowering map; iota_u J3 is a 2-form candidate if a time-flow vector u is explicitly defined.

| Carrier | Carrier dimension | Poincare-dual current degree | Intersection with Sigma_t^3 |

| Worldsheet W^2 | 2 | 2 | 1D curve |

This gives a coherent *candidate* reading: J3 and J2 may encode different carrier dimensions, rather than being dual descriptions of the same object. The 3-form and 2-form are **not** Hodge duals in 4D. A possible projection J2 = iota_u J3 is only a proposal; the source supplies neither the required u-map nor the conservation conditions that would make the projected current equivalent to the Blockwave J2.

## Discrepancy ledger

1. **Authorship:** Page One is a pasted user-role artifact without an embedded byline. Do not call it Nathan Direct.
2. **Carrier/rank:** Blockwave J2 is described as a worldsheet current; Page One J3 is described for 1D worldlines. These are different support dimensions.
3. **Duality:** the later narrative calls the 2-form a dual/shadow of the 3-form, but no map is supplied. In 4D the Hodge degrees are 3→1 and 2→2, not 3→2.
4. **WB7 correction note:** it says a 3-form current is Hodge-dual to a pseudoscalar. That degree is wrong for a 3-form current; a 4-form is dual to a scalar. The note may conflate a 3-form potential with its 4-form field strength.
5. **Blockwave phase field:** the assistant defines a 1-form as an exact scalar gradient and then takes its exterior derivative. On a smooth single-valued patch this gives zero curvature; defects, patching or an independent connection would need specification.
6. **Page One rank-3 coupling:** F3(theta) is not defined. The 4DHHMISC transcript's gradient-dot-J3 shorthand likewise needs an explicit dualization/contraction to be well-typed.
7. **Normalization:** the same-day transcript's derivative stiffness term and Page One's displayed coefficient differ by a factor of four. Do not silently normalize them.
8. **Signature:** Page One uses Lorentzian sqrt(-g) and a unit timelike vector, while current BEDROCK distinguishes native positive-Euclidean 4D geometry from derived/effective Lorentzian descriptions. Keep Page One as a historical Lorentzian formalization.
9. **Action versus density:** the first Page One block labels an integral over d^4x as L, while the second labels the integral as S. The former is an action, not a Lagrangian density.
10. **Conservation:** the rank-2 block states a divergence constraint; the rank-3 block supplies neither a corresponding conservation law nor a derivation from the proposed projection.
