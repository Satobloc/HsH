### Conditional mathematical target

Let Section denote the physical section on a timesheet and Window the instrument's acquisition region within that timesheet. The observed support is their intersection. A candidate relative-boundary inclusion is:

\[
\partial_\Sigma(\mathrm{Section}\cap\mathrm{Window})\subseteq
(\partial_\Sigma\mathrm{Section}\cap\overline{\mathrm{Window}})
\cup
(\overline{\mathrm{Section}}\cap\partial_\Sigma\mathrm{Window}).
\]

This is a proposed formalization target, not a compiled theorem or an H(s)H equation. The inclusion separates possible section-boundary and acquisition-window contributions. Equality of smooth faces, dimensions, and corner structure requires additional regularity and clean-intersection hypotheses. A finite timesheet-patch edge must be represented separately rather than silently attributed to the physical carrier. Sweep boundaries and response-threshold edges are additional layers, not folded into this set statement.

## 3. Mask-perturbation test and falsification

Use at least two independently acquired runs of the same measured system with shifted or expanded acquisition windows and a registered overlapping physical field of view. Hold the physical model, time registration, calibration, response model, and shared parameters fixed. Calibrate on the designated calibration run; leave the other acquisition(s) untouched for holdout.

- A physical edge should remain fixed in calibrated physical coordinates.
- An acquisition-mask edge should track the changed window.
- A response-threshold edge should track the calibrated response or detection threshold.
- Re-cropping one raw acquisition is a robustness check, not an independent holdout.

The standard comparator is the established physical-boundary model plus measured mask and instrument response. An H(s)H contact or edge feature is distinguishable only if its source-defined signature remains invariant under mask perturbation and produces a preregistered residual beyond the standard predictive envelope and measured uncertainty. If physical-boundary and mask-boundary sensitivities remain indistinguishable across available windows, the edge origin is not identifiable.

| Test element | Required specification |
|---|---|
| Null comparator | Standard physical boundary plus acquisition mask and calibrated response |
| H(s)H candidate | Source-defined contact/edge map, no post-hoc edge parameter |
| Shared parameters | Physical geometry, time registration, calibration, response, mask geometry, threshold, covariance |
| Calibration/holdout | Separate acquisition runs with different windows; no holdout reuse for tuning |
| Predicted observable | Edge support, normal/curvature, time evolution, cross-channel alignment in registered coordinates |
| Falsification | H(s)H fails if the feature tracks the mask, disappears after response correction, or remains inside the standard predictive envelope. The standard comparator is challenged only by a preregistered H(s)H signature persisting across independent windows/runs beyond measured uncertainty. |

No numeric threshold is justified until raw data, calibration, units, repeat-run structure, and covariance are available.
