## 1. New result

The archive contains a plausible 4D Hodge-dual bridge between a three-form current and a one-form/vector current. That bridge is not, by itself, the missing physical derivation. The unresolved object is the **charge-weighted, conserved current generated by the finite-core worldtube**, including its support, normalization, native-to-induced metric map, and spatial profile. The current must produce both the helion electric form factor and magnetic form factor from the same geometry.

This exposes a useful type separation that older notes blur: (1) a three-form electromagnetic source, (2) its Hodge-dual vector current, (3) a gauge potential, and (4) a Blockwave two-form medium current. These are not interchangeable.

The operational test is to use the measured helion charge radius as a calibration channel and the measured magnetic moment as a held-out channel, with no independent magnetic fit parameter. This test is **defined but not executable** until the geometry-to-current map exists.

## 5. Real measured helion inputs and scripted arithmetic

### Charge-radius channel

The CREMA Collaboration measured three 2S→2P transitions in muonic helium-3 and compared the extracted Lamb shift and fine/hyperfine structure with theory. Their reported helion rms charge radius is:

\[
r_h=1.97007(94)\ \mathrm{fm}.
\]

Source: Schuhmann et al., Science 388 (2025), 854-858, published 2025-05-22, DOI 10.1126/science.adj2610. PubMed PMID 40403064. The paper reports agreement with elastic electron scattering and about 15-fold better precision. This is an experimentally extracted nuclear radius, not a direct geometric envelope measurement.

### Magnetic-moment channel

Schneider et al. measured the ground-state hyperfine structure of ³He+ in a Penning trap. The directly measured bound-ion nuclear factor is:

\[
g'_I=-4.2550996069(30)_{\rm stat}(17)_{\rm sys}.
\]

Applying the calculated diamagnetic shielding correction
\[
\sigma_{^{3}\mathrm{He}^{+}}=0.00003550738(3)
\]
gives the bare-nucleus factor:
\[
g_I=-4.2552506997(30)_{\rm stat}(17)_{\rm sys}(1)_{\rm theo}.
\]

For nuclear spin I=1/2:
\[
\frac{\mu_h}{\mu_N}=I g_I
=-2.12762534985.
\]

The corresponding uncertainty components after division by two are:
- statistical: 1.5×10⁻⁹ μ_N;
- systematic: 8.5×10⁻¹⁰ μ_N;
- shielding-theory: 5.0×10⁻¹¹ μ_N.

Quadrature gives 1.7248×10⁻⁹ μ_N only if these components are treated as independent. They remain separately reported because no cross-covariance was supplied.

Source: Schneider et al., Nature (2022), DOI 10.1038/s41586-022-04761-7, arXiv 2206.05943, submitted 2022-06-13. This is a direct ³He+ hyperfine measurement plus a theoretical shielding correction to the bare-nucleus value, not a bare helion magnetometer reading.

\[
