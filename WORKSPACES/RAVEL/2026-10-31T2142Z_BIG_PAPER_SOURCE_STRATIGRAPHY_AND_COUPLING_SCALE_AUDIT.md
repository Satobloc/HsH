- F: filament carrier, with intrinsic support dimension k kept explicit, embedding into M⁴, finite-core data, coil diameter d_c and core thickness r_c as separate quantities, plus optional tension parameter τ.
- T_t: 3D timesheet/time-bulk with embedding, thickness δ_t, normal/flow data and evolution law.

- O: observable map.
- Π: source provenance, gauge, representation and non-uniqueness metadata.

The critical type distinction:
- If J₃ is the Poincare-dual current of a 1D support in M⁴, degree 3 is appropriate.
- The Poincare dual of a full 4D region has degree 0.
- A 3D trace has a degree-1 Poincare dual.
- Therefore J₃ cannot be assigned to “the whole 4D worldtube” without first specifying which support stratum it represents.

For a transverse intersection of a smooth k-dimensional support with a zero-thickness 3D hypersurface, dim(Xᵏ ∩ Σ³) = k − 1.
