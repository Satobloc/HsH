## Namespace / notation gate
- Source Oppenheim–Sajjad couplings **α, β** are labeled here `a_OS, b_OS` (dimensionless gravitational noise couplings, paper's conventions c=ℏ=1); they are **not** the fine-structure constant nor MK171's ring parameter.
- MK171 `alpha` is labeled `phi_AB`: dimensionless ring holonomy/flux offset; original spectrum `E_n = hbar²(n+phi_AB)²/(2mR²)`.
- `N` is the ring angular momentum quantum-number operator with eigenvalues integers; `D_ring` is a phenomenological pure-momentum-dephasing rate with units inverse time (for dimensionless N). `D2_OS` is the source's dimensionless classical metric diffusion measure. **No equation equates these D's.**
- `Phi_OS` = gauge-invariant non-dynamical Newtonian potential; `psi_OS` = propagating scalar curvature metric perturbation. No identification with SAT timesheet or finite-core fields is established.

## Comparator reconstruction, equation-level
1. **CQ source model:** paper Eq (5), page 5, includes unitary quantum term, Feynman–Vernon decoherence via stress-tensor bra/ket difference, and Einstein-equation residual squared with `D2_OS^{-1}` driving classical stochastic backreaction. Complete positivity imposes diffusion–decoherence tradeoff `D0 >= (8 pi G_N)^2 D2^{-1}`; authors select saturation for their model (Sec II). This is *not* equivalent to the bare MK171 N-double-commutator.
2. **Pure gravity:** Eq (8), page 6: `S_g = (1/2) ∫ sqrt(-g) [a_OS R_{mu nu}R^{mu nu} - b_OS R²] d⁴x`. This is an **Onsager–Machlup probability action**, not a dynamical quadratic-gravity amplitude. Linearized SVT decomposition yields `Box psi_OS = xi_scalar`, `Box h_TT = xi_TT` (Eqs 47-50, p11), noise amplitudes related to a_OS and b_OS. Scalar/tensor OM operators are nonnegative under the stated source conditions. The Newtonian field `Phi_OS` diffuses around a constraint; the vector field is a constraint mode, not an ordinary independent propagating field.
3. **Important exception:** the **vector** action (29) is indefinite. Sec IV B, pp12-14, shows the corresponding weight can become non-normalizable for long times and the matter coupling can favor re-coherence (Eq 61). Authors explicitly say it remains unclear how to handle the full vector sector; Appendix C offers preferred-frame/nonlocal options. The manuscript's claimed positive dynamical subset cannot be upgraded to a full off-shell covariance or full-theory positivity theorem.
4. **Other limits:** the four-dimensional spin-2 projector's positivity itself restricts Fourier momenta to nonspacelike sectors (Sec III, pp6-7); SVT physical tensor mode is PSD. Appendix A states a generic **non-conserved** noise matrix produces a mismatch between gauge-fixed OM and MSR/JD propagators for b_OS != 0, then constructs a conserved transverse alternative via spin projectors, Eqs (A25)-(A26). The source notes formal regularization needs.
5. **Matter:** For static pressureless dust `T^{mu nu}=m(x)U^mu U^nu`, Eqs (92)-(99) integrate `psi_OS` and then `Phi_OS`. The effective decoherence kernel is **spatially nonlocal**, and without the static approximation it is generally **frequency-dependent/non-Markovian**. Markovian reduced CP kernel discussed Eqs (95)-(96) (pp21-22) is guaranteed by `3 a_OS - 8 b_OS >0` given source positivity domain. Crucially, the matter coupling is to mass density/stress-energy and metric fields; **there is no derivation of angular-momentum N-noise on a flux-threaded ring.**
6. **Numerical bounds:** Eqs (80)-(81) derive leading low-f differential-acceleration noise (source's approximations for LISA Pathfinder); Eqs (89)-(91) derive a conditional `Omega_GW proportional to f³` tensor-noise constraint in a linearly frictional FLRW background. Source explicitly warns its integrated UV energy budget can be fatal absent running/nonlinear effects (p19), and its vector kernel is unsettled. Do not report full observational verification.
7. **Independent algebra checks:** Eq (102): set `x=(a_OS-3b_OS)/a_OS>0`; `f(x)=(1+8x)(2+x)/(18x) = [2/x+17+8x]/18`, derivative zero at `x=1/2`, minimum `25/18`, as printed. The scalar/tensor ratio `(1+8x)/(3x)` equals 4 at `x=1/4`, tends to `8/3` as `x->∞`. Under source ten-component positivity `x>=1/4`, ratio in `(8/3,4]`. Check done independently.
8. **MK171 independent arithmetic:** for electron m and ideal R=100nm, `T_rev(phi_AB=0)=1.085482182 ns`, `T_rev(phi_AB=1/2)=0.542741091 ns` (independent scipy CODATA calculation). These are benchmark values, not gravity predictions.

## Strongest discriminating counterexample, explicitly calculated
MK171's phenomenological noise term `-D_ring[N,[N,rho]]` leaves every angular-momentum occupation `rho_nn` constant, even while off-diagonal coherences decay. For contrast, classical Gaussian noise coupled to a **position-dependent ring potential** `A=cos(theta)` yields the equally valid CP generator `-D_ring[A,[A,rho]]`. Starting from `rho=|n=0><n=0|`, elementary harmonic matrix elements `<+/-1|A|0>=1/2` give **dP_0/dt=-D_ring** and **dP_{+1}/dt=dP_{-1}/dt=D_ring/2** at time zero. Consequently noise does not imply N-dephasing, and the revival fidelity formula in MK171 is not generic to stochastic classical gravity. This example is a **new independent mathematical counterexample**, not claimed as the actual Oppenheim–Sajjad reduction. The comparator's density/stress-tensor couplings make local-position-like effects an essential alternative to derive and test.
