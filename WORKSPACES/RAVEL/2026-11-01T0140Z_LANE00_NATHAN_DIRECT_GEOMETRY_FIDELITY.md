## Nathan Direct geometry

### 2024 moving-plane heuristic

Nathan corrected the assistant: the trace radius is the helix's cross-sectional radius, not its pitch. Assistant turn 73ff7431 had conflated pitch-dependent traversal/speed with pitch-dependent radius; user correction aaa2a1cc restored the distinction, and assistant turn e0d27f8b accepted it. Nathan later labels this an abstraction/heuristic, not a physical ontology.

### 2025 curve and wavefront

The June/July documents use a one-parameter curve gamma: R -> M^(3,1) and a 3D resolving wavefront/timesheet. The displayed gamma is one-dimensional. Calling that curve the entire 4D finite-core worldtube is a dimension/type mismatch. It can be a centerline or reduced support, but a thickening/fiber map is absent.

The July glossary gives ell_f = (2A/T)^(1/3) and m_eff = T ell_f/(c^2 Q). These are historical glossary equations. Later direct 2026 messages reassign ell_f to coil radius/diameter scale, not filament thickness. Preserve both versions rather than silently rewriting the older source.
