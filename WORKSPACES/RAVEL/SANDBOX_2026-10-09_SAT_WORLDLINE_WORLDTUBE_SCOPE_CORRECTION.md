## Braid scope and an empirical qualification
A physical rope heuristic may motivate a *probability/stability ranking* for 2-, 3-, 4-strand patterns under a specific mechanical preparation protocol. But (i) 4-strand mathematical braids exist, (ii) geometry alone does not establish a quantum chromodynamics selection rule, and (iii) tetraquark and pentaquark candidate resonances/states have been experimentally reported (including LHCb charm-sector discoveries). Their detailed internal configurations are often debated. Therefore **do not assert that tetraquarks/pentaquarks are empirically absent**. Correct discriminators ask whether certain 4- or 5-quark configurations are suppressed, short-lived, molecular, compact, or artificially favored relative to a stated dynamical ensemble; state the QCD and evidence boundaries.
