## The explicit geometric error in #450

For three sphere surfaces of equal radius R centered at the vertices of a noncollinear equilateral triangle (side d) in the z=0 plane, subtracting pairs of the sphere equations gives two independent in-plane linear constraints. Therefore x=y=0 in circumcenter coordinates, and the remaining sphere condition gives
\[
\boxed{(x,y,z)=(0,0,\pm\sqrt{R^2-d^2/3}).}
\tag{1}
\]
For R=d=1 the two triple-common **surface points** lie at z=±sqrt(2/3)=±0.816496580927726. The source calls this scalar a common circle radius. It is instead the **absolute height of each of two points**. A common circle appears for TWO intersecting sphere surfaces with coincident radical plane, not generically three noncollinear ones. Here the pairwise common-circle radius is sqrt(R²−d²/4)=0.86602540378. Explicit substitution into all three sphere equations has zero residual.

This error invalidates any downstream area/energy calculation that assumes a 1D circle common to the three surfaces unless an entirely different center/shell geometry is explicitly defined.

## An exact correction: THREE common solid sheaths, not sphere surfaces

The **outer response/sheath support** of a candidate finite worldtube can, strictly as a 3D toy, be represented by filled equal-radius balls. Unlike three surfaces, three filled balls have a measurable common volume. Let s(z)=sqrt(R²−z²) be the radius of each planar disk section, let their centers form the same triangle, and for s>d/sqrt(3) define
\[
v(s,d)=\sqrt{s^2-d^2/4}-d/(2\sqrt3),\qquad
\theta(s,d)=2\arcsin\!\left[\frac{\sqrt3\,v}{2s}\right].
\]
A geometric sector-plus-equilateral-triangle decomposition gives the exact **three-disk** common area
\[
\boxed{A_3(s,d)=\frac{3\sqrt3}{4}v^2+\frac{3s^2}{2}(\theta-\sin\theta).}
\tag{2}
\]
Set A3=0 when s≤d/sqrt(3). This has independent controls A3(s,0)=πs² and, for s=d=1, A3=(π−sqrt3)/2=0.7047709230, the standard Reuleaux-area limit.

The simultaneous **three-ball** overlap volume is
\[
\boxed{V_3(R,d)=2\int_0^{\sqrt{R^2-d^2/3}}
A_3(\sqrt{R^2-z^2},d)\,dz,\quad 0\le d<\sqrt3 R.}
\tag{3}
\]
For d≥sqrt3 R, volume is 0. Compare exact pairwise equal-ball overlap
\[
V_2(R,d)=\frac{\pi(4R+d)(2R-d)^2}{12}\quad (0\le d<2R).
\tag{4}
\]
At R=d=1: **V3=0.6718303352064228**, **V2=1.3089969389957472** (volume units). A separate 2^19-point scrambled Sobol check gives 0.67147827 (MC error ~3.52e-4), and the d→0 control gives 4π/3.
