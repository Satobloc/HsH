## 5. Minimal representation refinement

The new source material argues for a simpler starting point, consistent with the current O_eff/WWRD control:

R = (M⁴, g, W, Σ_t, I_t, O, Π; U?)

- M⁴, g: ambient 4D geometry and declared metric/frame convention.
- W: one ordinary finite rope/tube/worldtube object, with its embedded path, intrinsic support dimension kept explicit, finite-core geometry, coil scale and core thickness kept distinct, and optional effective stiffness/tension parameter τ.
- Σ_t: the 3D time-bulk/timesheet with declared geometry and evolution.
- I_t: active instantiation/intersection/cobordism interaction between W and Σ_t.
- O: separate observable/display map.
- Π: provenance, representation and gauge bookkeeping.
- U?: optional responsive-medium state, added only when a specific physical calculation requires it.

Do not multiply primitives prematurely. The current O_eff instruction says ordinary ropes/tubes, bending, twisting, tension and fluids remain valid effective objects; split them only when a concrete failure requires it. Torsion means twist until twist is not enough. J2/J3 are representations of the carrier/interaction, not automatically separate ontological primitives.

Keep intrinsic support dimension k distinct from ambient dimension 4. The 2025 SAT4D live draft starts with a 1D curve and calls the worldtube a 4D swept volume but does not provide the explicit normal/core thickening map. The 2026 direct “4D filament” phrase does not by itself settle intrinsic support dimension. The timesheet's 3D trace therefore cannot be inferred from a bare intersection formula without the missing interaction/thickening map.

The existing 3D geometry engine can support a local analogue for W, but it does not supply the 4D extension or the Σ_t/I_t law.
