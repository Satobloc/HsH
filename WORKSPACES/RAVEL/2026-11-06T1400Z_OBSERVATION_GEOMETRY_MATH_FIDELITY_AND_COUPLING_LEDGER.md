## 3. Five distinct classification axes

The source material contains multiple three-part classifications. They are different axes, not synonyms.

| Axis | Members | What it classifies | What remains open |
|---|---|---|---|
| Epistemic layer | Observation / geometric representation / mathematical abstraction | How a description is built and checked | The two fidelity maps and their uncertainty propagation |
| Geometric view | Reduced Minkowski diagram / full 4D bulk / 4D view with 3D slice | Different views or reductions of a proposed construction | Projection, embedding, slicing, and reconstruction maps |
| Dynamic allocation | Dynamic-block / worldline-only / mutually distributed | Where a chosen representation places the dynamics | Whether the representations are equivalent and under what conditions |
| Perturbation carrier | Worldtube / timesheet or timefront / responsive medium | Where a perturbation may be carried or propagated | Whether medium is substitute, addition, or equivalent description of timesheet |
| Perturbation morphology | Surface ripple / propagating structural loop or twist / persistent coil | The geometrical form of a disturbance | Exact objects, propagation maps, and boson/fermion correspondence |

Do not confuse a carrier with a morphology, a geometric view with an epistemic layer, or a dynamic allocation with a physical mechanism.

The direct source says a boson is a distortion of filamental structure and may be transferable between worldtubes; it does not establish a unique shell mode, a unique transfer law, or a final mapping of all three morphologies to boson/fermion categories.

## 6. Discrepancy ledger

1. **Ontology substituted for method.** The assistant initially introduced an ontological distinction where the direct source specified an epistemic/representational sequence. The user corrected it; the assistant acknowledged the substitution.
2. **Mathematical separability can manufacture false independence.** Separate fields or variables are acceptable only if the observed coupling relation remains explicit. The assistant's `Φ=0` is one proposed encoding, not a universal source law.
3. **Multiple triads were at risk of conflation.** Epistemic layers, geometric views, dynamic allocations, perturbation carriers, and perturbation morphologies must remain separate.
4. **Image search was not geometry construction.** The three assistant-selected images failed to represent the stated configurations. A diagram must be generated from explicit objects and relationships and inspected against them.
5. **Set intersection is not automatically a physical readout.** The historical `W∩Σ_τ` construction is a possible mathematical abstraction. It does not establish the active response of a timefront or responsive medium.
6. **The Kerr product ansatz adds structure.** `Σ_Kerr × R_τ` assumes a selected spatial section and a product/trivialization along a parameter. The direct source says to choose a spatial Kerr section and extend it along the filament; it does not specify the full product structure, orthogonal slicing, or global readout.
7. **The old force decomposition is too specific if treated as settled.** The direct source was qualitative; later direct material favors mutual curvature as a working representation while keeping worldline-only and timesheet-only alternatives open. No force law, conservation law, or mode equation follows from the wording alone.
8. **Symmetric attribution is conditional.** It is a default absent evidence of preferential role. Topology or other structural evidence can imply a non-equidistributed case.
9. **Branch ancestry matters.** The off-current-ancestry 01:54 user message was not merged into the active 02:07 source chain.
10. **Assistant labels are not automatically canonical.** “Reality-Approximation Principle,” `Φ=0`, the three coupling levels, and the displaced-driver test remain assistant-origin proposals unless explicitly adopted.
