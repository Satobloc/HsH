The later formal reconstruction in MovingPlaneTraceGeometry.lean uses the standard pair y=±z². For that specific reconstruction:

\[
\Delta(z)=y_+(z)-y_-(z)=2z^2.
\]

Under a constant-speed z-slice \(z(t)=z_0+vt\), the exact secant rate is:

\[
\frac{\Delta(t_2)-\Delta(t_1)}{t_2-t_1}
=2v\left(2z_0+v(t_1+t_2)\right).
\]

For the normalized mathematical witness \(z(t)=1-t\), the first two half-interval secant rates are -3 and -1. They differ. Thus this particular parabolic reconstruction does not produce steady-rate approach to the origin. The exit side is likewise nonuniform.

This corrects the assistant-side reconstruction, not Nathan's direct proposal. The original proposal remains under-specified until the parabola parameterization, moving-slice direction and rate criterion are fixed.
