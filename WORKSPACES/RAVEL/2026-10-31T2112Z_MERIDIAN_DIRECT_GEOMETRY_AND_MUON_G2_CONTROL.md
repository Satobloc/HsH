### Field and force
From the measured noRF muon-weighted NMR frequency:

- B = 1.4512955803 T.
- Table-derived relative uncertainty, including the CODATA gyromagnetic-ratio uncertainty: 56.783 ppb.
- For a positive muon on the reference orbit, F_lab = e beta c B = 6.9668086×10⁻¹¹ N.
- Field-only partial uncertainty: 3.96×10⁻¹⁸ N.

The report prose separately states total field uncertainty of 48-51 ppb. Table XXVIII's 3.5 Hz on 61.790920 MHz implies 56.8 ppb. This precision discrepancy is unresolved; the table-derived 56.8 ppb is carried conservatively for the partial force uncertainty.

Using rounded p0 and R0, p0 v/R0 = 6.9660384×10⁻¹¹ N, about 110.6 ppm below e beta c B. The implied radius is 7.111214 m, 0.786 mm below the rounded 7.112 m reference. This is a rounded-reference consistency check, not a physical residual or a 127 ppb test.

### Coordinate versus intrinsic curvature

For uniform circular motion in the Minkowski control:

- spatial path curvature: 1/R0 = 0.1406074 m⁻¹;
- coordinate second-derivative magnitude with w=ct: beta²/R0 = 0.1404436 m⁻¹;
- intrinsic Minkowski proper-time worldline curvature: gamma² beta²/R0 = 120.5695 m⁻¹;
- proper curvature radius: 8.294 mm;
- ratio of intrinsic to coordinate curvature: gamma² = 858.49;
- cyclotron frequency from the reference orbit: 6.704957 MHz.

At low velocity, gamma → 1 and the coordinate and intrinsic curvature measures converge. At the E989 magic gamma they differ by a factor of 858.49. This quantitatively preserves the direct user's warning that d²x/dw² is not generally the intrinsic four-dimensional curvature.
