## 5. Statistical architecture improvement

The article defines C_2 from each vortex's two nearest neighbors. The 2N_v directed neighbor relations are not independent observations. The independent sampling unit must be the experimental realization/shot, and the paper's 15-20 measurements per point must be confirmed as independent preparations rather than assumed independent from the caption alone.

The source-specific readout has a potential classification sensitivity: the Bragg signal can be weak for close vortices and absent for a small dipole because the surrounding velocity field nearly cancels. In that case the authors use the crescent shape of merged density-depleted cores to assign signs. This may be valid, but the fallback morphology rule is invoked in the exact close-pair regime whose prevalence is being measured. Its sign-error rate and dependence on pair geometry must be calibrated.

The proposed reanalysis is:
1. Preserve each shot's vortex positions and its N_+, N_- counts.
2. Permute signs over the fixed positions within each shot.
3. Recompute C_2, dipole fraction, and cluster fraction jointly for each permutation.
4. Preserve shot identity and time-point structure; estimate uncertainty at the realization level, not per neighbor edge.
5. Calibrate the Bragg-derivative sign rule using known-sign isolated vortices and prepared dipoles; keep high-confidence Bragg-only and morphology-fallback assignments distinguishable.
6. Recompute the joint statistics under calibrated sign-error rates and conservative relabeling bounds.
7. Verify whether the 15-20 measurements are independent preparations or repeated measures before choosing the bootstrap/block structure.

The published 0.5 reference is a balanced-random benchmark. The reanalysis should condition on each shot's actual sign counts rather than silently assume exact balance. No p-value, confidence interval, covariance, or revised C_2 estimate is claimed without the raw shot catalog.
