### Citation correction

The previously used ((11.2pm0.2)M_c(	ildeLambda/800)^{1/6},mathrm{km}) expression is **not Eq. (6)** of Raithel et al. Eq. (6) is the Newtonian single-star tidal-deformability expression. The quasi-Newtonian common-radius relation is developed in Eqs. (9)-(12): Eq. (10) is the equal-mass/equal-radius relation; Eq. (9) supplies the second-order mass-ratio correction. The paper discusses the q expansion around approximately 0.7-1.0, not q >= 0.6.

The previously stated “about 2% model uncertainty” is not supported by this citation. The paper's 6.5% accuracy statement concerns the Yagi-Yunes compactness relation, not a calibrated 2% radius error. The q correction is radius-dependent; at R=13 km and q=0.7, Table 1 gives about 1.4% in (	ildeLambda).
