The problem is not that these ideas are impossible. The problem is that no map is supplied:

The assistant's later treatment of the worldsheet as a reduced history surface is useful if a projection or quotient is eventually specified. It does not establish that the worldsheet is the tube-timesheet intersection or the tube boundary.
