### B. Phase field and number of timesheets

The assistant proposes
\[
\Phi(\mathbf x,w;\lambda)=nw-\omega\lambda+\psi(\mathbf x)\pmod{2\pi},
\qquad
\Sigma_\phi(\lambda)=\{p:\Phi(p,\lambda)=\phi\pmod{2\pi}\}.
\]
This is a proposal, not a direct equation. Global single-valuedness requires \(n\in\mathbb Z\), and \(\psi\)'s codomain/global status must be stated.

**Conditional proposition:** let \(X\) be connected and simply connected (e.g. \(S^3\) or \(B^3\)); \(w\in S^1\); \(n\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}\); and smooth \(\psi:X\to S^1\). Since \(X\) is simply connected, \(\psi\) lifts to \(\widetilde\psi:X\to\mathbb R\). For fixed \(\phi,\lambda\), the level equation has branches
\[
w_k(x)=\frac{\phi+\omega\lambda-\widetilde\psi(x)+2\pi k}{n}\pmod{2\pi},
\quad k=0,\ldots,|n|-1.
\]
Thus the regular level set is a disjoint union of \(|n|\) graph sections over \(X\). It is one connected graph only for \(n=\pm1\). For \(|n|>1\), a single physical timesheet requires a quotient, branch-selection rule, or an interpretation of multiple sheets.

This extends the Oct 22 degree gate by identifying connected components over a simply connected 3D base. If the global space is \(M_Q\), the phase must also satisfy seam/monodromy compatibility; the product result does not establish that.
