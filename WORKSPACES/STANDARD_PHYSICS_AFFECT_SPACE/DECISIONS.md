## D-002 | Conventional physics for this thought exercise | 2026-11-21

Use conventional physics and standard physical vocabulary. This project is not a vehicle for H(s)H/SAT theory development. Scientific statements remain open to ordinary source checking and model qualification.

## D-003 | Simple-first scope; reject carbon-cycle expansion | 2026-11-21

The first mapping should have one bounded entity/history. Exclude biosphere passage and the full carbon cycle. Do not substitute population averages for the path of one selected entity.

## D-004 | No assumed affect-space morphology | 2026-11-21

"Dendritic" was a provisional placeholder, not a structural hypothesis. Do not assume branching, convergence, connectedness, or any other geometry before mapping.

## D-005 | Move to a mesoscopic specimen: one chondrule | 2026-11-21

Replace the C-14 nucleus with one representative chondrule in the early Solar System. Track it from its chondrule-forming heating/melting episode through cooling/crystallization and disk transport to incorporation into a small, primitive, undifferentiated planetesimal. This scale is more involved than a molecular-cloud-only account while avoiding atmospheres, biosphere, and global carbon-cycle complexity. This is a class-level representative path, not a claim to know one individual chondrule's exact trajectory or heating mechanism.

## D-006 | Stop at incorporation into the small rocky body | 2026-11-21

For the first pass, the chondrule's mesoscopic record ends at accretion into the planetesimal. Exclude later impacts, disruption, meteorite delivery, terrestrial weathering, atmosphere, and biosphere.
