### 4.1 Measurement chain

The paper describes CW NMR excitation, magnetic-field sweep, a lock-in NMR signal and parallel digital-oscilloscope time series. Fourier spectrograms expose symmetric sidebands around the NMR carrier. Extracted mode frequencies Ω are tracked against frequency shift δω, temperature, field gradient and RF amplitude. Some modes are interpreted as oscillations of ϑ-solitons.

The standard physical comparator is nonlinear Leggett spin/order-parameter dynamics, Leggett–Takagi relaxation for the HPD steady state, and numerical one-dimensional ϑ-soliton simulations. The reported low-temperature relation is:

`\Omega^2 = S_{LT}(\delta\omega - \delta\omega_0)`

For two common high-temperature modes, the paper proposes a circular-membrane radial-mode interpretation:

`\Delta_n = a_n C/r`

with Bessel zeros (a_1=2.405), (a_2=5.520), giving (a_2/a_1\approx2.295). The measured ratio is reported as close to this value. The membrane is a model interpretation, not a directly imaged membrane. Modes 3 and 4 are not confirmed; modes 7 and 8 have insufficient visibility for confident classification.

### 4.2 Physical conditions and uncertainty

The paper reports 25.7 bar, 34.67 mT, a cylindrical cell 7.8 mm in diameter and 9 mm long, field homogeneity (1.5\times10^{-4}) (about 5 μT variation across the cell), gradients from 0 to 20 μT/cm, RF amplitudes including 238, 297 and 357 nT, and a field sweep rate of 0.807 μT/s.

Important limitations:
- Temperature correction/thermometry accuracy is about 0.1 (T_c); the sample overheats by about 0.2 (T_c) under strong excitation.
- Field inhomogeneity limits absolute δω calibration; relative changes are described as accurate.
- The actual field profile and HPD growth are not fully defined.
- The high-temperature localization point is assumed when assigning the frequency-shift origin.
- Modes 3/4 are not confirmed; modes 7/8 have insufficient visibility.

The approximately 10 μm dipolar core and 0.15–0.5 mm magnetization length are 3He-B quantities, not H(s)H worldtube thicknesses.

## 5. H(s)H bridge and the new architecture

### 5.1 Candidate-admission vector

The substantive architecture improvement is to stop collapsing candidate suitability into one score. Track five independent axes:

- **D — data access:** metadata, raw bytes, file integrity, calibration records.
- **G — geometric observability:** direct spatial measurement, inferred geometry, or spectral-only observables.
- **M — model-map closure:** source-defined map from H(s)H state to physical state and instrument readout.
- **U — uncertainty closure:** units, calibration, covariance, nuisance parameters and dependence structure.
- **C — comparator closure:** standard model, shared parameters, frozen calibration, holdout and pre-registered rejection rule.

Admission for a particular claim requires M, U and C to close, with D and G adequate for that claim. Public data access and geometric observability are orthogonal. An open dataset can be geometrically indirect; a spatially rich dataset can be inaccessible.

Applied comparatively:
- **HPD 3He-B:** promising for temporal soliton-mode spectra, public/open raw-data metadata, but indirect geometry and no H(s)H state/readout map.
- **Han et al. (2026) vortex ring:** synchronized stereoscopic surface measurement and 4D-PTV give richer spatial geometry, but Springer states supporting data are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request. It is a different measurement problem, not a substitute for HPD spectroscopy.
- **GW190521:** existing Hale polarization/readout architecture already covers this lane; not reopened.

### 5.2 Proposed HPD test contract if maps are supplied

- **Hypothesis:** a source-frozen H(s)H map predicts a definite joint distribution of sideband frequencies and mode-appearance boundaries as functions of δω, (T), ∇(B) and (B_{RF}). No independent per-mode/per-cycle fit parameters may be introduced after seeing the holdout.
- **Shared parameters:** magnetic-field/gradient calibration, RF amplitude, cell geometry, instrument transfer function, temperature correction and any standard-model relaxation parameters.
- **Calibration:** use the paper's normal-state NMR field/gradient calibration and obtain underlying calibration records; freeze nuisance parameters before comparing theories.
- **Holdout:** reserve entire demagnetization/warm-up cycles and distinct RF/gradient settings. Do not randomly split adjacent time windows or treat all ~600 measurements as independent before establishing cycle/run dependence.
- **Predicted observables:** raw time-domain NMR/oscilloscope signal, sideband spectrum, Ω(δω), low-temperature slope/offset, high-temperature gaps and mode-appearance boundaries.
- **Standard comparator:** Leggett dynamics plus Leggett–Takagi relaxation and published numerical 1D soliton-mode simulation; evaluate the circular-membrane/Bessel interpretation separately rather than silently treating it as directly observed.
- **Falsification rule:** after maps, covariance and holdout are frozen, reject a model if held-out joint observables fall outside a pre-registered 99% posterior-predictive region while the competing model remains inside. No numerical boundary is claimed until raw records and the full uncertainty/covariance model are obtained.
