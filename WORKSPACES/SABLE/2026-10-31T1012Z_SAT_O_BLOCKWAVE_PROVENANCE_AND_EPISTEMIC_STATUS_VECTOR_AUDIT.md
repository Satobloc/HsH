## 3. Minimal support-stratified interpretation

Under standard Poincare-dual bookkeeping in four dimensions:

`deg(PD(X^k)) = 4-k`  
`dim(X^k intersect Sigma^3) = k-1` for transverse intersection.

| Geometric support | Intrinsic support dimension | Poincare-dual form degree | Transverse intersection with a 3D timesheet |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Filament/worldline C1 | 1 | 3 | 0D point |
| Swept worldsheet W2 | 2 | 2 | 1D curve/loop |
| Finite-core 4D region K4 | 4 | 0 | 3D section |

This gives a plausible way for J3 and J2 to coexist without equating them:

- J3 may encode a one-dimensional filament/worldline support.
- J2 may encode a two-dimensional worldsheet or sweep.
- A regular worldsheet boundary may return a one-dimensional support, raising the Poincare-dual degree from 2 to 3.
- An independently parameterized sweep of a one-dimensional support may produce a two-dimensional worldsheet, lowering the Poincare-dual degree from 3 to 2.

These are different geometric maps, not Hodge duality. The actual sweep parameter, boundary conditions, orientation, regularity, core structure and physical interpretation are not specified by the source. A closed worldsheet has no boundary current unless additional structure is supplied.

A candidate minimal typed representation is:

`R_min = (M4, g_E, g_L?, C1, W2, K4, Sigma3, U?, O, Gamma, Pi)`

where g_E is the native positive Euclidean ambient metric, g_L? is an explicitly derived Lorentzian representation if justified, C1/W2/K4 are distinct support strata, Sigma3 is the timesheet, U? is the optional responsive-medium state, O is the readout, Gamma is the labelled interaction/composition graph, and Pi is source/representation provenance. This is a candidate interface only, not a promoted equation or adopted ontology.

The equation-to-geometry inverse is not unique. The Blockwave file constructs J2 from a one-form potential B; the stated gauge B -> B + dLambda leaves J2 unchanged. The current alone also does not uniquely recover an embedded support without orientation, multiplicity, regularity and support data. ## 4. Conditional mathematical audit of the Blockwave action

The recovered attachment defines:

`A_mu(theta) = alpha_* nabla_mu theta`  
`F_munu(theta) = nabla_[mu A_nu](theta)`

If alpha_* is constant, theta is C2 on the patch, and nabla is a torsion-free connection with symmetric connection coefficients, then the antisymmetric covariant Hessian vanishes and therefore F=0 locally.

The existing compiled Hessian theorem in PR #1135 covers the conditional core identity. This application does not establish that the source's assumptions hold physically. A nonzero local F term would require a stated defect/singularity prescription, a non-exact connection, nonconstant alpha_*, torsion, or other explicit structure. The attachment does not specify which is intended.

A nonzero global period of a closed phase 1-form can still occur on a nontrivial loop. Global winding/holonomy and local curvature are different invariants. The attachment uses both, but does not explain how the global period enters the local F_munu J^munu action term. This is a conditional missing-link finding, not a refutation of the whole Blockwave proposal.
