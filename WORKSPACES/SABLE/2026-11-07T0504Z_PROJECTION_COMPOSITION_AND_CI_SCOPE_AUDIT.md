## 3. Compositional map contract

No additional abstract theorem is needed before the physical/source maps exist. The current reusable contract is:

1. **Type every node:** declare domain, codomain, dimension, topology, regularity, support, orientation, metric/signature, units, admissible states and boundary conditions.
2. **Name every edge:** identify the actual map (embedding/thickening, intersection/pullback, slice transfer, coarse-graining, representation projection, observer/readout). Do not replace an absent map with an analogy.
3. **Declare composition:** for adjacent maps (A\xrightarrow{f}B\xrightarrow{g}C), identify (g\circ f), its validity domain, and preserved quantities.
4. **Require commuting diagrams:** if two routes from (A) to (C) are claimed equivalent, state the equality and its conditions. A slice map must commute with the ambient map; a coarse-graining map must preserve the declared readout or carry an explicit correction.
5. **Test fibers and information loss:** a downstream map cannot recover distinctions collapsed upstream. A proposed factor map exists only when the target map is constant on each fiber of the source projection.
6. **For isomorphism, require both directions:** maps and inverse, identity compositions, and preservation of the declared structures (not merely a common parent or matching notation).
7. **Connect to an observer:** the complete source-to-observable composition must be defined before an H(s)H response can be compared with standard nuisance responses.
