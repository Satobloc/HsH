## 4. Geometry and representation map

Let \(\Gamma=\{\gamma_i\}\) denote a candidate ensemble of worldlines. The December source leaves at least three representational possibilities open:

~~~text
A. TIMESHEET AS AN INDEPENDENT STRUCTURE

        Γ = {γ_i}  <---- interaction / backreaction ---->  Σ_t
             \                                           /
              \------------- readout R ----------------/

B. TIMESHEET AS A DERIVED AVERAGE FRONT

        Γ = {γ_i}  ---- T (synchronization, measure,
                           averaging, foliation) ---->  Σ_t

C. GRAVITY AS A COLLECTIVE CORRELATION RESPONSE

        Γ = {γ_i} ---- E (defined correlation/
                          entanglement object) ---->  E(Γ)
                    ---- C (coarse-graining) ---->  G_eff
                                                   (force / curvature)

CURRENT H(s)H WORKING MODEL

        W^4 (finite-core worldtube)  <---- relation open ---->  Σ^3
        responsive medium: provisional candidate, not required primitive
~~~

These are **candidate representations**, not equivalent theories.

### Minimum maps required

1. **Derived-timesheet map:** \(\mathcal T:\Gamma\rightarrow\Sigma\). It needs a common parameter or synchronization rule, an ensemble measure, an averaging/front construction, regularity, causal orientation, and conditions under which the result is a 3D hypersurface or foliation.
2. **Collective-correlation map:** \(\mathcal E:\Gamma\rightarrow\mathcal E(\Gamma)\). It needs a typed state/correlation object and a defined meaning of “statistical tug”; entanglement entropy, mutual information, pair correlations, and an effective force are not interchangeable by vocabulary.
3. **Gravitational-response map:** \(\mathcal C:\mathcal E(\Gamma)\rightarrow G_{\mathrm{eff}}\). It must specify how the coarse-grained object produces a metric, curvature, stress-energy contribution, or force law, and how that result compares with the Minkowski/GR baseline.
4. **Observable-preservation map:** for a claimed reduction \(O_\Gamma=\bar O_\Sigma\circ\mathcal T\), the observable \(O_\Gamma\) must be constant on every fiber of \(\mathcal T\). Otherwise the timesheet loses information required by that observable.

The 2025 messages do not define these maps. The November 2026 working position is more disciplined: mutual worldtube–timesheet curvature is a **working hypothesis**, while worldline-only and timesheet-only descriptions remain possible representations. The October 21 direct correction retired the earlier reciprocal filament/time-surface deformation account after the Klein-Fabric model became common; the November 4 position is not a silent revival of that retired model. See the [source-locator correction to #1554](https://github.com/Satobloc/HsH/blob/main/WORKSPACES/COMMON/PUBLISHED_SUBMISSIONS/nathan-direct-worldtube-timesheet-source-recovery-20261110t1100z-s0-001.md).
