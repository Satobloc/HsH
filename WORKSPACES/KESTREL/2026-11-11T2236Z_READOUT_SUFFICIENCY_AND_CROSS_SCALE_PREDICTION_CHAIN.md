## 3. Deterministic readout-sufficiency criterion

For maps

\[
P_0:X_0\to Y_0,\quad C_0:X_0\to X_1,\quad P_1:X_1\to Y_1,
\]

a deterministic induced map \(D_0:P_0(X_0)\to P_1(X_1)\) satisfying \(D_0(P_0(x))=P_1(C_0(x))\) exists iff

\[
P_0(x)=P_0(x')\Rightarrow P_1(C_0(x))=P_1(C_0(x'))
\]

for all \(x,x'\in X_0\).

The downstream quantity must be constant on every fibre of the upstream readout. If not, it is not a deterministic function of that readout alone. The choices are to measure an additional state variable, refine the readout, or specify a probabilistic conditional model. Failure does not establish a new hidden physical primitive and does not license a post-hoc free variable.

For two stages, the existing witness-carrying composition result requires each stage to satisfy its own fibre-constancy condition; the intermediate image witness makes the second map well-typed.

This is an abstract map criterion, not a physical construction, not a new incorporation tier, and not Factory Floor admission.
