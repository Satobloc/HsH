## 3. Substantive architecture improvement: response-locus-preserving coarse closure

Three prior results are deliberately kept distinct:

1. **Kestrel h785 / issue #1644:** a response-locus axis distinguishes path-neighbourhood response, response distributed through the active timesheet bulk, and response at a support boundary/contact interface. It is independent of geometric thin-core/centerline reduction and of whether a response is null, absorbable, or irreducible.
2. **Kestrel h800:** support boundary, timesheet-section boundary, and contact interface are distinct typed roles; the early 4D construction is historically described as an abstraction/heuristic, not an assertion of ultimate ontology.
3. **Kestrel h732 / Nadir PR #1189:** a deterministic induced coarse map exists exactly when the target composite is constant on every fibre of the source projection. A fibre mismatch is an obstruction, not a license to invent a memory kernel.

The new synthesis is a **joint-output sufficiency gate**. It asks whether a proposed reduced state preserves every response channel and limiting readout that the model claims to explain, rather than testing one scalar output and silently discarding the rest.

### 3.1 Candidate representation ladder

Let \(X_f\) denote a future source-defined fine state. The following are proposed map slots, **not yet instantiated physical maps**:

- \(q_0:X_f\to X_0\): the minimal worldtube/worldline plus timesheet representation.
- \(q_1:X_f\to X_1\): the pair plus source-defined response history/internal state that could be represented by an elaborated timesheet. This is not automatically a new primitive.
- \(q_2:X_f\to X_2\): the pair plus an independently typed responsive-medium state \(M\), the provisional third-primitive alternative.

A genuine hierarchy requires explicit forgetful maps \(p_{10}:X_1\to X_0\) and \(p_{21}:X_2\to X_1\), with:
\[
q_0=p_{10}\circ q_1,\qquad q_1=p_{21}\circ q_2,
\]
and therefore:
\[
q_0=(p_{10}\circ p_{21})\circ q_2.
\]
These equations state the required composition contract. They do not claim that the maps or state spaces have already been derived from SAT-O, Blockwave, or a measured physical system.

### 3.2 Joint target, keeping formerly conflated loci separate

The target must be a tuple, not one undifferentiated "surface response":
\[
O=(O_{\rm path},O_{\rm bulk},O_{\rm support},O_{\rm section},
O_{\rm contact},O_{\rm thin},O_{\rm null}).
\]

The components denote, respectively:
- response in the path-neighbourhood;
- response distributed through the timesheet bulk;
- response at the worldtube/support boundary;
- the timesheet-section boundary readout;
- response at a genuine contact interface;
- the thin-core/centerline limiting readout;
- the response-null limiting readout.

These are target-channel slots, not asserted fields or measured quantities. In particular, a support boundary is not automatically a contact interface, and a timesheet-section rim is not automatically a material contact. The thin-core limit and response-null limit remain independent.

A response-locus label is a classification of where a declared response is read out. It is not itself a new physical field or ontology. Each component needs an operational definition and source/measurement map before use.

### 3.3 Conditional admission rule

For each candidate representation \(q_k\), ask whether there exists a deterministic reduced readout \(\bar O_k\) such that:
\[
O=\bar O_k\circ q_k
\]
on the declared reachable domain.

By the existing fibrewise factorization theorem, this holds exactly when:
\[
q_k(x)=q_k(y)\ \Longrightarrow\ O(x)=O(y)
\]
for every admissible pair \(x,y\) in that domain.

This gives a disciplined primitive-admission sequence:

| Result | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| \(O\) factors through \(q_0\) | The two-primitive representation is sufficient for this declared output set; a third primitive is not required for these outputs. |
| \(q_0\) fails, but \(O\) factors through \(q_1\) | The base pair omitted response history/internal state; an elaborated pair representation suffices. A separate medium is not forced. |
| \(q_1\) fails, but \(O\) factors through \(q_2\) | A separately typed medium is required **within this declared model family and output domain**, provided \(M\) is source-defined or independently measurable and not an unconstrained fit variable. |
| \(q_2\) also fails | The candidate family or declared output set is inadequate. Do not automatically add another free field; recover the source-defined missing state or reject the proposed reduction. |

A failure of \(q_0\) alone does **not** establish a third primitive. It establishes only that the chosen \(q_0\) projection loses information needed by at least one target component. The result is conditional on the admissible state spaces, maps, output domain, and measurement resolution.

This is the missing bridge between the response-locus axis and the existing coarse-graining theorem: a reduced representation must preserve the *joint* response-locus and limit tuple, not merely one selected component.
