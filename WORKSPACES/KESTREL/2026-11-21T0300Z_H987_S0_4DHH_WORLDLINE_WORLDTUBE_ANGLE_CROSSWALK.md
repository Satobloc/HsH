## Executive synthesis

The most faithful compact reading is:

1. **One carrier with nested internal morphology** is the June 23 direct proposal: a 4D helical worldline/worldtube, super-nested, with features changing across layers.
2. **Layer scaling and interaction coupling are different unknowns.** The scaling factor's placement between nested levels is explicitly unresolved. The coupling factor varies per interaction when more than one H(s)H structure is involved.
3. **Superhelical nesting and braid nesting are distinct.** The July 11 direct source says so explicitly.
4. **The near-light-speed 45° picture is a separate historical kinematic interpretation.** It is not identified in the source as the current theta4, and it is not yet a coordinate-invariant geometric proposition.
5. **The current finite-core worldtube baseline is a later refinement, not a license to backfill missing maps.** The 1D centerline, finite-core 4D support, 3D timesheet section, and readout still require explicit transformations.

The new reconciliation is not a new equation. It is a separation of three different operations that had been easy to blur: recursive nesting within one carrier, coupling between multiple carriers, and kinematic deformation of a carrier relative to a time-surface frame.
