The first thirteen NATHAN-labelled blocks contain the main particle/black-hole geometry. The remaining blocks turn largely to methodological and archival instructions. The following is a faithful summary of the labelled text, not an authenticated raw-message attribution.

2. **Black-hole side:** a spinning disk is described around a spherical event horizon. The horizon is compared with an inverse geodesic array, while a perpendicular disk is compared with a CSC array. The speaker explicitly qualifies the description as not yet fully accurate.
3. **Causal requirement:** the labelled speaker says that without CTCs there is no model, and associates the global construction with toroidal or Klein-like topology and a Kerr-related CSC. No Lorentzian metric, time orientation, or timelike tangent field is supplied in the transcript.
4. **Inversion:** the direct phrase is that the particle/black-hole relationship may be "literally inversion", later qualified as either inverse objects or different scale versions. No domain, codomain, metric, or explicit transformation is supplied.
5. **Circumference ratio:** the ratio of inner to outer circumference is proposed as a representation of an equation-of-state quantity. The source does not define a density functional or derive an equation of state.
6. **Planck scale:** the inner circumference is described as minimal at the Planck length, with the wording subsequently clarified to a circumference of 2 pi Planck lengths and an association with Planck mass. The statement remains qualitative and ambiguous about the outer scale.
7. **Untwisting:** gauge-symmetry collapse and final untwisting near the neck/horizon are proposed qualitatively. The relation to Einsteinian length contraction is asserted descriptively, not as a defined map.
8. **Alternative topology sketches:** a torus inner circumference and tube circumferences, two linked rings, and a crossed figure-eight arrangement are explored. These are distinct candidate configurations, not one established topology.

## Specific fidelity discrepancies

1. **CTC topology is not causal geometry.** The fundamental group of a torus or Klein bottle does not by itself supply a Lorentzian metric or a closed timelike curve. A CTC claim needs a metric, time orientation, and a closed curve whose tangent is timelike.
2. **CSC is not yet Kerr's ring singularity.** The transcript uses a Kerr-related comparison, but the acronym, locus, and exact identification are unresolved. Do not promote the analogy to an identity.
3. **Point inversion does not establish ring-to-sphere duality.** Euclidean point inversion maps a circle to a circle or line, not a one-dimensional circle to a two-dimensional sphere. The Lorentzian expression with denominator x^mu x_mu is undefined on nonzero null vectors, so it is not a globally defined map on Minkowski space.
4. **The assistant's radius-swap is a different map.** The proposed (R,a) -> (L^2/a,L^2/R) is a separate two-scalar construction. It is not derived from the point-inversion map and has no direct-source authorization.
5. **Torus intersection and Hopf linking are different.** A meridian and a parallel on a torus intersect. A Hopf link consists of disjoint linked circles. The transcript explores both pictures, so they must not be silently combined.
6. **A circumference ratio is not an equation of state.** An EoS requires a specified energy density, pressure, conservation law, and relation to the geometric variable. Those maps are absent.
7. **The Planck/Compton identity is conditional.** If one assumes C_in=2 pi ell_P, C_out=2 pi hbar/(m c), and m_P ell_P=hbar/c, then C_in/C_out=m/m_P. The algebra is valid under those assumptions. The outer Compton circumference is assistant-added, so the identity is not a source-derived physical law.
8. **Rapidity is not automatically horizon untwisting.** L=L0 sech(chi) is the standard Lorentz-contraction expression for a boost parameterized by rapidity. It does not establish that a Kerr horizon is reached by an infinite internal twist parameter or that the source's rotation is a Lorentz boost.
9. **The historical support types differ.** SAT-O1 starts with a one-dimensional filament curve and describes a four-dimensional volume swept by vibration. Blockwave introduces a conserved rank-two current. The current BEDROCK finite-core/worldtube language is not automatically either construction.
