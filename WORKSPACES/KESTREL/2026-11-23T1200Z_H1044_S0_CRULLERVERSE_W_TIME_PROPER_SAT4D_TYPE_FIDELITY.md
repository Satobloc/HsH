## Executive finding

The new source-level distinction is not simply “Minkowski versus Euclidean.” It is **Minkowski as a geometric comparison lens versus Minkowski as the ontology and metric of the model**.

A September 21, 2026 Nathan Direct message recommends applying Minkowski geometry, discarding imported labels, and looking at geometry. A November 8, 2026 raw user-role message separately specifies a preferred four-dimensional spatial Crullerverse, with w as its fourth spatial direction and time proper distinct from w. The latter message tentatively places particle worldtubes roughly parallel to a toroidal circumference C and preserves three different possibilities for how a Kerr-ER mouth relates to a timesheet.

Those propositions do not authorize silently identifying the Crullerverse with the historical Lorentzian space \(\mathbb R^{3,1}\). The 2025 SAT4D/SAT-O1 equations and the Blockwave attachment are candidate historical representations. They use a Lorentzian metric, a timelike unit vector, and/or a two-dimensional worldsheet current. They are not source-equivalent to the later four-spatial-dimensional description unless explicit coordinate, metric, thickening, projection, and composition maps are supplied.

Existing Lean work already proves several useful conditional statements. The relevant exact-PR-head CI build succeeded. It does not prove that the historical Minkowski formalization faithfully translates the Crullerverse source.

**Net result:** source-to-formalization discrepancy clarified; no new theorem or equation correction is justified until the missing maps are defined.

A separate Lorentzian or emergent GR-like metric could still be introduced, but the source-to-metric map must be defined. The direct source's mention of a GR-metric-like cross-sectional structure is not yet that map.
