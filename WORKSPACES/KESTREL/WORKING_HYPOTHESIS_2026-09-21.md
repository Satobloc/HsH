- Entering astronomy/physics underprepared mathematically, then finding paleontology as a domain in which he could excel while continuing to range widely across disciplines.

- Working at institutions including COSI and AMNH, with long-term proximity to working scientists while remaining unusually broad and technologically adventurous in education practice.
- Never abandoning the attempt to think four-dimensionally.

## 4. Old 4D idea meets LLM reconstruction

Nathan had carried a 4D geometric idea for decades.

Around 2023–24 he began floating it to LLMs and repeatedly received the reaction that, once rendered carefully, the geometry was not obviously absurd. That initiated formal reconstruction.

Nathan's description of the developmental rhythm is:

> obvious from the geometry -> next obvious consequence -> apparent problem -> maybe wrong -> test the small set of possible continuations -> one works -> next mountain -> another structure falls out.

The core hypothesis is that much of SAT was not invented by free association but constrained by a persistent geometry-first rule: take Minkowski seriously, remove labels, inspect the geometry, and only introduce additional choices where the inherited physics leaves genuine underdetermination.
