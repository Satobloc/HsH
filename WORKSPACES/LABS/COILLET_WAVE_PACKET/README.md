A candidate name for a **fundamental-scale single traveling coil/packet**, intended as the smallest member of a possible recursive coil hierarchy. Scale, invariant definition and particle assignment remain open.

## Candidate structural branches

### A. Particle/wave registration branch

Nathan's current intuition is approximately:

`traveling coil packet + sufficiently defined holonomic closure/correlation -> particle-like registration`

versus

`extended/nonclosed carrier excitation -> wave-like propagation/measurement outcome`

This is worth formalizing, but **“particle-wave duality obtained” is not yet warranted**. Standard quantum mechanics does not require a literal material wave that physically collapses into a particle. H(s)H needs an explicit map from its geometric state to amplitudes, probabilities and detector records before this can count as a replacement or derivation.

### B. Transparency / parasitic-excitation branch

A traveling second-order loop on a persistent first-order coil may sharpen the early SAT language of “parasitic vibrations/excitations.” Candidate use cases include EM propagation through matter and neutrino transparency. A valid model must calculate interaction strengths/cross sections or suppressions rather than merely note that one structure can pass along another.

### C. Photon / neutrino branch

Candidate versions include:

- photon as a fundamental-scale traveling `coil-let` on an otherwise comparatively straight vacuum carrier;
- neutrino as a higher-order single coil on a vacuum carrier or matter coil;
- different closure, chirality or carrier state generating different observed sectors.

Hard constraint: a shared geometric family must still reproduce the photon as a spin-1 boson with electromagnetic gauge coupling and the neutrino as a spin-1/2 fermion with weak coupling, chiral structure, flavor mixing and oscillation. Geometry alone is not enough unless the relevant representation/statistics/couplings are derived from it.

### D. Three-sphere / ᚼ generation branch

The three-sphere construction is a plausible place to look for allowed-state generation because the existing project already has a clean symmetric carrier relation. The candidate mechanism should define:

1. sphere/intersection state;
2. carrier circle or collapse state;
3. coil order/index;
4. closure invariant;
5. chirality sign/state;
6. ᚼ map between indexed states;
7. one scale map with explicit calibration quantities and held-out predictions.

### E. Chirality-change branch

Nathan proposes that some interactions, or a measurable fraction of interactions at particular incident angles, may produce chirality changes. This becomes testable only after defining a chirality operator/state variable and an interaction/transition rule. There is no generic standard-physics law “incident angle causes chirality flip” that can simply be imported; the project must derive a transition probability or selection rule and compare it with the correct experimental observable.

### F. BH::BB ironing / wringing branch

Candidate picture: a black-hole side preferentially removes or “irons out” traveling kinks while preserving some embedded topological/twist information, feeding persistentized twist structure through the BH::BB recurrence and into the continuing extrusion surface (“CF Mattermall”). The larger recurrent worldline/toroidal update picture is nicknamed GHDDEF.

This branch needs conservation, information, horizon/causal and thermodynamic accounting before it can become more than topology-inspired cosmological architecture.

### G. Non-worldtube/Kelvin vacuum branch

Alternative to a BEC-like sea of vacuum worldtubes:

`background medium -> fundamental filament/vortex formation -> enrolled/persistent matter state -> BH::BB recurrence`

with unbound EM-like propagation represented by Kelvin/vortex-fluid behavior and matter represented by a persistent/enrolled surface or filament state.

Any such medium model must recover the observed Lorentz-covariant low-energy phenomenology and satisfy the relevant null tests. “Aether” here is therefore a candidate emergent-medium construction, not permission to import a preferred-frame model unnoticed.

## Immediate hard constraints / places this could be wrong

1. **Particle-wave duality:** a suggestive geometric mechanism is not yet a derivation of the Born rule, interference amplitudes, entanglement or detector statistics.
2. **Photon vs neutrino:** spin/statistics and gauge/weak representation differences must emerge, not be assigned by picture.
3. **Mass at `c`:** under ordinary special relativity a free excitation with nonzero invariant rest mass cannot travel exactly at `c`. If H(s)H gives a photon or neutrino a nonzero invariant mass while retaining exact `c`, it must explicitly modify or rederive the dispersion/invariant structure. Do not call an internal/effective geometric scale “rest mass” by default.
4. **Chirality:** angle-dependent chirality change requires a mathematical transition law and the correct experimental comparison class.
5. **Transparency:** qualitative pass-through geometry is insufficient; calculate coupling/cross-section consequences.
6. **Closure:** “holonomic closure = particle” must be represented by a well-defined invariant and connected to quantum observables.
7. **Recursive universes/coils:** scale recursion is an architectural hypothesis until scale laws and cross-level observables are specified.
8. **BH::BB:** topology does not by itself solve conservation, information or causal consistency.
9. **Vacuum medium:** any Kelvin/fluid analogue must reproduce the strong empirical success of Lorentz invariance and avoid an unobserved preferred-frame residue.

| T1 closure invariant | Define a scalar/discrete invariant distinguishing closed coil packet from open propagation | If definition depends on observer/display convention, reject or reformulate |

| T5 coupling/transparency | Geometry -> EM/weak interaction strength or cross-section scaling | Pure visual penetrability is insufficient |

| T7 three-sphere/ᚼ map | Sphere state -> coil index/closure/chirality/scale | Fit a limited calibration set, predict held-out ratios |
| T8 photoneutrino | Common carrier model -> distinct photon/neutrino observables | Must recover polarization/helicity, flavor/mixing and interaction-sector differences |
| T9 BH::BB | Explicit conserved quantities, information map and causal orientation | Vague topological continuity is not enough |
| T10 Kelvin medium | Effective equations and symmetry limit | Must recover Lorentz-covariant observations and pass applicable null tests |
