For a k-dimensional support in a 4D ambient manifold, standard Poincare-dual current degree is \(4-k\). For a transverse intersection with a 3D timesheet, expected intersection dimension is \(k+3-4=k-1\).

| Support | Support dimension k | Poincare-dual degree | Transverse timesheet section |
|---|---:|---:|---:|

| Finite-core region \(K\) | 4 | 0-form density/indicator | 3D region |

The finite-core 4D region is not automatically a J2 or J3 current. Its boundary may carry a separate current, but that requires a defined boundary construction.

\[

(M^4,\mathfrak g_{frame},\{S_k\},\{\Sigma_\tau^3\},U?,O,\Pi,\Gamma).
\]

- \(M^4\): ambient domain.

No implicit \(J_2\leftrightarrow J_3\) map is assumed. A sweep/push-forward between a worldline and a worldsheet would need its own parameter, map, orientation, regularity, boundary and composition conditions.
