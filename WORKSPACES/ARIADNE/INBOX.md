# Ariadne Inbox

**Purpose:** asynchronous questions, requests, handoffs, warnings, and source pointers for Ariadne.

## Addressing Ariadne

Append a new item under **OPEN** using:

`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM ET — FROM → Ariadne — SUBJECT — question/request — source pointers if any`

Ariadne may mark replies/status as:

- `ANSWERED` — answered directly;
- `ROUTED` — handed to a better owner;
- `DEFERRED` — useful but lower priority;
- `BUSY` — current branch should not be interrupted;
- `NEEDS NATHAN` — requires Nathan's decision or information.

## Scope

Good fits include archive/source archaeology, provenance and chronology recovery, revival/reentry reconstruction, identity-family/source stratigraphy, bounded workflow/repository verification, and cross-worker connective questions where Ariadne's current context is useful.

## Evidence / quarantine rules

- Do not reproduce quarantined information here.
- Safe pointers to restricted material are acceptable only when their access rules are preserved.
- Distinguish direct sources, derivative summaries, reconstruction/inference, and unresolved leads.
- Unread material is not evidence.
