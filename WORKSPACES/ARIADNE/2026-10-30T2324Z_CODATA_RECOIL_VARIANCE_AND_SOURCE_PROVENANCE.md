## 4. CODATA 2022 source and measurement chain

Primary reference: Mohr et al., *CODATA recommended values of the fundamental physical constants: 2022*, J. Phys. Chem. Ref. Data 54, 033105 (2025), [official NIST PDF](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/JPCRD2022CODATA.pdf). The publication year is 2025; the adjustment uses data available through 2022-12-31.

The source distinguishes four input data:
- D3: (h/m(^{87}\mathrm{Rb})=4.59135925890(65)\times10^{-9}\;\mathrm{m^2/s}), LKB / Morel et al. (2020), Nature 588, 61–65, DOI 10.1038/s41586-020-2964-7.
- D4: (h/m(^{133}\mathrm{Cs})=3.0023694721(12)\times10^{-9}\;\mathrm{m^2/s}), UC Berkeley / Parker et al. (2018), Science 360, 191–195, DOI 10.1126/science.aap7706.
- D5: (A_r(^{87}\mathrm{Rb})=86.9091805291(65)), dimensionless.
- D6: (A_r(^{133}\mathrm{Cs})=132.9054519585(86)), dimensionless.

CODATA Section VI describes atom-recoil measurements as atom interferometry with atoms in superpositions of momentum states. Photon momenta are obtained from the photon energy-momentum relation; Bloch oscillations transfer many photon momenta to the atoms to improve sensitivity. Morel's Rb experiment is a matter-wave/Bloch-oscillation measurement. Parker's Cs experiment uses multiphoton Bragg diffraction and Bloch oscillations in a Ramsey-Bordé interferometer.

D5 and D6 are not new recoil measurements. They are relative atomic-mass inputs from the 2020 Atomic Mass Data Center (AMDC) evaluation, based on a least-squares adjustment of accepted experimental mass data. AME2020 Part I describes input-data evaluation and adjustment procedures; Part II provides the evaluated tables. Their covariance is therefore an evaluation product, not an additional atom-interferometer correlation.

**Correlation scope:** (r(D5,D6)=+0.1032) in CODATA Tables I/XXVI is the AMDC Rb/Cs relative-mass-input correlation. It is not the correlation between the D3/D4 recoil experiments and not the correlation between the final alpha determinations. Table XXVI lists correlations above (|r|>0.0001); D3-D4 and the other D3-D6 cross-pairs are not listed there.

CODATA's final 2022 alpha adjustment multiplies the uncertainties of all six input data D1-D6 in Table XXV by an expansion factor of 2.5: D1 electron anomaly, D2 its theory-uncertainty correction, D3/D4 atom recoil, and D5/D6 AMDC mass inputs. The adjustment is global (133 input data and 79 adjusted constants). A uniform (z/2.5) sensitivity for this standalone D3-D6 ratio diagnostic is not the CODATA global least-squares residual.
