## 3. New architecture improvement: three independent necessity axes

The current user lane requires a distinction between structures that are genuinely forced and those that are merely convenient. The Nov 9 prior-context constraint is consistent with the current BEDROCK and Primary Operational Guideline, but its raw source locator remains unresolved.

I added a reusable Structure Admission Gate with three independent axes:

1. SOURCE_FORCED: explicitly present in a primary source or direct user instruction. This establishes source authority, not physical truth.
2. MAP_FORCED_FOR_THIS_REPRESENTATION: required to make a specifically defined map well-typed and well-defined after testing whether existing primitives, reparameterizations, or derived quantities suffice. This establishes a representation requirement, not a new physical ontology.
3. EMPIRICALLY_REQUIRED: the minimal model fails a predeclared test on a real, versioned measured system, while the smallest extension produces a distinct, identifiable prediction on independent holdout data after calibration, uncertainty/covariance, shared-parameter freeze, standard comparator, and instrument/readout effects are accounted for.

A structure may be source-forced but empirically unvalidated; map-forced only for one chosen representation; empirically required without having appeared in an earlier source; optional on all axes; or unresolved because definitions or data are missing. The unqualified word FORCED should not collapse these statuses.

This extends the existing projection-aware evidence-chain work. It does not create another map framework or duplicate Kestrel's law-valued factorization theorem. The new contribution is the separation of source, representation, and empirical necessity, with a minimality test before any new primitive is admitted.
