## 7. Formal map contract and existing Lean evidence

The October 21 assistant expression \(H_{i\to j}=\pi_j\circ\pi_i^{-1}\) is not generally valid when \(\pi_i\) is non-injective. The appropriate generic condition is fiber compatibility.

For a common parent space \(X\), projections \(\pi_i:X\to Y\) and \(\pi_j:X\to Z\), a single-valued map on the image of \(\pi_i\),

\[
H:\operatorname{range}(\pi_i)\to Z,\qquad
H(\pi_i(x))=\pi_j(x),
\]

exists exactly when

\[
\forall x,y\in X,\quad
\pi_i(x)=\pi_i(y)\Rightarrow\pi_j(x)=\pi_j(y).
\]

It is unique on \(\operatorname{range}(\pi_i)\). Extending it to all of \(Y\) requires additional conditions or an explicit extension choice. Composition consistency,

\[
H_{i\to k}=H_{j\to k}\circ H_{i\to j},
\]

must then be checked on the relevant images; it is not automatic from a diagram or analogy.

Two existing Lean modules already formalize relevant conditional mathematics:

1. [Kestrel PR #873](https://github.com/Satobloc/HsH/pull/873), module \`KestrelProjectedIncidence.lean\`, head \`94b6d96c91806a95505260a3c52220f4af294185\`, blob \`0fe5390faa10113830132b12cdb98172194adfee\`. The theorem \`hallway_factorization_iff_fiber_compatibility\` formalizes the factorization criterion; related results distinguish actual intersection from projected overlap. Exact-head Actions run [#44090154878](https://github.com/Satobloc/HsH/actions/runs/44090154878), job \`134062868110\`, passed checkout assertion and Lean compilation. The existence construction uses classical choice; the module contains no \`sorry\` or \`admit\`. It is a generic set-map theorem, not an instantiated Klein Fabric → GR/QFT map.
2. [Ravel PR #1133](https://github.com/Satobloc/HsH/pull/1133), module \`RavelCarrierSheetDimensionGate.lean\`, head \`fc7f92722fe5e0757d55f961b54a12bdee66d5da\`, blob \`7e7e359817c00a9faf4c1577f95952ce42265b16\`.
