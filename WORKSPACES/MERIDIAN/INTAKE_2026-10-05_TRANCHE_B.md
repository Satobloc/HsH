## High-value source-authority findings

### A. Derive from the Lagrangian means literally derive from the Lagrangian

In \`Lagrangian derivations explained — raw.json\`, Nathan's user turn is explicit: "I keep asking for derivations from primitives. What I mean is from the Lagrangian."

This should control reconstruction methodology. A geometric relation can be used as a kinematic identity or solver lemma, but claims of dynamics, force, propagation, mass, or transition rates should ultimately be shown as consequences of an action/energy functional rather than merely attached to a geometric analogy.

### B. Field-theory formalism is an interface, not the core ontology

\`Reasoning with SAT-HSH Skill — raw(3).json\` contains the explicit later correction:

> I don't particularly like the field theory framing. I still think it should remain worldline at core, but using the ER bridge as that line. A line, with thickness. But still a line, not a field.

This is already represented in HsH's verified provenance surfaces. The tranche strengthens the instruction: old compact-phase / current / field-kernel machinery may survive as a calculational representation or effective action, but should not silently replace the finite worldtube as the core object.

### C. Two interaction families are dated source structure, not yet a proof of fundamental-force reduction

The same conversation contains the dated Nathan statement of two "current theory thinking" interaction families: Electrogravity and Interbraid. Existing HsH provenance already preserves this wording and its tentative clauses. Keep the categories operational; do not infer that all mechanisms inside them are already derived.

### D. Old coil-count quantization can be made gauge covariant

\`Quantum discreteness explanation — raw.json\` contains Nathan's direct question about integer coil count producing discreteness while coil radius/stretch can vary. This led to new Meridian XCVIII:

\[
N=\frac1{2\pi}\oint(\theta'+\omega_s)\,ds
\]

on a selected normal two-plane, with \(\omega_s\) the normal-frame connection.

This makes the integer winding sector gauge invariant while leaving radius, pitch, strain, stiffness, and sector energy free to vary continuously. It also separates **sector quantization** from **click/snapback transition kinetics**.

### E. Historical "mass as drag" is compatible with constitutive-response language, but not yet a derivation

\`Mass in SAT framework — raw.json\` contains Nathan's direct hypothesis that filaments and timesheet should be treated as massless unless evidence requires otherwise, with observed mass instantiated by their interaction/drag.

This is useful as a reconstruction target, but the accompanying historical assistant/NotebookLM equations mix several incompatible generations of SAT formalism. Current H(s)H should formulate "mass" operationally as a response coefficient derived from the chosen worldtube/timesheet action, rather than inherit historical tension scales or target masses.

### F. "Pure geometry first" recurs as a user correction

\`Geometric Foundations Evaluation — raw(1).json\` contains a direct correction during a spinning-disc/worldtube exercise: "We aren’t doing substrate dynamics. We are doing pure geometry ... In four dimensions."

This reinforces solver discipline: geometry fixtures should be solved geometrically before constitutive interpretation is layered on.
