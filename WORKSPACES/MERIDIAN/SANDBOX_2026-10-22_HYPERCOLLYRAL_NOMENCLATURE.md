# Hypercollyral nomenclature (2026-10-22)

**User-selected term:** Hypercollyral.

Working designation for the four-dimensional Crullerverse / hypertoroidal construction and its geometry, replacing assistant-suggested alternatives such as Hypercollyroid, Hypercrulloid, Hypercoloral and Hypercrustulumnar in current conversation. Name alone does not fix topology, embedding, metric, or dynamics.

**Dimensional distinction:** a 4D bulk can have a 3D boundary/hypersurface; a generic transverse intersection of two 3D hypersurfaces in 4D has dimension 2. A 3D ball-like timesheet footprint requires finite 4D volume or a specified nontransverse configuration. Keep full hypercollyral geometry separate from lower-dimensional torus analogues.

Research note, not a derivation or an addition to BEDROCK.
