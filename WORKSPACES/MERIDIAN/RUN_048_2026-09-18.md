## Recovered Nathan-direct geometric ancestry
The early 2024 construction is more foundational to later SAT readout language than the historical glossaries alone reveal:

1. Nathan first explicitly holds the geometry **static and without a time dimension**: a straight line and a helical line intersect a plane in two points, `S` and `H`.
2. He then introduces time operationally by **moving the plane** along the lines while maintaining its orientation.
3. Nathan corrects the observation frame: restrict attention to what appears on the moving 2D plane. In that readout, `S` is stationary while `H` traces a circular orbit around it.
4. Nathan explicitly corrects the assistant that the orbit radius is **not controlled by helix pitch**. It is the cross-sectional radius / distance from the helix to its long axis. Pitch affects the rate of angular motion when combined with plane speed, not the orbit radius.
5. Nathan then introduces a single diagonal line intersecting the moving plane. The intersection point moves across the plane; after adding coordinates, the apparent in-plane motion is controlled by the line's orientation relative to the plane/propagation axis.
6. Nathan combines these: tilt the long axis of the helix while retaining a single helix-plane intersection. The observed two-point system translates across the plane while one point orbits the other.
7. When the assistant proposes a geometrically impossible varying-radius perfect-helix explanation for two points approaching/contacting/separating, Nathan rejects it explicitly: a perfect helix has no maximum/minimum radius and its axis/helix intersections remain equidistant. He then asks for a geometry that genuinely produces the desired readout.
