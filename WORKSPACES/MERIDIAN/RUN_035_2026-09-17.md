## Main result

The named Whirlygig formalization is best typed as a **functional reduction / surrogate of the Donut toy**, not an established equivalent representation. It preserves three coarse invariants of intent:

1. two inputs;
2. coupled/orthogonal rotational handling;
3. a composite trace on a torus.

It does **not** preserve, by any sourced map yet recovered, the antecedent's anchor geometry, Great-Annulum topology, moving chord/midpoint plane, explicit sweep vector, pivot/inner-circumference radial construction, even-multiple winding constraint, or the stronger readout claim `Curvy_Lisa = QwrtR = derivative of Q wrt R`.

The most consequential mismatch is therefore not cosmetic complexity. The full toy claims a relational/derivative readout generated by constrained mechanics; the named surrogate directly parameterizes a torus and calls the resulting Lissajous the interaction trace. No source equation establishes that these are the same operator.
