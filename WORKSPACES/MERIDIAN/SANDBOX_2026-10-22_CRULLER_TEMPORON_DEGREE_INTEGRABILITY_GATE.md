## Exact toy intersection theorem
Let E be a connected closed loop and restrict a phase map p:E=S1 -> S1 with degree m. For regular target sheet phase a, n_+ positive crossings and n_- negative crossings satisfy
n_+ - n_- = m; total n=n_++n_- = |m|+2k, k>=0 when orientation indexed appropriately. A degree-2 choice therefore has 2,4,6,... regular preimages. This conclusion is *conditional on a degree-2 phase map*, not derivable from generic toroidal geometry alone. A degree-1 phase map may have one regular preimage. Number of time sheets is separate from footprint count per sheet.

Numerical fixture: p_A(u)=2u + A sin(3u) mod 2pi, sheet p=1.5 rad. A=0.4 has two intersections at u=0.55065865135 and 3.99927437536, both positive. A=1.7 has four at u=0.22294281658 (+),1.26333647464 (-),1.59731211080 (+),4.10442773285 (+). Signed count remains 2. The phase modulation is a geometric toy, not a wave, dynamics, or a temporon physical derivation.

## Integrability gate
A sheet-normal 1-form n proportional to dT must obey n wedge dn=0 (Frobenius). For n=dw+epsilon*x*dy, dn=epsilon dx wedge dy, giving n wedge dn=epsilon dw wedge dx wedge dy !=0 for epsilon !=0; such an arbitrary direction perturbation cannot be a foliation normal. Conversely n=d(w+epsilon sin(kx)) is exact and integrable (after normalization when appropriate). A quantized mode of a time-normal must respect its constraint; `delta u` alone does not imply independent sheet families, and distinct sheets in a foliation cannot simply be multiplied into simultaneous electron population.
