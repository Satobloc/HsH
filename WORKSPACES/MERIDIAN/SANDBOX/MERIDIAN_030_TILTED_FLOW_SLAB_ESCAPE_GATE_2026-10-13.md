# Meridian 030: tilted-flow slab escape gate (SANDBOX, 2026-10-13)
Source-grounded scheduled reconstruction, not original Meridian participation or a physical claim.

Let X=(w,x,y,z), preferred unit flow a=(cos(theta),0,0,sin(theta)), tau=a·X, and g=I-2aa^T. Apply Morrow's fixed-endpoint two-center homotopy X_±=(w,±r/2), |r|=R. For the additional condition that both centers occupy the same slab centered at t(w)=cos(theta)w, |tau(X_±)-t(w)|<=h/2 iff h>=R|sin(theta)|, since max|r_z|=R. This is not entailed merely by the existence of a time function.

With q=(R/L)M, M=3.170352416021 from Morrow's numerical minimax, the center tangents are guaranteed timelike if cos(theta)>q sin(theta) and 1+q²<2(cos(theta)-q sin(theta))².

At R=1,L=20,theta=0.5,h=0.5,D_core=0.8: R sin(theta)=0.4794255386<h, q=0.1585176208, and the conservative upper bound for g(tangent,tangent) is -0.2599497211. Equal-w separation R>D_core persists, yet h<D_core. This does not refute Quarry 139: its h<D_core condition caps the actual fourth-direction bypass, whereas this resolving slab caps tau, a different coordinate.

A 701x701 scripted grid confirmed max r_z/R=1, sampled M=3.1703303372, and sampled maximum g=-0.3266098768 at theta=0.5; independent finite differences checked normalized tangent. Grid values are numerical, not global bounds. Full tube injectivity and physical confinement remain unproved.
