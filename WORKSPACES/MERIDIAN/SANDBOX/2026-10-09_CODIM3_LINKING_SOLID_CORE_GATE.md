## Exact fixture (LOCAL:MERIDIAN-CODIM3-2026-10-09)

In Euclidean R4 take a closed oriented filament `C(t)=(cos t,sin t,0,0)`, bounding disk `D={(x,y,0,0):x²+y²≤1}`. Take a 2-sphere `S_b(θ,φ)=(b+ρ cosθ,0,ρ sinθ cosφ,ρ sinθ sinφ)`, with `b≥1, 0<ρ<1`. It bounds the filled 3-ball `B³_b={(x,0,z,w):(x-b)²+z²+w²≤ρ²}`.

A 1-cycle and 2-cycle have integer linking in R4 (`1+2=4-1`), unlike two 1-cycles under unrestricted R4 isotopy. Oriented disk intersections give `Lk(C,S_b)=-1` for `1≤b<1+ρ`, `0` for `b>1+ρ`, undefined at `b=1+ρ`. Independently, numerical degree of the Gauss map `(C-S)/|C-S|: S¹×S²→S³` agrees to <2e-9 for noncontact fixtures; determinant density invariant under 50 SO(4) rotations.

**Critical distinction:** minimum 4D distance to the *shell* is `|b-1-ρ|`; minimum distance to the *filled 3-ball* is `max(0,b-1-ρ)`. Hence every nonzero-linking fixture intersects the filled core. At `b=1,ρ=0.5`, C misses the S² boundary by 0.5 but passes through its filled interior. **Meridional S² linking cannot by itself provide noncontact Interbraid between disjoint solid finite-core filaments.**

A genuine solid thickening of a 1D centerline in R4 is 4D, with 3D boundary and S² meridional cross sections. The whole boundary, a meridional S², a 3D timesheet intersection, and the filled core are distinct geometric objects.
