## B. Hallway factorization theorem and constructive counterexample

Given a shared primitive space P and observable maps `pi_C:P->C`, `pi_F:P->F`, a deterministic single-valued hallway `h:C->F` satisfying `pi_F=h o pi_C` exists on the image iff **pi_F is constant on every fiber pi_C^{-1}(c)**. A Moore-Penrose pseudo-inverse is a choice of representative, NOT proof that a hallway is well-defined. At differentiable points a necessary local condition is `ker Dpi_C subset ker Dpi_F`. Global fiber constancy remains the exact criterion.
