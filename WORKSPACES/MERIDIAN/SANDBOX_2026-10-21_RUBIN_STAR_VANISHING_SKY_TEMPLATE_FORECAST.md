## Minimal explicit projected-ring template

Define f(n)=arcsin(n·a), and hypothesized event excess in |f(n)-δ0|<w. This is a geometrical observational *template*, not a consequence proven from the current torus. The latitude ring is a great circle iff δ0=0 and a small circle otherwise. Chiral handedness is NOT encoded by this static ring. For a whole-sky isotropic selection, half-width w and |δ0|+w<pi/2, exact area fraction:
p= [sin(δ0+w)-sin(δ0-w)]/2=cos(δ0) sin(w).
