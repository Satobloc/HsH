where `u_i,u_j` are declared oriented phase axes/directors (or an equivalent transverse marked frame). For a chosen oriented generator `J_u`, a phase-bearing factor can be represented locally as `R_u(phi)=exp(phi J_u)`. Then a marked-frame factorization such as `F_i = B_i R_{u_i}(phi_i)` separates carrier/base orientation `B_i` from phase. Hence phase is not recoverable from `F_i`, and therefore not from `Q_ij`, unless the base-frame gauge is fixed by geometric/material data or phase is retained explicitly.
