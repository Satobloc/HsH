## 1. Three category errors an untrained instance is most likely to make

1. **Treat the four-dimensional history as a three-dimensional object moving through an external time.** The carrier/history must be typed separately from the observed three-dimensional section or apparent motion.
2. **Move `c` or another speed onto the wrong object.** Current training specifically requires distinguishing timesheet/resolver, timesheet-filament intersection/readout propagation, material/carrier geometry, phase/parameter rates, and observed motion. `c` cannot be transferred among those categories by shorthand.
3. **Confuse representation, parameterization, or slicing with physical mechanism.** In particular: spacetime coordinate `w` cannot silently double as an evolution/variational parameter, and rotating/reorienting a representation cannot be assumed to alter the underlying carrier or resolver physics.

These are the three errors most strongly exposed by my full reads of Fundamental Intuitions Extended, 4D Thinking Primer, and `DEVELOPMENT_FULL_CONVOS/H(s)H TIME RESIDUALS.txt`.

## 3. Blind/sequestered test of genuine 4D/SAT competence

Give the candidate an unseen, source-authentic SAT/H(s)H construction packet containing:

- one carrier/history description;
- one resolver/timesheet description;
- one intersection/readout description;
- one projected/observed description;
- one deliberately ambiguous speed or parameter statement;
- one diagram or geometric solver snapshot.

Withhold later corrections and all familiar SAT labels. Require the candidate to:

1. build a typed object table before equations;
2. assign every coordinate, parameter, velocity/rate, and map to an object;
3. distinguish intrinsic geometry from resolver/intersection behavior and projected observation;
4. identify which transformations are coordinate/representation changes and which would change the modeled state;
5. state at least one quantity that cannot be inferred from the supplied information;
6. make one prediction about how a stated change in resolver orientation/advance would alter the **readout**, without changing the underlying history unless the packet explicitly says it does;
7. identify the ambiguous sentence and refuse to repair it by assumption.

Score against a held-out Nathan-authored correction/key. Vocabulary recall should earn essentially no credit. A strong candidate can preserve object typing and underdetermination even when SAT terminology is removed.

## 4. Solver family as generative calculator: held-out benchmark + negative control

Yes, but the test should first establish whether the solver has a recoverable operator rather than merely a persuasive visualization.

### Historical held-out benchmark

**Whirligig GR↔QM replay.** Freeze one historical raw-source input set and withhold the reported result. The worker must reconstruct the original Whirligig operator from firsthand artifacts, explicitly state state-space/input/output/constraints/invariants/free choices, execute or reproduce the historical procedure, and report the held-out output before seeing the archived answer. Then independently recompute the same target with ordinary mathematics.

Pass conditions should distinguish:
- recovery of the historical procedure;
- reproduction of the held-out numerical/structural result;
- agreement or disagreement with independent calculation;
- number and nature of discretionary interventions needed.

The strongest result is not necessarily a match; a clean failure can show that the solver is genuinely constrained.

### Negative control

Use the **same frozen source packet but remove or alter one indispensable source-authored constraint/map while keeping the remaining inputs superficially plausible**. The solver should no longer recover the same held-out relation. If it can still be steered to the target with small discretionary changes, the system is too flexible to count as a generative calculator for that benchmark.

A second negative control is intervention-budget based: give an unrelated or intentionally mismatched target only after the operator is frozen. The worker is not allowed to add new transforms/weights/axes. Success at manufacturing the requested relation would count against the solver, not for it.

## 5. Small task that discriminates translation from first-principles rebuild

Take **one historical solver claim** and prohibit use of its polished equation/result. Permit only the earliest firsthand geometric instructions/diagram and standard mathematics.

The worker must independently derive the minimal operator implied by the source, list every assumption/free degree of freedom, and compute the output. Only after freezing that packet may the later SAT equation/result be revealed.

Classification:
- **first-principles rebuild:** the later expression/output emerges, or a clearly related restricted form emerges, without importing it;
- **translation:** the later equation has to be used as a target/template to define the operator or choose free parameters;
- **underdetermined:** multiple operators/results remain compatible with the early source;
- **historical mismatch:** the reconstructed operator gives a different result.

My preferred first specimen remains the Whirligig GR↔QM chain because it simultaneously tests source archaeology, object typing, intervention budget, geometric-to-formal translation, and independent mathematical checking.
