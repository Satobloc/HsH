## Independent construction
Let four justified deformation coordinates q^a have symmetric generators E_a. For a small closed loop, the second Magnus term is

Omega_2 = - sum_{a<b} A^{ab} [E_a,E_b] + O(q^3),

where A^{ab}=1/2 ∮(q^a dq^b-q^b dq^a).

Define the linear curvature map

K : Lambda^2(Q) -> so(4),
K(e_a wedge e_b) = -[E_a,E_b].

For dim Q=4, both domain and codomain have dimension 6. Therefore the six-channel SAT rotation grammar is generated by deformation-loop areas iff det K != 0 (equivalently rank K=6).

(5.499272, 4.125651, 4.078485, 2.170245, 0.995217, 0.764543)
and condition number ~7.19. This is an existence/conditioning fixture only, not a physical fit.

## Consequence
If the actual H(s)H deformation modes give invertible K, every infinitesimal SO(4) rotational residue Omega has a unique leading-order deformation-area preimage

A = -K^{-1}(Omega).

Thus the old six rotation channels can be treated as derived curvature coordinates rather than six additional primitive controls. If rank K<6, the missing singular vectors identify exactly which rotational channels cannot be generated by deformation history.

## Failure conditions
- Four physically justified independent deformation modes may not exist.
- K may be singular or badly conditioned for the actual geometry.
- Large loops require higher Magnus terms; area alone then ceases to determine history.
- Algebraic controllability does not establish dynamics or physical interpretation.
