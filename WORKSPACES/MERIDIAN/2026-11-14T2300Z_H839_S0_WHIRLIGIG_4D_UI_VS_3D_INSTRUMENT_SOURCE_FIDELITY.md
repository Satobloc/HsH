## 7. Mathematical counterexample and formal proof boundary

The existing Kernel 0 W5 counterexample is exact. For the family

\[
y_\omega''+\omega^2 y_\omega=0,\qquad
y_\omega(t)=\cos(\omega t),\quad \omega>0,
\]

the normalized phase-plane curve is

\[
\gamma_\omega(t)=
\left(y_\omega(t),\frac{y_\omega'(t)}{\omega}\right)
=(\cos(\omega t),-\sin(\omega t)).
\]

Over one period its unparameterized image is the same unit circle for every positive \omega. The curve image alone therefore cannot recover the equation's frequency. The clock/parameter channel, and more generally the operator/coefficient information, must be retained. This is a mathematical counterexample, not a physical fixture or measurement.
