## Why this matrix exists

The current evidence does not support either of these simplistic stories:

> Nathan invented moving-section filament geometry and particle braids from nothing.

or:

> SAT is just Hinton/Braid Theory/Bilson-Thompson under new names.

The actual record is stratified. Different predecessors own different pieces, at different model levels. Nathan's documented construction both **rediscovered old geometry** and **reassigned older ingredients into a different Minkowski-first physical grammar**.

The useful question is therefore:

> **Which object, map, or relation is old; which is independently reconstructed; which is later imported; and which remains a more specific SAT mapping whose exact antecedent is not yet located?**

## Layer-by-layer priority status

### Layer A — moving-section explanation of apparent motion

**Historical priority unavailable to SAT.**

Hinton and Smith are decisive antecedents. Hinton's checked wording is particularly close:
- spiral;
- moving film;
- moving point;
- circular apparent motion;
- stationary higher structure;
- apparent bodies/motion from filament intersections.

Nathan's March-2024 construction is a **rediscovery/reconstruction candidate**, not an origin candidate for this generic mechanism.

### Layer B — formal braid mathematics

**Historical priority unavailable to SAT.**

Artin's braid programme formalizes strand isotopy/classification long before SAT. Artin's 1947 *Theory of Braids* explicitly says it is a rigorous successor to his earlier `Theorie der Zöpfe` and replaces intuitive projection methods with braid coordinates.

SAT may use, reinvent, or translate braid mathematics, but cannot claim priority for the underlying braid group machinery.

### Layer C — matter/particle structure described using braids

**Historical priority unavailable to SAT at broad level.**

Bilson-Thompson and later particle-braid work decisively predate SAT on:
- physical particle states represented through braid topology;
- three-strand braid structures in particle models.

The object map differs from SAT, but the broad idea is old.

### Layer D — quarks + worldlines + strings/helices + mesons/baryons

**Strong pre-SAT near-neighbor exists.**

Bars & Hanson 1976 place quark quantum numbers on worldlines joined by relativistic strings, include a spin-(	frac12) construction with helical classical motion, and build meson/baryon quark-string models.

This does not establish Nathan's braid relation, but it removes any credible claim that the combination “quark worldline + string + helix + hadron” is unprecedented.

### Layer E — constituent quark worldlines themselves forming 2-/3-strand composite braids

**Exact external antecedent not yet located in the bounded search.**

Nathan's checked assignment is:
[
	ext{quark worldline strands}
overset{2/3	ext{-braid}}{longrightarrow}
	ext{mesonic/hadronic composite history}.
]

The searched external sources instead give:
- string-connected quarks without an explicit braid;
- elementary quark/lepton states encoded by a braid;
- 3-braids describing fermionic/topological structures.

## What this does to the “Hintonian revival” framing

The current evidence makes that phrase less metaphorical.

A defensible historical reading is:

1. nineteenth/early-twentieth-century higher-space thinkers developed moving-section and filament/thread constructions;
2. relativity supplied a physically disciplined four-dimensional spacetime grammar;
3. braid theory supplied formal strand topology;
4. later particle/string/topology programmes explored neighboring pieces at different object levels;
5. Nathan, beginning from Minkowski-style geometric reconstruction rather than from that literature, documented a moving-section/helix/intertwining programme in 2024;
6. later prior-art review revealed that the route had backed into an older branch of geometric thought;
7. SAT/H(s)H then diverges or specializes depending on the exact object-level mapping being tested.

This is a **lineage hypothesis supported by construction-level correspondences**, not a claim that one historical school directly generated SAT.

## Paper A implication

Paper A's strongest eventual ancestry wording should not be:
> SAT independently invented all of these ideas.

Nor:
> SAT is a rediscovery of Hinton/Braid Theory.

A better research-facing statement is:

> SAT's documented Minkowski-first development independently reconstructed several constructions with substantial older higher-space and braid-theoretic antecedents, while assigning some of those structures to different physical objects. The scientifically interesting question is therefore not whether the ingredients have prior art, but which object-level maps are inherited, independently rediscovered, newly specialized, or mathematically inequivalent.

That framing is stronger because it survives prior-art discovery rather than depending on ignorance of it.
