## Overall assessment

R0 has a strong publishable *shape* for a sandboxed comparative paper: it states the outside motifs first, repeatedly denies equivalence/validation/influence, and turns resemblance into explicit tests capable of weakening the thesis. The most important revision need is not more SAT enthusiasm; it is **claim-by-claim tightening of the comparison layer** so the paper cannot slide from “selected structural motifs can be juxtaposed” into “the literature is converging on one architecture.” I recommend **MAJOR REVISION, retain sandbox status**.

## Major comments

### TERN-A-01 — Separate source-supported statements from the paper's synthesis more mechanically

The four outside-paper subsections are currently concise enough that a reader can easily treat the final sentence of each as something the cited authors themselves establish. Add citations at the exact sentences carrying each source claim, then explicitly mark the transition from source result to this paper's comparative abstraction. This is especially important for phrases such as “orientation/topology at one level appears as a physical discrete distinction at another,” “counterexample to the assumption that spacetime must itself be quantized,” and the recurrence/discreteness inference. The abstraction may be defensible, but its authorship should be unmistakably this paper's.

**Disposition requested:** citation + wording hardening, not removal of the synthesis.

### TERN-A-02 — “Convergence” and “proliferation” need a declared selection rule or weaker wording

The title, abstract, provenance section, discussion and conclusion use “convergence,” “recent proliferation,” and “appearing in multiple contemporary contexts.” Four selected contemporary motifs plus Hypothesis H can motivate a comparative exercise, but they do not by themselves establish a field-wide trend. State how these works were selected and what “convergence” means operationally here, or consistently narrow the claim to “structural convergence among the selected examples.”

This is the single biggest rhetoric-to-evidence mismatch in R0.

### TERN-A-03 — Independence/provenance language needs a precise evidentiary scope

The abstract calls SAT “independently developed”; later text says the motifs have documented development and no influence is claimed. The paper should distinguish at least three propositions: (a) SAT motifs are documented before particular recent publications; (b) no influence has been identified in either direction; (c) independent development in the stronger historical sense. Only assert the strongest proposition for the interval/sources actually supported by chronology. Exact SAT primary-source microcitations are therefore central evidence, not optional polish.

### TERN-A-04 — Keep the Hypothesis-H section explicitly asymmetric until an object-level map exists

The draft handles this fairly well, but “candidate four-dimensional effective realization, reduction or compactified shadow” still risks sounding like a live reduction hypothesis with more structure than currently supplied. The proposed `R_4` is useful if presented as a *test schema*. I suggest stating directly that no candidate domain/codomain functor, field-content map, compactification datum, or dynamics-preserving reduction is yet given. “Structural shadow” should remain the default description until at least one such object-level map survives the paper's own Reduction Test.

### TERN-A-05 — The holonomy paragraph is excellent; make its typing discipline the model for the entire comparison

The sentence requiring transported object, connection and path is one of R0's strongest safeguards. Extend that discipline to the other rows of the correspondence table. For each proposed correspondence, identify what data would have to be typed before the analogy becomes mathematical: orientation object/map, continuous evolution/transport law, closure criterion, observable/readout map, and invariant compared. This would convert the table from motif matching into a compact research protocol.

### TERN-A-06 — Oppenheim/Sajjad paragraph appears to make a broader philosophical inference than the local result warrants

“Supplies a sharply stated counterexample to the assumption that spacetime must itself be quantized before it can participate consistently in quantum phenomenology” is much broader than the nearby description of a specific postquantum-classical-gravity framework and weak-field analysis. Unless the cited paper itself supports that scope, narrow to what its consistency construction demonstrates under its own assumptions. Do not make one model carry a universal anti-quantization conclusion.

### TERN-A-07 — Carroll/Diachenko/Dulani inference should distinguish mathematical recurrence from physical discreteness

The draft says exact global recurrence can arise from continuous unitary evolution and then concludes that “Discreteness in the global observable behavior therefore need not imply a discrete underlying temporal substrate.” The first clause may support the second only for a carefully specified kind of discrete/recurrent observable structure. Tighten the language so recurrence/periodicity is not silently generalized to arbitrary observable discreteness or quantization.

### TERN-A-08 — SAT comparison object needs a compact provenance table, not just narrative chronology

Because the thesis depends on comparison with an “independently developed” SAT lineage, add a small table: motif / earliest currently documented SAT source / date / status then / current status. Include only claims actually recovered. This will also make later PRIOR_ART auditing much easier and prevent current H(s)H language from being projected backward.

## Minor comments

1. Define “Minkowski-first” once in operational terms; it currently carries both historical and methodological weight.
2. “falsifiable at the level appropriate to a structural paper” may invite unnecessary Popper-language debate. “discriminating” or “testable as a structural correspondence” is cleaner.
3. “categorical rather than algebraic” could be misread as a category-theory claim. If ordinary-language categorical is intended, replace it.
4. In the correspondence table, “Minkowski-first continuous background” should be historically/status qualified if later H(s)H geometry no longer treats that exact background in the same way.
5. The Conclusion's “strongly similar” is stronger than most of the careful body. Prefer “shows a substantive architectural resemblance under the selected comparison criteria” unless the revision defines a metric/criterion justifying “strongly.”
6. Bibliography normalization is publication-blocking for this paper because the argument is explicitly provenance-sensitive. Fill every DOI/arXiv/journal datum from public sources before Nathan-facing release.

## What I would preserve

Preserve the SAT-free-before-SAT ordering; the repeated non-equivalence/non-validation caveats; the explicit separation of endpoint closure from connection-dependent holonomy; the five failure-capable tests; and the plan to run PRIOR_ART only after the independent first round. Those are doing real epistemic work rather than decorative cautioning.

## Recommended R1 target

R1 should not become longer by much. The best revision is tighter: exact source citations at claims; declared selection/convergence scope; a compact SAT chronology/provenance table; weaker independence language where not directly established; and object-typing requirements generalized from the holonomy test to every correspondence row.

**Frozen verdict:** MAJOR REVISION / PROMISING SANDBOX PAPER. No claim here requires abandonment of the comparative project; several claims require narrower scope and better provenance before Nathan-facing review.
