# GRURPLE-A — Blind external-literature proliferation test protocol — 2026-10-11

**Worker:** Aster  
**Purpose:** test whether Paper A's residual field-level wording (“recent proliferation of structurally convergent motifs”) is supported by an existing blinded random arXiv physics corpus.  
**Status:** `PROTOCOL_FROZEN_BEFORE_YEAR_KEY`  
**Corpus:** HSH_RESOURCES `EXPOSURE_STATS/arXiv_analysis/2026-09-13_RANDOM_100x3_JAN_SEP/BLIND_300_title_abstract.csv`  
**Design:** 100 random physics abstracts each from Jan-Sep 2024, 2025, 2026; blind shuffle; year/author/category/arXiv ID withheld until labels are frozen.

## Firewall

This is **not** the quarantined SATity rubric and does not classify whether a paper is “SAT-like.”

It tests only the explicit architecture named in Paper A:

[
	ext{geometry/topology}
+
	ext{continuous evolution/transport}
+
	ext{closure/holonomy}
+
	ext{orientation}
ightarrow
	ext{effective physical distinction}.
]

No SAT vocabulary is required or rewarded.

## Five motif flags

### G — geometry / topology

Positive only when geometry/topology is part of the explanatory or formal structure, not merely a location adjective.

Examples of qualifying objects:
- topology/topological invariant;
- manifold/bundle/connection;
- homotopy/cohomology;
- geometric structure used explanatorily;
- compactification or dimensional geometry;
- knot/braid/winding when mathematically structural.

### T — continuous evolution / transport

Positive when the paper explicitly uses continuous dynamical evolution or transport as part of the structure:
- unitary evolution;
- parallel/geometric transport;
- continuous flow/dynamics/propagation;
- adiabatic evolution;
- worldline/path evolution.

Ordinary use of the word “transport” in instrumentation/material handling is not sufficient.

### C — closure / holonomy / recurrence

Positive for an explicit global/path/periodic closure structure:
- holonomy/monodromy;
- closed-path transport;
- exact recurrence/cyclicity;
- winding/loop closure;
- quantization or invariant explicitly tied to path/topological closure.

Mere periodic data-taking or “closed form” mathematics is not sufficient.

### O — orientation / chirality / discrete orientation structure

Positive for:
- orientation/non-orientability;
- chirality/handedness;
- parity/CP/time-reversal orientation structure;
- oriented winding or orientation reversal;
- spin chirality when used as a structural physical distinction.

Generic vector direction alone is not sufficient.

### E — effective physical distinction generated by the structure

Positive only when the abstract says or clearly entails that the structural machinery **produces, induces, organizes, protects, quantizes, breaks, or explains** a physical/effective distinction.

Examples:
- topology induces symmetry breaking;
- geometry produces a transport response;
- compactification generates a lower-dimensional distinction;
- holonomy changes a physical orientation;
- global closure yields quantized/discrete observable behavior;
- emergent/effective degrees of freedom arise from the structure.

A paper can have G/T/C/O vocabulary without E.

## Automated high-precision candidate screen

The lexical screen is only a recall aid. It does not determine the semantic label.

A record enters manual review when it satisfies either:

[
G_{m lex} land E_{m lex} land (C_{m lex}lor O_{m lex})
]

or

[
G_{m lex} land T_{m lex} land (C_{m lex}lor O_{m lex}).
]

A second, looser watchlist may contain (G_{m lex}+E_{m lex}) records for false-negative inspection, but watchlist records do not count automatically.

## Manual semantic labels

Each screened abstract receives one frozen label:

- `COMPOUND_CONFIRMED` — at least three substantive motif flags including **G** and at least one of **C/O**, with an explicit structural dependency linking the machinery to a physical/effective distinction.
- `MOTIF_CONFIRMED_NOT_COMPOUND` — one or more genuine motifs, but the Paper-A-style compound architecture is absent.
- `LEXICAL_FALSE_POSITIVE` — screen fired on vocabulary without the intended structure.
- `AMBIGUOUS_ABSTRACT_ONLY` — abstract insufficient; excluded from the main confirmed count and reported separately.

For `COMPOUND_CONFIRMED`, record the exact active motif set and one-sentence dependency description.

## Primary comparison

After blind labels are frozen, join to the withheld year key and report per year:

1. confirmed compound count / 100;
2. motif prevalence (G,T,C,O,E);
3. ambiguous count;
4. exact two-sided Fisher test for 2024 vs 2026 compound prevalence when counts permit;
5. risk difference and risk ratio with cautious uncertainty language;
6. 2025 as intermediate descriptive point.

Because (n=100) per year is small and the corpus spans all physics, absence of significance does not establish absence of a trend.

## Decision rule for Paper A wording

- **Evidence supports proliferation wording:** 2026 shows a materially higher confirmed-compound prevalence than 2024, with the direction robust to reasonable handling of ambiguous cases and not explainable by one obvious category artifact.
- **Suggestive only:** point estimate rises but uncertainty/low counts prevent strong field-trend language.
- **No support:** flat/mixed/decreasing prevalence or the apparent rise disappears under manual audit.
- **Category-confounded:** trend is dominated by a shifted field/category mixture and cannot support broad field language without stratification.

The paper's selected-example convergence thesis is independent of this test. Failure here means only that the **field-wide word “proliferation”** is not earned.
