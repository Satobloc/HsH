## Reference 6 — atom-surface nanograting phase shifts

Recommended primary citation:

A. D. Cronin and J. D. Perreault, “De Broglie wave phase shifts induced by surfaces closer than 25 nm,” *Journal of Physics: Conference Series* **19**, 48–55 (2005). DOI `10.1088/1742-6596/19/1/008`; arXiv `physics/0506163`.

The paper reports atom-optics experiments in which beam atoms pass within 25 nm of nanofabricated material grating bars and the analysis explicitly incorporates de Broglie-wave phase shifts caused by atom–surface interactions. This directly supports the manuscript's narrow claim that a material nanograting is not merely an ideal binary aperture and that wall interactions can enter the outgoing diffraction pattern.

A stronger later experimental corroborant, optional rather than necessary for the present sentence:

V. P. A. Lonij, C. E. Klauss, W. F. Holmgren, and A. D. Cronin, “Atom Diffraction Reveals the Impact of Atomic Core Electrons on Atom-Surface Potentials,” *Physical Review Letters* **105**, 233202 (2010). DOI `10.1103/PhysRevLett.105.233202`.
