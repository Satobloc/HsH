## 5. Type-safe candidate map, with conditions

The following is a **proposed mathematical completion**, not a quotation from Nathan Direct and not a claim that the physical core is a ball.

Let M be a four-dimensional manifold with a chosen metric of the appropriate signature. Let γ:I→M be an embedded centerline. Let Nγ be its rank-three normal bundle, defined using that metric and the centerline's causal character. Choose an admissible compact fiber K_s⊂N_sγ with nonempty three-dimensional interior. Do not set K_s=B³ unless that shape is independently authorized.

A local thickening can then be written schematically as

    X(s,y) = exp_{γ(s)}(E_a(s)y^a),    y ∈ K_s,
    W = image(X).

For W to be a regular four-dimensional support, X must be locally immersive and, on the chosen tube domain, injective or otherwise have its self-overlap explicitly modeled. The tube radius must lie within a valid normal-neighborhood bound. A physical Kerr shell, hollow coil, or ER-bridge cross-section would require its own K_s and boundary conditions.

Represent a time-indexed timesheet as a regular level set

    Σ_τ = {p ∈ M : F(p,τ)=0},    d_p F ≠ 0.

Define the slice/readout support

    R_τ = W ∩ Σ_τ.

If the intersection is transverse, dim(R_τ)=4+3−4=3. If the boundary intersection is also transverse, dim(∂W ∩ Σ_τ)=3+3−4=2.

By contrast:

- γ(I)∩Σ_τ is generically 1+3−4=0 dimensional at fixed τ;
- a two-dimensional worldsheet intersected by Σ_τ is generically one-dimensional;
- a four-dimensional support intersected by Σ_τ is generically three-dimensional.

The normal-bundle projection π_N:W→γ is available only where the tube coordinates are unique. Its restriction π_N|R_τ maps the 3D section back to the centerline, with core fibers. A separate observation map O_τ:R_τ→Y is still required to define the measured/experienced particle state. No inspected direct source or historical specification supplies this final map.

This makes the unresolved phrase “3D intersectional trace” precise enough to test without forcing an interpretation: it could denote the 3D finite-core section R_τ, or it could refer to a family of centerline intersection points across τ. The source does not yet choose between those meanings.
