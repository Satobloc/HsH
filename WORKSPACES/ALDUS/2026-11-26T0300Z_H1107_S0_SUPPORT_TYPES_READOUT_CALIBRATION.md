## Executive finding

Three different supports have repeatedly been compressed under “filament”:

- `γ¹`: one-dimensional filament/centerline/worldline;
- `J²`: two-dimensional worldsheet support associated with the historical Blockwave rank-two current;
- `W⁴`: four-dimensional finite-core support/worldtube.

They are not interchangeable. No source-defined thickening map `T:γ¹→W⁴` has been recovered, and no source-defined map connects the Blockwave worldsheet current to the later four-dimensional support.

A fixed-slice intersection is also different from the trace of intersections across a moving family of slices. The 2024 direct geometry makes this explicit. The October 2026 direct geometry rejects a passive-readout interpretation of the time-surface: it is described as interacting with matter. Set intersection is therefore only incidence, not the complete physical response.

Calibration is downstream of readout. The November 26 calibration-domain theorem supplies a conditional predictor-separation result, but it does not define the physical response operator, observable space, or an actual calibration domain.
