# Stable Geometry Glossary — supplement to Aldus h784 source-fidelity addendum

**Status:** terminology and source-fidelity aid only. This glossary does not add a physical premise, select a core shape, or promote sandbox mathematics into BEDROCK.

## Geometric objects

| Term | Stable meaning | Source status |
|---|---|---|
| M⁴ | Four-dimensional ambient manifold in which the candidate geometry is embedded. Its metric/signature must be stated; “4D” alone does not imply Euclidean or Lorentzian. | Mathematical container; signature unresolved in the direct geometry record. |
| γ | One-dimensional centerline/worldline/filament. Keep it distinct from any finite-radius support around it. | Nathan Direct baseline. |
| Σ | Three-dimensional timesheet/hypersurface. A family Σ_τ may be used in a model, but τ is only a label until its physical meaning is specified. | Nathan Direct baseline; explicit level-set family appears in assistant reconstruction. |
| Nγ | Rank-three normal bundle of γ, defined relative to a chosen metric and the causal character of γ. | Standard mathematical construction; not yet a source-authorized physical structure. |
| K_s | Admissible cross-sectional fiber in the normal space at γ(s). It may be hollow, shell-like, or otherwise constrained. Do not silently set K_s=B³ or identify it with a sphere. | Shape unresolved; Kerr-shell/hollow-core possibilities appear in direct messages. |
| X | Candidate thickening map from normal-bundle coordinates to M⁴, e.g. X(s,y)=exp_{γ(s)}(E_a(s)y^a). | Standard/assistant reconstruction, not Nathan Direct. |
| W | Image of X, a four-dimensional finite-core support when X is regular and its self-overlap is controlled. W is not identical to γ. | Candidate worldtube/core support; physical existence and shape unresolved. |
| Σ_τ | A time-indexed 3D hypersurface, optionally represented as F(p,τ)=0 with d_pF≠0. | The level-set form is a sandbox model choice; τ is not automatically physical time. |
| R_τ | Slice W∩Σ_τ. If W and Σ_τ intersect transversely, dim(R_τ)=3. | Conditional mathematical object; not yet identified with the direct phrase “3D intersectional trace.” |
| ∂W∩Σ_τ | Boundary slice. Under transverse intersection, it is generically two-dimensional. | Conditional consequence of the candidate support map. |
| π_N | Normal-bundle projection from W back to γ, available where tube coordinates are unique. | Standard map; its physical interpretation is unresolved. |
| O_τ | Observation/readout map from R_τ to a measured or experienced state space Y. | Missing composition edge; no inspected source defines it. |

## Dimensional distinctions

- **Worldline / centerline:** 1D curve γ in M⁴.
- **Worldsheet:** 2D support. A transverse intersection with a 3D hypersurface is generically 1D.
- **Timesheet:** 3D hypersurface Σ in M⁴.
- **Worldtube / finite-core support:** 4D support W, if a valid thickening map is supplied. A transverse intersection with Σ is generically 3D.
- **Centerline hit:** γ∩Σ_τ is generically 0D at fixed τ.
- **Finite-core section:** W∩Σ_τ is generically 3D under transversality.

The phrase “3D intersectional trace” remains unresolved between a finite-core section R_τ and a family of centerline hits across τ. Dimensional convenience is not source evidence.

## Current-strata labels for currents

- **J_μ:** vector current appearing in the historical SAT4D draft, with two non-identical uses in that draft and no derived map between them.
- **J^{μν}:** assistant-origin conserved two-form current in the Blockwave action. Its rank and support type differ from J_μ and from a 4D core support.
- **J_{μνρ}:** later user-role paste with unresolved embedded authorship. No map to either J_μ or J^{μν} has been established.

Never merge these currents by notation alone. A degree-changing map requires explicit source, domain/codomain, projection, and composition conditions.

## Source-status vocabulary

- **Nathan Direct:** role-verified user-authored message or user-authored source document, with the strength of attribution stated.
- **Assistant reconstruction:** an equation, action, interpretation, or derivation generated by an LLM. It may be useful, but it is not Nathan Direct.
- **Historical source:** an older document or transcript whose date, byline, role graph, or version status may be incomplete.
- **Standard reference:** established mathematical construction used conditionally, not evidence that the physical system realizes it.
- **Proposal / sandbox:** a model choice introduced for exploration, including the Meridian SPEC and EXP002.
- **Independent measurement:** a sourced observation with identifiable system, dataset, provenance, units, calibration, and uncertainty. None was used in this geometry-fidelity pass.

## Physical-shape cautions

- “Hollow outer coil” is a direct descriptive constraint; it does not define a normal ball.
- “One helical core” and “no second-order core” constrain interpretations but do not supply a cross-sectional embedding.
- A Kerr shell or ER-bridge cross-section is a possibility mentioned in direct discussion, not a selected geometry.
- A Euclidean B³ tubular neighborhood is a coordinate construction, not proof of a spherical physical carrier.
- The R4 SO(4) frame and the direct Minkowski/w=ct description need an explicit signature-aware map before being combined.
