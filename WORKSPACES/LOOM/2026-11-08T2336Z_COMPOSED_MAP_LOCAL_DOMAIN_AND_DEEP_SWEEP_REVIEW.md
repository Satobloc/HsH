### Exact applicability gap

The PR narrative discusses local error and Lipschitz bounds, but the current Lean hypotheses quantify globally over the full intermediate types:

- `hLip` and `hFirst` quantify over all inputs/intermediate pairs.
- `hSecond` quantifies over every point of the intermediate space.
- The three-stage `hLipG`, `hLipH`, and `hThird` are likewise global.

The theorem is sound if those global uniform bounds hold. It does **not** establish the operationally weaker claim that bounds verified only on a calibrated local subset are sufficient.

### Proposed map-edge gate delta

Do not duplicate the existing map-class or uncertainty ledgers. Add only these missing obligations when the theorem is instantiated:

1. **Bound scope:** mark each deterministic approximation and Lipschitz bound as `GLOBAL_UNIFORM` or `RESTRICTED_DOMAIN`.
2. **Reachability/invariance:** for a restricted domain, identify the calibrated admissible subset and show that both the reference and approximate trajectories remain inside it for every input in the declared calibration/holdout domain. A finite calibration sample alone does not establish holdout coverage.
3. **Metric-unit signature:** record the input/output metric units or explicit dimensionless normalization maps. Lean's nonnegative-real constants do not encode physical units. (L_i) has output-distance/input-distance units; (L_i\epsilon_i) and the next-stage (epsilon_{i+1}) must be commensurate in the same target metric.
4. **Reference-map identity:** identify the exact/reference map whose Lipschitz constant is used. It is a declared model comparator, not automatically physical truth. Freeze its parameterization and calibration before holdout. If both compared chains are approximate, bound both against a common reference or account for both errors.

Keep deterministic map-approximation bounds separate from statistical covariance, set-valued fiber ambiguity, source-semantic uncertainty, nuisance-span absorption, and dependence/provenance. Do not combine these channels by an unqualified scalar sum or root-sum-square.

A minimal operational edge record therefore adds `bound_scope`, `reachable_subset`, `exact_and_approx_trajectory_membership_evidence`, and `metric_unit_or_normalization_signature` to the existing source/domain/codomain/error-bound record.
