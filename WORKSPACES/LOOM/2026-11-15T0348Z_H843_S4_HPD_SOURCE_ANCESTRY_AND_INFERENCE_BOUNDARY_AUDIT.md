## 3. Source genealogy

### 3.1 Theory antecedent

V. V. Zavjalov, **“Dynamics of ϑ-solitons in the HPD state of superfluid ³He-B,”** arXiv:2212.12232v1, submitted 23 December 2022. The PDF carries a manuscript date line of 23-12-2021; preserve both source dates rather than silently normalizing them.

This is a single-author antecedent by the first author of the 2024 experiment, not independent theoretical confirmation. It contains:
- a one-dimensional numerical Leggett-equation simulation of the ϑ-soliton core/tails and their oscillations;
- a separate membrane model with mass per unit area and tension;
- a circular membrane pinned to the walls of a cylindrical cell, perpendicular to the cell axis;
- C² = 2 c_perp² - c_parallel²;
- Bessel-zero radial frequencies, with the first mode 2.405 C/r;
- an illustrative T = 0.6 Tc, P = 25.7 bar, r = 3.9 mm estimate of 1.7 kHz.

The preprint says its work was motivated by unexpected modes in 2017 Helsinki experiments that were still unpublished at the time. It also says further experimental and theoretical investigation was needed. The membrane calculation is a model antecedent, not an independently measured membrane.

### 3.2 Experimental article

V. V. Zavjalov, A. Savin, E. Sergeicheva and P. J. Hakonen, **“Spectroscopy of Oscillation Modes in Homogeneously Precessing Domain of Superfluid ³He-B,”** Journal of Low Temperature Physics 214 (2024), 385–398, DOI [10.1007/s10909-024-03051-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10909-024-03051-y).

The experiment uses continuous-wave NMR in a DryDemag cryostat. The cell is cylindrical, 7.8 mm internal diameter and 9 mm long. The NMR signal is recorded by a lock-in amplifier and a digital oscilloscope. Fourier spectrograms show sidebands around the NMR carrier; extracted mode frequencies are tracked against frequency shift, temperature, field gradient and RF amplitude.

The paper's Data Availability statement identifies about 600 measurements and says the deposit includes oscilloscope records, spectrograms and extracted oscillation modes.

## 4. Observation versus inference

### Directly measured/reported

- NMR time-domain signals and lock-in signal.
- Oscilloscope traces and Fourier spectrograms.
- Symmetric sidebands around the NMR carrier.
- Extracted oscillation frequencies and their dependence on frequency shift.
- Variation across temperature, field gradient and RF amplitude.
- Approximately 600 measurements in the dataset deposit.

### Inferred or model-conditioned

- Spatial localization is inferred from mode-frequency dependence on frequency shift in a field gradient; no direct spatial image of the soliton is reported.
- The identification of high-temperature modes 1 and 2 as ϑ-soliton oscillations is the authors' interpretation.
- The circular-membrane geometry is explicitly introduced as the paper's **central conjecture**.
- The high-temperature mode-2 onset is assumed to identify the soliton localization point and is used as the zero reference for frequency shift. The field value is converted to position using the applied gradient. This assumption enters the extraction of gaps Δ1 and Δ2.
- The membrane radius is not directly measured. The 2022 antecedent's illustrative radius is the cylindrical cell radius, 3.9 mm.
- The 2022 antecedent treats a membrane pinned to cell walls; the 2024 paper applies a membrane/radial-mode picture to a soliton described as localized somewhere in the cell volume. That is an additional geometric step, not a directly observed map.

The paper states that modes 1 and 2 are common; modes 3 and 4 resemble later simulated modes but are not confirmed; modes 7 and 8 may resemble the first mode but have insufficient visibility; modes 5 and 6 appear in pairs with frequency ratio 2 and are not assigned the same gaps.

### Calibration and uncertainty boundaries

The article reports:
- field homogeneity 1.5 × 10^-4, corresponding to about 5 μT across the cell;
- applied field-gradient magnitudes from 0 to 20 μT/cm;
- actual field profile and HPD growth not fully defined, with possible multiple minima/maxima and complicated HPD-surface topology;
- sample overheating of about 0.2 Tc under strong excitation;
- thermometry/temperature-correction accuracy of order 0.1 Tc;
- limited absolute frequency-shift calibration, while relative changes are better defined.

The paper's main results are obtained in a coherent HPD filling the cell, but the mode-2 localization-point assumption remains explicit. No uncertainty/covariance model was retrieved from the raw data.

## 5. Mathematical and notation firewall

The ³He-B order parameter is a 3×3 matrix:
A_aj = R_aj(n-hat, vartheta) exp(i phi),
where R_aj is a three-dimensional rotation matrix and vartheta is its spin/orbital rotation angle. This is the ³He-B vartheta variable.

It is **not** HsH BEDROCK's theta4, which is the filament-timesheet intersection angle measured from the time normal, with vacuum/null alignment theta4 = 0. No source-defined map connects these variables, the ³He-B order parameter, an H(s)H filament/worldtube, or the NMR readout. Similar Greek lettering is not a geometric equivalence.

The membrane model states:
Delta_n = a_n C/r.
If the same nonzero C/r applies to both modes, then algebraically:
Delta_2/Delta_1 = a_2/a_1.
Using the paper's roots, 5.520/2.405 = 2.2952182952..., consistent with the article's rounded 2.295.

This is a conditional algebraic identity and arithmetic check only. It does not establish the premises, membrane shape, boundary conditions, soliton radius, or a unique inverse from the observed ratio to circular geometry. The gap extraction also depends on the assumed mode-2 localization reference. No physical calculation or fit was performed.
