## 6. Discrepancy ledger

1. “Orthogonal” is not defined as an inner product. The assistant's \(\\langle G,S\\rangle=0\) is an added formalization, not a source equation.
2. A literal set intersection requires a common ambient space and embeddings for GR and string-theory structures. None is supplied.
3. The assistant's \(F^A(\\lambda,\\rho,\\phi)\) has three parameters and at most three-dimensional local image. It is not a four-dimensional tube around a one-dimensional centerline in four dimensions.
4. The assistant sweep intersecting a three-dimensional timesheet generically yields a two-dimensional surface; a four-dimensional tube yields a three-dimensional region. Neither preserves the direct-source point readout without a further projection.
5. SAT-O1 gives a one-dimensional curve and one-parameter action, then describes a four-dimensional worldtube without a thickening map.
6. SAT-O1's three independent sinusoidal coordinates do not automatically yield a constant-radius, constant-pitch helix. A map to the Rotoexpando standard helix remains to be defined.
7. Blockwave's \(J^{\\mu\\nu}\) is a two-form worldsheet current, not the direct-source one-dimensional filament. The 2D-worldsheet/3D-timesheet loop readout is distinct from the 1D-filament/3D-timesheet point readout.
8. The Rotoexpando point-pair-to-oriented-segment map is now formalized. The nested-helix-to-framed-sphere map remains explicitly pending in the manuscript.
9. Rotoexpando uses Euclidean \(\\mathbb R^d\) and \(SO(d)\); SAT-O1 uses \(\\mathbb R^{3,1}\); the direct June 2026 statement invokes Minkowski geometry. No Euclidean-to-Lorentzian isometry or causal-structure map is specified.
10. The torus embedding needs \(R>a>0\) for an embedded ring torus. Rational winding closure requires a constant rotation number.
11. The recursive closure claim for \(\\mu=1\), \(\\alpha=2\\pi p/q\) omits the nontrivial-root condition. At \(q=1\), the one-step sum is 1 and \(z_1=z_0+D_0\\ne z_0\) when \(D_0\\ne0\).
12. The local bound \(a<1/\\kappa_{\\max}\) does not guarantee global tube embedding. A precise nonlocal reach/separation condition is also required; the excluded-neighborhood notation is not formalized.
13. The Blockwave identity \(A=\\alpha_*d\\theta\) is locally flat for a smooth real phase. Compact global winding is possible, but nonzero local curvature needs extra structure.
14. SAT-O1's Hopf/Borromean and “no stable four-or-more interlocks in 4D” claims require a precise embedding category and linking invariant. Rotoexpando's spherical braid configuration is not automatically the same object.
15. The schematic nested-order arrows and Whirligig compression remain proposals until their domains, codomains, projection maps, composition laws, and loss conditions are explicit.
