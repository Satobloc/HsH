\[
r_c=a_0+\epsilon\cos(kz+m\theta+\omega t),\qquad \epsilon\ll a_0.
\]

The measured left/right contour samples are converted to:

\[
\eta(z,t)=\frac{r(z,t,0)-r(z,t,\pi)}{2},\qquad
