## 5. Degrees of freedom vs. observables  

| Observable                | Equation                                 | Solves for…   |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------|---------------|
| \(m_e\)                  | \(Q_e = m_0/m_e\)                        | \(Q_e\)       |

To regain predictivity, you need **fewer** truly independent topological inputs than you have observables.  That could happen if:

- **Universal filament scale** \((T,\ell_f)\) is fixed once and for all (e.g. Planck tension+length), so you don’t treat \(m_{\rm Pl}\) as a free parameter.

- **Discrete spectrum** of allowed \(Q\) values (e.g. from a fixed finite category of knots) so you get only one or two viable fits, not a continuous infinity.

If you can reduce the **effective** number of free \(Q\)’s below the number of measured constants—say down to three or four—then every extra measured observable becomes a true test of the framework rather than merely a fitted input.
