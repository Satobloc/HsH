Search what was actually said.

Search message bodies across the public SAT/H(s)H conversation catalog, not only titles. Boolean words and mathematical-symbol queries are supported as candidate retrieval. The research Mersearch engine is more capable than this static public reader.

What this search includes and what it cannot do
This is a static, public-only front end over the Conversation Viewer's approved, curation-aware inverted index. Search matches mean all requested tokens occur somewhere in that conversation, not necessarily in the same user message. Quoted strings, formula syntax, AND/OR/NOT are candidate filters. They do not certify phrase order, mathematical equivalence, proximity, or speaker authorship. For exact context, open the original viewer. Private HSH_RESOURCES content and internal material are not indexed here. Full Mersearch mathematical normalization, field search, NEAR/n and all-repository research require a protected service and are not claimed here.
