ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

An idea has a history.

Mersearch is a provenance-first research interface for the public SAT/H(s)H development record. It is designed to find original calculations, conversations, revisions, and the mathematical paths connecting them.

The historical record includes experimental work, speculations, dead ends and corrections. Search is evidence discovery, not endorsement of any theoretical claim.

The public website includes a curated conversation-title catalog. It does not search the text inside those conversations. Full-text, equation and comprehensive chronology searches require the separate Mersearch research engine, with its own authorized corpus.
