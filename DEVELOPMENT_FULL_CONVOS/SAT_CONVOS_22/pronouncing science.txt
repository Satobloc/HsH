🔤 Greek Letters (with pronunciation and common mathematical usage) 

Symbol Name Pronunciation Common Usage 

α Alpha AL-fuh Angles, coefficients 

β Beta BAY-tuh Coefficients, symmetry breaking terms 

γ Gamma GAM-uh Gamma function, Lorentz factor 

Δ Delta (capital) DEL-tuh Change (e.g., Δx), discriminants 

δ Delta (lowercase) DEL-tuh Small change, variation, Kronecker delta 

ε Epsilon EP-sih-lon or EP-suh-lon Arbitrarily small number, strain 

ζ Zeta ZAY-tuh Zeta functions, damping ratio 

η Eta AY-tuh Efficiency, conformal time 

θ Theta THAY-tuh or THAY-duh Angle, scalar field (e.g., θ₄) 

κ Kappa KAP-uh Curvature, coupling constant 

λ Lambda LAM-duh Wavelength, eigenvalues, cosmological const. 

μ Mu MYOO Coefficient of friction, mass scale 

ν Nu NEW Frequency, neutrino 

ξ Xi KSIGH Random variables, decay width 

π Pi PIE Circle ratio, product notation 

ρ Rho ROW Density, correlation coefficient 

σ Sigma SIG-muh Standard deviation, stress, sum (∑) 

τ Tau TOW (like "cow") Time constants, twist state 

φ Phi FEE or FIE Golden ratio, electric potential, angles 

χ Chi KAI (rhymes with "high") Susceptibility, characters 

ψ Psi SIGH Wavefunction 

ω Omega OH-meh-guh or OH-may-guh Angular frequency, asymptotics
