Step 1 — Recall the SAT form for Δaℓ

From our loop analysis:
\Delta a_\ell \;\propto\; \theta_\ell^2 \cdot \frac{m_\ell M}{m_h^2},
where

	•	\varepsilon_{\ell\ell_4}=e^{-\lambda\,\Delta K_{\ell\ell_4}},
	•	M = heavy rung mass, common to both muon and tau,
	•	m_h = Higgs mass, cancels in the ratio.

So the scaling is:
\Delta a_\ell \;\propto\; \varepsilon_{\ell\ell_4}^2 \; m_\ell \,\frac{v^2}{M}\,.

Step 2 — Form the ratio τ vs μ

\frac{\Delta a_\tau}{\Delta a_\mu} \;=\;
\left(\frac{\varepsilon_{\tau\ell_4}}{\varepsilon_{\mu\ell_4}}\right)^2
\cdot \frac{m_\tau}{m_\mu}.

The v, M, m_h factors cancel. This is pure SAT geometry plus known masses.

Step 3 — Plugging in numbers
