III. 2024 EXPECTEDNESS SCORE
================================================================================

Question:
From a 2024 point of view, how expected was it that mainstream science would be asking
this question, in this way, at this level of interest, by 2026?

E0 — Already obvious / mainstream
The idea was already a visible mainstream trajectory before Feb. 2, 2025.
Do not count strongly unless the paper adds SAT-specific packaging.

Examples:
- generic emergent spacetime
- ordinary gauge holonomy
- ordinary worldline formalism
- ordinary topology in QFT
- ordinary black-hole information work
- ordinary numerical-relativity worldtubes
- generic AI-for-science

E1 — Known but niche / scattered
Ingredients existed, but were not broadly integrated or newly prominent.
Weak-to-moderate signal if post-2025 papers package them in SAT-shaped ways.

E2 — Unexpected prominence / packaging
The idea existed in pieces, but its 2026 prominence, framing, or cross-domain packaging
would have been surprising from 2024.

E3 — Highly unexpected SAT-shaped cluster
The paper combines several SAT-native motifs that were not obviously on a shared
mainstream trajectory in 2024.

I. NULL BASELINE
================================================================================

Start with a strong, respectful null.

Assume competent scientists are pursuing questions naturally motivated by:
- their own previous papers;
- their field’s standard problems;
- known mathematical machinery;
- active technical literatures;
- collaborators’ expertise;
- standard next-step generalizations.

“They think Klein bottles are weird.”
“They suddenly discovered topology.”
“They are copying SAT because they used geometry.”

“They are exploring whether a controlled mathematical structure in their existing framework
can generate physically meaningful lower-dimensional effects.”

Only after that, ask whether SAT-like timing, packaging, or functional role remains.
