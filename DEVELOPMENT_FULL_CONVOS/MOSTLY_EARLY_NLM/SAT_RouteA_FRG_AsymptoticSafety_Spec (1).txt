
SAT — Route A: 4D Continuum UV Completion via Asymptotic Safety (FRG)
=====================================================================

Purpose
-------
Provide a strictly 4D, single‑medium, diffeomorphism‑invariant UV completion of SAT by exhibiting a
non‑Gaussian UV fixed point of the Functional Renormalization Group (FRG) flow of the effective average
action Γ_k[g,u,θ]. This ensures finite, predictive continuum behavior as k→∞ without adding new fields.

Core Setup
---------
• Effective average action Γ_k with IR scale k; Wetterich equation:
    ∂_t Γ_k = 1/2 Tr[(Γ_k^(2) + R_k)^{-1} ∂_t R_k] − Tr_ghost[(Γ_k,gh^(2) + R_k,gh)^{-1} ∂_t R_k,gh],
  where t = ln(k/k_0), Γ_k^(2) is the Hessian w.r.t. fluctuating fields.
• Background field method: split g = ḡ + h (metric or tetrad), u = ū + v with ū^2=−1, and θ = θ̄ + ϑ.
• Gauge fixing: de Donder (gravity), covariant background gauge for u, and standard Faddeev–Popov ghosts.
• Regulator: covariant Litim‑type R_k(Δ) = Z_k (k^2 − Δ) Θ(k^2 − Δ) for each spin sector (scalar/vector/tensor).

Minimal Truncation (Level‑0)
----------------------------
Retain the operators of the minimal SAT Lagrangian, allowing k‑dependent couplings:
  Γ_k = ∫ d^4x √−g [
    (Z_R,k/2) R − Z_Λ,k Λ_k
    + λ_k (u·u + 1)
    + (Z_u,k/2) (∇_μ u_ν)(∇^μ u^ν)
    + (Z_θ,k/2) ∂_μ θ ∂^μ θ + U_k(θ)
    + (α1_k/M_*^2) (∇u)^2 (∂θ)^2
    + (α2_k/M_*^2) (∇_μ u_ν) ∂^μ θ ∂^ν θ
    + (β_k/M_*^2) (∂θ)^4
  ] + S_gf + S_gh .

Notes:
• U_k(θ) = μ_k^4 (1 − cos θ); around a minimum θ=0, U_k ≈ (m_{θ,k}^2/2) θ^2 + … with m_{θ,k}^2 = μ_k^4/Z_θ,k.
• Optional (equivalent) kinematic split of (∇u)^2 into expansion/shear/vorticity with Z_{θu,k}, Z_{σu,k}, Z_{ωu,k} (positive).

Dimensionless Couplings
-----------------------
Define:
  g_k = k^2 G_k = k^2 / (8π Z_R,k) ,    λ̂_k = Λ_k / k^2 ,
  z_u = Z_u,k / Z_u,k0 ,    z_θ = Z_θ,k / Z_θ,k0 ,
  m̂_{θ}^2 = m_{θ,k}^2 / k^2 ,    α̂1 = α1_k k^2 / M_*^2 ,    α̂2 = α2_k k^2 / M_*^2 ,    β̂ = β_k k^2 / M_*^2 ,
and treat λ_k (constraint multiplier) as non‑running at Level‑0 (or eliminate u^0 with the constraint).

Hessians and Projectors
-----------------------
• Decompose h_{μν} into TT tensor, vector, and scalar blocks; use York decomposition. Project onto TT for the graviton.
• For u: define projectors parallel/orthogonal to ū_μ: P_{⊥μν} = ḡ_{μν} + ū_μ ū_ν. Work in background with ū^μ = (1,0,0,0).
• For θ: scalar block with periodic potential expanded to quadratic order for flows; keep full periodic U_k in a separate local potential approximation if desired.

Regulator Choice
----------------
Use Litim regulators per sector:
  R_k^tensor = Z_R,k (k^2 − Δ_2) Θ(k^2 − Δ_2),
  R_k^vector = Z_u,k (k^2 − Δ_1) Θ(k^2 − Δ_1),
  R_k^scalar = Z_θ,k (k^2 − Δ_0) Θ(k^2 − Δ_0),
with Δ_s the appropriate Laplace‑type operator on spin‑s fields (including background curvature terms).

Beta Functions (Strategy)
-------------------------
• Insert the Hessians + R_k into the Wetterich equation; evaluate traces via heat‑kernel expansion with threshold functions Q_n[W] adapted to Litim regulators.
• Extract flows for (g_k, λ̂_k) from TT and scalar blocks (gravity), for z_u from the u‑block, for z_θ and m̂_{θ}^2 from θ‑block, and for α̂1, α̂2, β̂ from mixed diagrams.
• Structure (schematic):
  ∂_t g_k = (2 + η_R) g_k ,   ∂_t λ̂_k = −2 λ̂_k + A_g(g_k,λ̂_k) + A_m(m̂_{θ}^2, z_θ, …),
  ∂_t z_u = η_u z_u ,   ∂_t z_θ = η_θ z_θ ,   ∂_t m̂_{θ}^2 = (η_θ − 2) m̂_{θ}^2 + B_θ(g_k, …),
  ∂_t α̂i = (2 + η_mix,i) α̂i + C_i(g_k, z_u, z_θ, …),   ∂_t β̂ = (2 + η_β) β̂ + C_β(…),
  where anomalous dimensions η_* = −∂_t ln Z_* and A,B,C are thresholded polynomials of the couplings.
• Expectation: (g_k, λ̂_k) admit a non‑Gaussian fixed point (g*, λ̂*). The matter/elastic sector (z_u, z_θ, m̂_{θ}^2, α̂’s, β̂) flows to finite fixed points with a small number of relevant directions (predictivity).

Ward/Nielsen Identities
-----------------------
Check modified Ward identities due to regulator breaking; ensure background independence via bimetric flows or the split‑symmetry Ward identity at leading order. Gauge‑parameter independence of physical critical exponents is used as a stability test.

Fixed‑Point Search & Critical Exponents
---------------------------------------
1) Solve β_i({g}) = 0 for the set {g*, λ̂*, z_u*, z_θ*, m̂_{θ}^2*, α̂1*, α̂2*, β̂*}.  
2) Linearize: M_{ij} = ∂β_i/∂g_j |_* ; eigenvalues −θ_I are critical exponents.  
3) Predictivity criterion: number of positive θ_I (relevant directions) is small (e.g., 2–5). Those define the finite set of UV boundary data to be fixed by experiment; all other couplings are predictions.

Consistency/Unitarity Checks
----------------------------
• Propagator poles: ensure all Z_* > 0 and no wrong‑sign residues in TT/u/θ sectors.  
• Spectral positivity: Källén‑Lehmann densities non‑negative in each sector at fixed point (check via 2‑pt).  
• Causality: standard FRG analytic continuation; IR limit reproduces GR + compact θ + elastic u.

Holonomy/Compactness
--------------------
• θ remains compact; FRG is performed on the local fluctuations around each topological sector.  
• Global sum over sectors is handled at the path‑integral level; local flows do not spoil integer winding labels or loop–flux algebra.

Practical Computation Plan
--------------------------
P1) Flat/Minkowski background (ḡ=η, ū=(1,0,0,0), θ̄=0). Compute Level‑0 β‑functions in this background to get first fixed point.  
P2) FRW background cross‑check (small curvature): verify stability of fixed‑point values and exponents.  
P3) Add minimal bimetric correction to enforce split symmetry (background independence).  
P4) Switch on one mixing at a time (α̂2 first), confirm it is irrelevant or weakly relevant with small dimension.  
P5) Global scan over regulators (Litim vs exponential) and gauges (de Donder parameter) to test scheme‑stability.

Pass/Fail UV Criteria (Route A Acceptance)
------------------------------------------
✓ Existence of a non‑Gaussian fixed point with finite g*, λ̂* and finite matter/elastic fixed‑point values.  
✓ Small number of relevant directions (predictive UV critical surface).  
✓ Stability under regulator/gauge changes (exponents vary mildly).  
✓ Positivity/causality checks pass (no ghost poles, positive residues).  
✓ IR recovery: running couplings flow to values consistent with GR tests and SAT phenomenology.

Matching to SAT Parameters
--------------------------
• Identify Z_R,k → 1/(8π G_k); fit G_{k→0} to Newton’s constant.  
• m_{θ,k} and f_k matched to the observed (or targeted) SAT phase sector scale; ρ_k from u‑mode speed.  
• α̂’s and β̂ matched (or bounded) by mixing observables (e.g., GW→θ pumping, birefringence limits).
