A) Minimal SAT–QM (holonomy version): spec you can read now

Kinematics (single medium).
Fields: metric g_{\mu\nu}, unit time-flow u^\mu with u^\mu u_\mu=-1, compact phase \theta\equiv\theta+2\pi.
For the minimal QM sector, treat g_{\mu\nu},u^\mu as fixed background (semiclassical) and quantize (\theta,\pi_\theta).

Canonical variables and commutator.
Canonical momentum: \pi_\theta = \sqrt{-g}\,f^2\,g^{0\nu}\partial_\nu \theta.
Equal-time commutator:
[\hat\theta(t,\mathbf x),\hat\pi_\theta(t,\mathbf y)] = i\,\hbar_{\text{eff}}\,\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf x-\mathbf y).
(\hbar_{\text{eff}} is an emergent medium constant to be fixed by SAT microphysics; keep explicit.)

Holonomy & flux operators (topology drives discreteness).
For a closed loop C and an oriented surface \Sigma,
	•	Phase holonomy: \hat U[C] := \exp(i\oint_C d\hat\theta).
	•	Momentum flux: \hat\Pi[\Sigma] := \int_\Sigma \hat\pi_\theta\, n_i\, d\Sigma^i.

In the presence of \theta-defects, the loop integral detects an integer winding N(C)=(2\pi)^{-1}\oint_C d\theta\in\mathbb Z. The basic loop–flux algebra is
\hat U[C]\;\hat\Pi[\Sigma] \;=\; e^{\,i\,\hbar_{\text{eff}}\,I(C,\Sigma)}\;\hat\Pi[\Sigma]\;\hat U[C],
with I(C,\Sigma)\in\mathbb Z the oriented intersection number.
→ This is the minimal holonomy-generated quantization: integers from topology + a single \hbar_{\text{eff}}.

Hilbert space (kinematical → physical).
In a finite region, choose basis loops \{C_b\} and surfaces \{\Sigma_a\}.
	•	Kinematical space: direct sum over topological sectors,
\mathcal H_{\text{kin}}=\bigoplus_{\mathbf N\in\mathbb Z^{b_1}} \mathcal H_{\mathbf N},\qquad
\hat U[C_b]\ket{\mathbf N,\psi}=e^{i\,2\pi N_b}\ket{\mathbf N,\psi}.
Within \mathcal H_{\mathbf N}, states are periodic functionals \psi[\theta] or (equivalently) functionals of the fluxes (GNS of the loop–flux algebra).
	•	Physical space: impose diffeomorphism invariance (group averaging). The u^\mu unit-constraint is already solved by the background choice.

Minimal Hamiltonian (FRW background).
On ds^2=-dt^2+a^2 d\mathbf x^2, u^\mu=(1,0,0,0),
\hat H_\theta=\int d^3x\left[
\frac{\hat\pi_\theta^2}{2 a^3 f^2}+\frac{a f^2}{2}(\nabla\hat\theta)^2+a^3\,\mu^4(1-\cos\hat\theta)
\right].
Small oscillations (no defects) give \omega_k^2=k^2+m_\theta^2 with m_\theta=\mu^2/f.
Topological sectors add soliton bands: kinks have a center-of-mass zero mode and shape modes; multi-kink sectors are labeled by the integer windings and linkings.

Path integral form (sum over sectors).
\mathcal Z=\sum_{[N]}\int \mathcal D\theta_{[N]}\,
\exp\!\left\{\frac{i}{\hbar_{\text{eff}}}\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\,\mathcal L_{\min}(\theta,u,g)\right\},
a sum over topological sectors [N] (inequivalent boundary conditions/defect content). Intrinsic elastic terms couple long-wave strain to \theta fluctuations, enabling mode conversion inside the same medium.

Holonomy-driven quantization rules (useful shortcuts).
	•	Topological charge operator \hat N[C]=(2\pi)^{-1}\oint_C d\hat\theta has integer spectrum.
	•	Loop–flux uncertainty: \Delta N\,\Delta\Pi \gtrsim \tfrac12 |I(C,\Sigma)|\,\hbar_{\text{eff}}.
	•	Action holonomy (Bohr–Sommerfeld): for a collective coordinate q, \oint p\,dq=2\pi n\,\hbar_{\text{eff}}.
	•	Berry/holonomy phases from adiabatic transport classify spin/statistics tags of defect composites.

Consistency & limits.
No higher time derivatives (no Ostrogradsky). Stability for f^2>0,\ \rho>0. Diffeomorphism invariance gives \nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0. GR is recovered at leading order; \hbar_{\text{eff}} is fixed by matching to a SAT calibration (e.g., a single interferometric observable).

Minimal predictions (observable fingerprints).
	1.	Achromatic, integer phase steps along lines of sight that pierce topological sheets.
	2.	Loop–flux shot-noise/uncertainty with coefficient \hbar_{\text{eff}}.
	3.	Discrete zero-mode ladders for kink composites; absence of stable high-multiplicity exotics consistent with allowed holonomy classes.
