The Lorentz question in SAT: three compatible designs

A) “Gauge–khronon” (no inherent preferred frame)

Idea. Make the time-flow a gauge redundancy of a scalar label T(x):
u_\mu \;=\; -\frac{\partial_\mu T}{\sqrt{-\,\partial_\alpha T\,\partial^\alpha T}}\,,\qquad
T \;\to\; f(T)\;\;\text{(monotonic reparametrization)}.
	•	The action is built from foliation invariants (e.g., \nabla_\mu u_\nu combinations), but the choice of T is gauge.
	•	In exact vacuum (no defects, \nabla u=0,\ \partial\theta=0), any timelike direction is equivalent → no physically preferred frame.
	•	Matter couples only to g_{\mu\nu} (minimal coupling). u^\mu enters only via suppressed, higher-dimensional intrinsic terms (your \alpha_{1,2},\beta mixings).
	•	This is the cleanest way to match precision Lorentz tests: boost invariance is recovered in the IR because the “clock” is pure gauge.

How to implement in your \mathcal L_{\min}.
	•	Replace the free u^\mu with the khronon definition and add the gauge T\!\to\! f(T) invariance.
	•	Keep the unit constraint u^2=-1 automatically enforced by construction; \lambda can be dropped.
	•	Use the shear/expansion/vorticity decomposition of \nabla u; restrict to hypersurface-orthogonal sector (vorticity =0).

What this buys. Local Lorentz is respected in all matter processes; any residual LV lives only in tiny curvature/strain effects (next-to-leading order, tunably small).

B) “Spontaneous but screened” (de facto, not de jure)

Idea. Treat u^\mu as a bona fide field but arrange the couplings so that:
	1.	In vacuum, the eom admit \nabla u=0,\,\partial\theta=0 with degenerate u^\mu directions (no physical choice).
	2.	In the presence of stress-energy, u^\mu aligns with the timelike eigenvector of T^\mu{}\nu: the local rest frame of the medium (de facto time-flow).
\text{Add an alignment term}\quad \chi\!\left(u\mu - \frac{j_\mu}{\sqrt{-j^2}}\right)^2,
\quad j_\mu \equiv f^2 \partial_\mu\theta,
with \chi large but finite.

How to stay safe experimentally.
	•	Decompose your (\nabla u)^2 into Einstein-Æther couplings (expansion/shear/vorticity). Choose the combos that give:
	•	GW speed c_T=1 exactly,
	•	PPN preferred-frame parameters \alpha_1=\alpha_2=0,
	•	No gravitational Cherenkov losses.
	•	Keep \alpha_{1,2},\beta small and organized as higher-dimensional operators suppressed by M_*^{-2}.

What this buys. You keep a physically meaningful flow where matter is, but you don’t introduce an absolute one. All Lorentz-violation signals become environmental and are bounded by data.

C) “Emergent/coarse-grained flow” (statistical origin)

Idea. Microscopically, filaments have no absolute preferred time. The coarse-grained congruence u^\mu is the averaged direction of energy transport:
u^\mu \;\propto\; \langle T^{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\rm micro}\,\xi_\nu \quad \text{(normalized timelike eigenvector)}.
You then use the same elastic terms for \nabla u as a hydrodynamic description.
Upshot: Lorentz invariance is exact in the micro-rules; the apparent flow is a state-dependent order parameter.

What is “de facto local time-flow,” really?

	•	Vacuum (T_{\mu\nu}\propto g_{\mu\nu}): the eigenvector is not unique → no physical frame.
	•	With fields/defects: eigenvector is unique; that defines the local comoving frame (your “de facto flow”).
This matches the GR intuition (fluid 4-velocity), so mainstream Lorentz tests apply unchanged.
