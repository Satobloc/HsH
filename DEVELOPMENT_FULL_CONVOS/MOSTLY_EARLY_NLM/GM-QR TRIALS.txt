DOUBLE-ROTATION / TWO-RATE RECURRENCE SOLVERINTRO Recovers the two rotational rates underlying a sampled 4D double-rotation orbit.MATHEMATICAL STRUCTURE / PIECES • Two planar frequencies ω, ν. • SO(4)-type double rotation / two orthogonal rotation planes. • Sample sequence obeying an order-4 palindromic recurrence. • Characteristic roots: e^(±iωτ), e^(±iντ). • Recurrence coefficients → cosine roots: x = cos(ωτ), y = cos(ντ). • Exact discriminant: Δq = 4 sin²[(ω+ν)τ/2] sin²[(ω−ν)τ/2]. • Block-Hankel/TLS numerical recovery. • Lag/decimation τ = L dt as explicit conditioning variable.ORIGINAL PURPOSE / OTHER PURPOSES Original: determine whether two hidden rotational rates can be reconstructed from sampled geometric trajectories. Other: chirality/rotation diagnostics; parameter recovery; benchmark for solver identifiability; possible input to ᚼ relative-transform reconstruction.USABILITY / CERTAINTY • Exact recurrence mathematics: strong. • Lag-conditioned discriminant: analytic. • Numerical recovery: working on tested easy-gap benchmark. • Adjacent sampling can be catastrophically ill-conditioned. • Moderate decimation produced <1% median gap error in current easy-gap test. • Small-gap resolution boundary still being measured.SOLVER ECOLOGY / INTEGRATION Provides parameter extraction rather than complete geometry generation. Natural upstream feeder to ᚼ and rotational-state comparison. Needs fixed, answer-blind lag-selection rule and standardized output schema.OUTWARD-FACING SPEC “This solver estimates two underlying rotational rates from sampled 4D trajectory data.
