Minimal Working Lagrangian

\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}
&= \mathcal{L}{\text{fol}} + \mathcal{L}{\theta} + \mathcal{L}{\text{gauge}} + \mathcal{L}{\text{ferm}} + \mathcal{L}{\text{top}}
\\[6pt]
\mathcal{L}{\text{fol}}
&= \lambda\!\left((\partial T)^2+1\right)
	•	c_1\,(\partial_\mu u_\nu)(\partial^\mu u^\nu)
	•	c_2\,(\partial_\mu u^\mu)^2
\\[6pt]
\mathcal{L}{\theta}
&= \tfrac{1}{2}\,\Delta^{\mu\nu}\partial\mu\theta_4\,\partial_\nu\theta_4

	•	\tfrac{1}{2}(u^\mu\partial_\mu\theta_4)^2
	•	\mu^2\cos\!\big(3\theta_4\big)
\\[6pt]
\mathcal{L}{\text{gauge}}
&= -\tfrac{1}{4}\sum_I F^I{\mu\nu}F^{I\,\mu\nu}
\\[6pt]
\mathcal{L}{\text{ferm}}
&= \sum_a \bar\psi_a\!\left(i\gamma^\mu\partial\mu - m_a(\theta_4)\right)\psi_a
	•	\sum_{a,I} g_I\,\bar\psi_a\gamma^\mu A^I_\mu T^I\psi_a
\\[6pt]
\mathcal{L}{\text{top}}
&= \tfrac{\kappa}{32\pi^2}\,\theta_4 \sum_I F^I{\mu\nu}\tilde F^{I\,\mu\nu}
\end{aligned}}

⸻

Definitions
	•	u_\mu \equiv -\dfrac{\partial_\mu T}{\sqrt{-(\partial T)^2}}, unit timelike vector.
	•	\Delta^{\mu\nu} \equiv \eta^{\mu\nu} + u^\mu u^\nu, projector onto spatial directions.
	•	\tilde F^{\mu\nu} = \tfrac{1}{2}\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}F_{\rho\sigma}.
	•	\theta_4 is 2\pi/3-periodic.
	•	Fermion masses m_a(\theta_4) must respect the discrete symmetry, e.g. m_{0a} + y_a \sin^2\theta_4.

⸻

Why this is “lean but viable”
	•	Foliation sector: just enough to define the preferred time direction and stabilize fluctuations.
	•	\theta_4: periodic scalar with discrete symmetry and minimal kinetic structure.
	•	Gauge fields: standard Yang–Mills.
	•	Fermions: Dirac fields with mass modulated by \theta_4, plus gauge interactions.
	•	Topological term: axion-like coupling of \theta_4 to F\tilde F.
