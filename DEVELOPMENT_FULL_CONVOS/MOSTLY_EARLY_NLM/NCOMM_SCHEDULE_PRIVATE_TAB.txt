The selection process for the Nobel Prize in Physics is a rigorous, invitation-only sequence spanning roughly 14 to 16 months. Managed by the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences, the cycle functions strictly under closed-door conditions.

By Invitation Only: Every September, the Nobel Committee for Physics sends out roughly 3,000 confidential nomination forms to a select, elite group of global scientists.
Eligible Nominators: You cannot apply or request a form. Nominations are only accepted from qualified individuals including prior Nobel Laureates, members of the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences, tenured physics professors at Nordic universities, and selected physics chairs worldwide.
Strict Bans: Self-nomination is explicitly prohibited, as are posthumous nominations (the nominee must be alive).
Deadline: Completed nomination forms must be returned to the Committee by January 31.
