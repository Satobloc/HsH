The Avery Protocol is a multi-stage methodological gauntlet and testing framework within the Scalar Angular Torsion (SAT) physics model. Designed as a filter for strict epistemic hygiene, it prevents theorists from using SAT as a speculative "hypothesis generator" that layers arbitrary assumptions onto standard physics.
Instead, the protocol mandates procedural neutrality—treating SAT as a disciplined, zero-parameter geometric extraction procedure operating on standard-physics 4D maps.

Key Stages of the Avery Protocol
	1.	Step 1: The Setup Pipeline (4D Benchmark Mapping) Beginning with SAT’s current grammar—empirically constrained Minkowski/worldtube topology—standard physics data is used to construct objective 4D benchmark maps from raw observable data, completely independent of human coordinate vantage points.
	2.	Step 2: The Purge (Label Stripping & Epistemic Hygiene) Inherited human labels and historical terminology (such as "magnetic field," "mass," "force," or "charge") are completely stripped away. Theorists are forbidden from using narrative names and must inspect only the pure, naked structural topology and geometric stress tensors (e.g., spatial curves or twists).
	3.	Step 3: Extraction of Candidate Geometries Candidate SAT structures are strictly extracted from observable differences, invariants, or topological necessities in the benchmark map. Theorists cannot guess or invent candidate structures; if the geometry does not present a candidate, the required output is a null result or open question.
	4.	Steps 4 & 5: The Squeeze (The Necessity Test & Zero-Parameter Economy) Extracted candidate features are thrown into a crucible called "the squeeze". Mere empirical compatibility is rejected in favor of a strict zero-parameter economy: the geometry must be structurally mandatory, eliminating "fudge factors" or arbitrary constants.
	5.	Step 6: The Compression Criterion / Cross-Sector Explanatory Test Described as the crown jewel of the protocol, this gate forbids single-problem "band-aid" fixes. To survive, a proposed structural candidate must organically resolve multiple, seemingly unrelated anomalies across entirely different domains on a universal scale.
	6.	Step 7: The Integration Test & Failure Rigidity The final gate demands absolute procedural loyalty over personal preference or historical bias. Under failure rigidity, if a candidate hypothesis contradicts a previously admitted, heavily compressed structural necessity, the theorist must immediately abandon it ("systematically murder your darlings"), regardless of how much time or effort went into developing it.

Core Philosophy
The Avery Protocol relies on the principle that the map is not the territory. By removing historical names, enforcing cross-sector compression, and requiring zero added parameters, it ensures that SAT models are stripped of semantic contamination, leaving only geometric structures that withstand every test of logic and physical necessity.
