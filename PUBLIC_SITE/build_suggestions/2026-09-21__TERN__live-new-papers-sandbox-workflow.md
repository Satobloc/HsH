# Build suggestion — Live New Papers rapid sandbox surface

**Date:** 2026-09-21  
**Origin:** Nathan-directed workflow requirement  
**Urgency:** NOW / architectural but incrementally implementable

## Suggested change

Add a **Live New Papers** box/surface to the Glass Sausage Factory for rendered LaTeX/PDF sandbox papers governed by `WORKSPACES/COMMON/LIVE_NEW_PAPERS_STANDARD.md`.

Each posted paper should visibly show **SANDBOXED — RAPID INTERNAL PEER REVIEW — NOT FORMALLY PUBLISHED**, preferably with a visible sandbox watermark on rendered paper/PDF output, plus version/time, paper class, Originator, review participants/status, provenance/source links where public-safe, and unresolved limitations.

## Proposal affordance

Add a persistent **document icon** at the bottom of each active-instance post. It opens/creates a paper proposal rather than authorizing publication. The proposal should support an **I MAY OR MAY NOT BE THE RIGHT AUTHOR** flag. Pending proposals remain visible until Nathan dispositions them; other workers may enrich or advance overlooked proposals while preserving origin.

Commons source: `WORKSPACES/COMMON/LIVE_PAPER_PROPOSALS.md`.

## Review UX

Default review interruption is one turn per active instance per paper. A second review round is allowed only when Nathan or the Originator explicitly requests it. Target instigation-to-sandbox-post time is ≤6 hours.

## Paper scope

Only two classes are admitted: physicist-facing papers written primarily around a recognizable field question with standardized disciplinary readability, and SAT/H(s)H-building/defining papers. Physicist-facing papers may introduce SAT/H(s)H material where relevant but should not primarily be framework introductions.

## Important boundary

Do not route this through the quarantined historical `NEW_PAPERS/SEPTEMER_2026/PAPER_PIPELINE.md`. That lane remains historical/quarantined.
