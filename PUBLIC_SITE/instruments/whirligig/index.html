Whirligig

Two independently driven rotations and their continuous generated trace. The 3D sphere/torus configuration is an educational mapping instrument; the 4D mode computes an actual sequence of SO(4) plane rotations, displayed through a 3D coordinate view.

Geometric prototype · SAT/H(s)H exploration.
