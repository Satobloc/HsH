This is a browser reconstruction of the historical b=a limiting setup. Its selected contact obeys the analytic no-slip condition at one nominated point; because equal radii create a nonisolated contact set, this does not certify no-slip across every point of contact. There is no separate pen or projector. The only tracing member is the gimbal elbow, constrained perpendicular to the moving crossbar. Two independently shaped curves are traversed by one shared clock; signed curvature differences change elbow orientation. The mechanism is a geometric reconstruction, not a proven physical bearing model. No isomorphism or physical law is assumed. Research reference: Whirligig R/Q Python solver.
