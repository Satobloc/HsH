Whirligig: crossbar and pointer arm

Crossbar: sphere-local N–S diameter. Pointer: pivots at the crossbar midpoint, stays perpendicular to the crossbar, and makes one full relative revolution per complete petal-curve traversal.

The moving petal anchor and shared tangent still determine the sphere orientation. The pointer's one-turn rotation is an additional driven motion about the sphere's N–S axis.
